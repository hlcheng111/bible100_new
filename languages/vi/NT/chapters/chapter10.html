  • Nắm vững mạch lạc lịch sử

  • Nhận biết các nhân vật và sự kiện Kinh Thánh liên quan

“And when they had preached the gospel to that city and made many disciples, they returned to Lystra, Iconium, and Antioch.” (Acts 14:21)

Once the journey ended, they retraced their steps.

Why didn’t they return directly to Antioch?

l   They wanted to consolidate the new churches by encouraging and strengthening the new believers.

l   They warned about the challenges ahead.

l   They appointed elders.

l   They prayed and fasted with them.

l   They entrusted them to the Lord.

Back in Antioch, they related the events of their journey to the church. All the believers rejoiced together.
