这一课讲述以色列史上王国分裂时期的后半部分。就如第十课所说，这是国家慢慢衰落和百姓灵性软弱的时期。在这一课我们会讲到南国犹大的情况。南国的衰落没有北国那样快，主因是南国有耶路撒冷和神的殿，也就是神所选择要立他名的地方。此外，南国也有一些好君王，他们在不同程度上跟随神，给百姓带来好的影响。在他们统治期间，百姓的信仰获得复兴，因而减慢了国家衰落的速度。他们也留下了一些榜样给我们学习；在列王纪上、列王纪下和历代志下，你都可以找到这些榜样。

This lesson covers the second half of Israel's history during the period of the divided kingdom. As discussed in Lesson 10, this was a period of slow national decline and spiritual weakness among the people. In this lesson, we will examine the situation of the southern kingdom of Judah. The southern kingdom declined less rapidly than the northern kingdom, primarily because it had Jerusalem and God's temple, the place God had chosen to establish his name. Furthermore, the southern kingdom also had some good kings who followed God to varying degrees and had a positive influence on the people. During their reigns, the people's faith was revived, slowing the decline of the kingdom. They also left us examples to learn from, which can be found in the books of 1 Kings, 2 Kings, and 2 Chronicles .

To the north, the Babylonian Empire grew stronger, while to the south, Egypt gradually became a major regional power. Weak Judah was caught in the middle, its situation truly precarious!

北国拥有最好的农地，雨水也较多，所以形势较为有利。然而，主前 722 年，较强大的北国被灭，时为以色列王何细亚第 9 年（参王下 17:1）。北国比南国的人口更多，可是北国比南国更早灭亡。为什么？原因之一是，北国和南国都有 19 个王，但北国没有一个王是好的，而南国其中 8 个王是好的。神因着这些好的君王就保守犹大。在列王纪下和历代志下，有两句话常常重复出现；一句是：行耶和华眼中看为正的事，另外一句是：行耶和华眼中看为恶的事。两个国家的其中一个分别就在于他们的王所作的事，到底是正，还是恶。在耶路撒冷的圣殿有合宜的祭司制度，也有对耶和华的敬拜，这是南国不断得到力量和保护的重要原因。只要百姓继续顺服神和敬拜神，神就会与他们同在。

The northern kingdom had the best farmland and more rainfall, making it more advantageous. However, the more powerful northern kingdom was destroyed in 722 BC, the ninth year of King Hoshea of Israel (see 2 Kings 17:1). The northern kingdom had a larger population than the southern kingdom, yet it perished earlier. Why? One reason is that both the northern and southern kingdoms had 19 kings, but none of the northern kings were good, while eight of the southern kings were. God protected Judah because of these good kings. Two phrases recur frequently in 2 Kings and 2 Chronicles: one is "doing what is right in the eyes of the Lord," and the other is "doing what is evil in the eyes of the Lord." One of the differences between the two kingdoms was the actions of their kings: whether they were righteous or evil. The proper priesthood and worship of the Lord in the temple in Jerusalem were crucial factors in the continued strength and protection of the southern kingdom. As long as the people continued to obey and worship God, He would be with them.

Therefore, today, as a co-worker, the most important thing is not how good your work is in the eyes of others or yourself, but whether it is right in the eyes of God and whether it is acceptable to Him. If what you do is right in the eyes of God, you and your church will be blessed.

They were taken captive to Babylon three times and their nation was destroyed . During the years of captivity, the people of Judah should have learned a lesson from the Israelites’ captivity (see 2 Kings 17 : 7-23 ) . However, “ the people of Judah did not keep the commandments of the Lord their God, but walked in the statutes that the children of Israel had established ” ( 2 Kings 17:19 ) . How did they embark on the path of destruction? Let us examine this now.

问题：  question:

 神表示他憎恶什么？什么是他不能容忍的？

   What does God say He hates? What is something He cannot tolerate?

 

 

解释：  explain:

犹大的问题在于百姓不再真心顺服和事奉那位带领他们出埃及的神。不错，他们做足了律法的要求，实行了律法吩咐他们的一切外在事情：奉献、献祭、守节期、烧香、祷告、守严肃会和其他礼仪。可是，即使外在的行为做得又正确又恰当，他们的内心其实已远离神。百姓把与宗教有关的事情都做对了、做好了；他们对宗教问题有正确的答案，也使用了正确的宗教术语。然而，他们的品格、道德标准以至内心的态度都有偏差。

Judah's problem was that the people had lost their true obedience and service to the God who had led them out of Egypt. True, they fulfilled the Law's requirements and performed all the outward duties commanded: offerings, sacrifices, festivals, burning incense, prayers, solemn assemblies, and other rituals. However, even though their outward actions were correct and appropriate, their hearts had actually drifted away from God. The people did all the right things regarding religion; they had the right answers to religious questions and used the correct religious terminology. Yet, their character, moral standards, and even their inner attitudes were flawed.

Incense is an abomination to me. New moons and Sabbaths, and the calling of solemn assemblies, I detest. I cannot tolerate iniquity with solemn assemblies. 14 Your new moons and your appointed festivals are an abomination to me; they are a burden to me. I am weary of bearing them. 15 When you spread out your hands in prayer, I will hide my eyes from you; even if you make many prayers, I will not listen. Your hands are filled with blood.” 16 Wash yourselves, make yourselves clean, put away the evil of your doings from my sight; cease to do evil, 17 learn to do good, seek justice, relieve the oppressed, defend the fatherless, plead for the widow.

这个问题叫做仪式主义。仪式主义令神的百姓以外在的活动和行为，作为信仰的重心。不论教会的背景或习惯如何，仪式主义可以是任何教会的问题；仪式主义可以肆虐城市的大教会，亦能支配乡村的小教会。无论在哪里，仪式主义都不能代替作主耶稣真正门徒应具备的品质。

This problem is called ritualism. Ritualism causes God's people to center their faith on outward activities and behaviors. Regardless of their background or customs, ritualism can be a problem in any church; it can plague large urban churches as well as small rural congregations. Nowhere can ritualism replace the qualities that make a true disciple of Jesus.

没有内在的敬虔，献祭就毫无用处。仪式主义就是以表面的仪式或行为作为信仰的重心。无论在哪里，仪式主义都不能代替作真门徒应具备的品质。

Without inner piety, sacrifices are useless. Ritualism is the focus of faith on outward rituals or actions. No matter where it occurs, ritualism can never replace the qualities that make a true disciple.

这是对中国教会的强烈警告。历代教会都很容易受仪式主义影响，罗马天主教会就是一个例子，现今的教会同样也很容易受仪式主义影响。我们的教会又如何？今天，你教会的敬拜是怎么样？你们唱诗的时候，是否在自娱自乐，还是真的在敬拜神，衷心地向神发出感谢和称颂？今天，我们是否表里一致？我们所说的，跟我们所做的是否一致？还是我们只有讲道和听道，却缺少行道？

This is a strong warning to the Chinese church. Churches throughout history have been susceptible to ritualism, the Roman Catholic Church being a prime example, and churches today are equally susceptible to ritualism. What about our churches? What about your church's worship today? When you sing, are you simply entertaining yourself, or are you truly worshipping God, offering heartfelt thanks and praise? Are we consistent in what we say and what we do? Or are we merely preaching and hearing, but lacking in practical action?

我们的事奉又如何？是否徒具外表，而失去了起初的爱心？什么是起初的爱心？热恋中的男女总是常常想到怎样讨对方喜悦，并且因着爱对方而改变自己的坏习惯，甚至是性情。他们的动机很单纯──因为爱，乐意为对方做一切的事情；不管是大 事还是小事，他们都存着爱去做，不会在事前事后计较得失。

What about our service? Is it merely a facade, having lost the love we first had? What is first love? A man and a woman in love constantly think about how to please each other, and out of love they change their bad habits, even their temperament. Their motives are pure — out of love, they are willing to do anything for their partner. Whether it's a big thing or a small one, they do it out of love, without calculating the gains and losses before or after.

我们的活动是否太多，以至没有时间专注与神的关系？我们是否只干大量外在事务却忽略了内里对神的爱心，把优先次序弄错了？如果我们的敬拜和事奉只是徒具仪式，没有渴慕的心，没有敬畏神的心，我们的聚会、我们的事奉就会缺少神的同在。现在就为自己和你的教会祷告吧。

Are we so overwhelmed with activities that we have no time to focus on our relationship with God? Are we so busy with external things that we neglect our inner love for God, mistaking our priorities? If our worship and service become mere rituals, without a longing heart and a reverent fear of God, our gatherings and our services will lack God's presence. Pray now for yourself and your church.

除此以外，我们还该作什么？  Besides this, what else should we do?

Micah 6:6-8 With what shall I come before the LORD, to bow myself before the Most High God? Shall I offer a burnt offering of calves a year old? 7 Will the LORD be pleased with thousands of rams, or with ten thousand rivers of oil? Shall I offer my firstborn for my transgression, the fruit of my body for the sin of my heart? 8 He has shown you, O man, what is good; and what does the LORD require of you but to act justly, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your God?

第一，我们要行公义。神所作的都是公义的，因此，行公义的一个意思是遵行神的旨意。对当时南国的人来说，神的旨意就是神给他们的律法，包括他们如何看待不同阶层和需要的人士。可是，以赛亚第 1 章指出：他们的手都满了杀人的血；神要他们学习行善，寻求公平，解救受欺压的，给孤儿伸寃，为寡妇辨屈。

First, we must practice justice. All that God does is just. Therefore, practicing justice means obeying God’s will. For the people of the Southern Kingdom at that time, God’s will was the law He gave them, including how they viewed people of different classes and needs. However, Isaiah 1 The chapter points out: Their hands are filled with the blood of murder; God wants them to learn to do good, seek justice, rescue the oppressed, defend the orphans, and plead for the widows.

我们不仅要听道、讲道，更要行道。行道是要付代价的，妥协是容易的，我们在士师记已经学过这个功课。然而，我们知道，遵行神的命令是蒙福的路。

We must not only listen to and preach the Word, but also practice it. Practicing the Word comes at a price, and compromise is easy, as we learned in the book of Judges. However, we know that obeying God's commands is the path to blessing.

 

第二就是好怜悯。你好一样东西，就代表你很喜爱那样东西。好酒的人很喜爱喝酒。同样，好怜悯就是让爱成为你最喜爱的事情──很喜爱去爱别人。弟兄姊妹，爱看不见的神有些时候是很容易的，可是要去爱你看得见的人，有些时候是很难的。爱人就是行道一个最具体的表现。

The second is to love mercy. If you love something, it means you deeply enjoy it. A wine lover loves to drink. Similarly, loving mercy means making love your greatest passion — loving to love others. Brothers and sisters, loving the invisible God is sometimes easy, but loving the people you can see is sometimes difficult. Loving others is the most concrete expression of practicing the Word.

 

第三就是存谦卑的心，与你的神同行。怎样可以行公义，好怜悯？要存谦卑的心，跟着你的神。爱人不是容易的。我们承认我们的不足，倚靠神去爱。一个小孩子跟爸爸走山路，路不好走，上坡的时候，他爸爸在他的旁边，伸出手，准备拉他。要是他不承认自己软弱，不拉着爸爸的手，他就很容易会跌倒，上坡也会感到很吃力。不管遵行神的旨意和爱人有多困难，靠着神的帮助，我们就可以行公义，好怜悯。

The third is to walk humbly with your God. How can we do justice and love mercy? We must do so humbly and follow our God. Loving others is not easy. We must acknowledge our shortcomings and rely on God to love. A child was walking with his father on a difficult mountain path. As they climbed the hill, his father stood by, reaching out his hand to help him. If the child had not acknowledged his weakness and held his father's hand, he would have easily fallen and would have struggled to climb the hill. No matter how difficult it is to obey God's will and love others, with God's help, we can do justice and love mercy .

Hezekiah called it "ingot."

问题：  question:

解释：  explain:

有些犹大王行耶和华眼中看为正的事。希西家登基的时候，拆毁国中的偶像。可是，他却忽略了自己心中的偶像。我们已经提过：以别的事物为你最大的满足或者是保障，那就是你的偶像。希西家除去了外面的偶像，可是他心里面有没有偶像呢？列王纪下 20 章 13 节提到希西家王在巴比伦外交官的面前，炫耀他的财富，没有因为蒙受神的恩典得医治，把荣耀、感谢归给神。他的偶像就是这些东西，就是他的财富。他喜欢巴比伦使者的拜访和问候，大有可能是他想靠巴比伦来对抗亚述。由于他心中有偶像，倚靠自己的财富和巴比伦，不再专心倚赖神，神就差遣先知以赛亚向他宣布：“凡你家里所有的，并你列祖积蓄到如今的，都要被掳到巴比伦去，不留下一样……”(王下 20:17)。也就是说，希西家虽然没有敬拜有形的偶像，却因为倚靠无形的偶像，导致南国将来也要被掳。这是我们一定要记取的教训。

Some of Judah's kings did what was right in the eyes of the Lord. When Hezekiah came to power, he tore down the idols of the land. However, he neglected the idols in his heart. We have already mentioned that if you find your greatest satisfaction or security in something else, that is your idol. Hezekiah removed the idols of the outside world, but did he have idols in his heart? 2 Kings 2 : 0 chapter 1 3 Verse 16 mentions that King Hezekiah flaunted his wealth before the Babylonian diplomats, instead of giving glory and thanks to God for his healing through His grace. His idols were these things— his wealth. He welcomed the visits and greetings from the Babylonian envoys, likely hoping to rely on Babylon to counter Assyria. Because he harbored idols, relying on his wealth and Babylon instead of wholeheartedly relying on God, God sent the prophet Isaiah to announce to him: " All that is in your house, and that your fathers have stored up until this day, will be carried into exile to Babylon; nothing will be left ... " ( 2 Kings 20:17 ) . In other words, although Hezekiah did not worship visible idols, his reliance on invisible idols led to the future captivity of the southern kingdom. This is a lesson we must remember.

当人在思想上开始以无形的偶像的荣耀取代神的荣耀，很自然就会膜拜以石头、木块造成的有形偶像。希西家的儿子玛拿西行神眼中看为恶的事，事奉偶像，比外邦人更甚。虽然之后也曾有过信仰复兴的时期，但拜偶像的情况依然存在。即使有好君王进行宗教改革，但当这些君王去世之后，百姓又再依靠偶像，使拜偶像的风气愈演愈烈，最后甚至连耶和华的圣殿，也摆满了百姓膜拜的其他偶像（参结 8）。在这样的情况下，神又怎能不管教他们呢？

When people begin to replace God's glory with the glory of invisible idols, they naturally begin to worship visible idols made of stone and wood. Hezekiah's son Manasseh did what was evil in God's sight, worshipping idols even more than the pagans. Although there were periods of religious revivals, idolatry persisted. Even when good kings instituted religious reforms, after their death, the people returned to idolatry, further escalating the idolatry until even the temple of the Lord was filled with other idols worshipped by the people (see Ezekiel 8). In such circumstances, how could God not discipline them?

 

 

今天，我们应该不会敬拜有形的偶像，可是我们有否像希西家一样，敬拜无形的偶像呢？你有没有为自己某方面的事情来夸耀呢？让你在神以外，感到很有价值的东西，大有可能就是你的偶像。举例来说，如果你夸你的服事，你的服事就是你的偶像。

Today, we probably don't worship visible idols, but do we, like Hezekiah, worship invisible idols? Do you boast about something in yourself? Anything that makes you feel valuable, apart from God, is likely your idol. For example, if you boast about your service, your service is your idol.

 

 

第 84 步  差遣先知

Step 84​   Sending Prophets

动作：左手升至胸前停下，右手指着前面  

Action: Raise your left hand to your chest and stop, with your right hand pointing forward.

这一步的动作分成两部分：  This step is divided into two parts:

（1）左手手掌张开，由下往上升至胸前，说：“差遣”。左手上升的动作代表兴起，停下来的时候就变成一本圣经。

(2) Pointing forward with the index finger of his right hand (rebuking), he said, “Prophet.”

 

经文：  Scripture:

代下 36:15~16 耶和华他们列祖的神因为爱惜自己的民和他的居所，从早起来差遣使者去警戒他们。16 他们却嬉笑神的使者，藐视他的言语，讥诮他的先知，以致耶和华的忿怒向他的百姓发作，无法可救。

耶 25:3~4 从犹大王亚们的儿子约西亚十三年直到今日，这二十三年之内，常有耶和华的话临到我；我也对你们传说，就是从早起来传说，只是你们没有听从。4 耶和华也从早起来，差遣他的仆人众先知到你们这里来；只是你们没有听从，也没有侧耳而听。

Jeremiah 25:3-4 "From the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, until this day— twenty-three years — the word of the LORD has come to me. I have spoken to you, rising up early and speaking, but you have not listened. 4 The LORD also sent all his servants the prophets to you , rising up early and sending, but you have not listened or inclined your ear to hear."

 

问题：  question:

         神差遣什么人去把他的信息传给百姓？神何时开始差遣这些人？  

         先知耶利米什么时候起来传讲神的话？他当时传了多少年？

解释：  explain:

当南国落在徒具仪式与拜偶像的情况中，神差遣了大量先知去警告南国。先知把神的话告诉他的百姓：如果他们仍然顽梗不听，圣殿便会被毁（参耶 7:12~15），耶路撒冷也会变成颓垣败瓦，并为地上万国所咒诅。并且宣布，他们若不悔改，神的审判就会临到他们。

When the southern kingdom fell into ritualism and idolatry, God sent numerous prophets to warn the southern kingdom. These prophets told the people God's words: if they continued to stubbornly refuse to obey, the temple would be destroyed (see Jeremiah 7 : 12-15 ), Jerusalem would be reduced to ruins, and she would be cursed by all the nations of the earth. They also declared that if they did not repent, God's judgment would come upon them .

先知因为顺服神而传讲信息，他们无疑受了很多的苦，还常常泪流满面；可是，他们仍然继续宣告神的信息。

The prophets undoubtedly suffered greatly and often shed tears because they preached the message in obedience to God; however, they continued to proclaim God's message.

先知虽然带来警告的信息，但同时也带来极大的盼望。他们不但宣告百姓会因为不顺服神而遭受审判，也给他们宣告一个盼望，就是弥赛亚将要来临，建立永恒的国度。

Although the prophets brought messages of warning, they also brought great hope. Not only did they proclaim that the people would be judged for their disobedience to God, but they also gave them hope that the Messiah would come and establish an eternal kingdom.

弟兄姊妹，我们也有一个信息要宣告──这个信息既是警告，又是极大的盼望。我们为宣告这个信息的献身和承担，是否犹如以往的先知？我们是否愿意把福音传遍每个村落、每个家庭，即使被拒绝、被逼迫，甚至被杀害也在所不计？

Brothers and sisters, we too have a message to proclaim — a message that is both a warning and a great hope. Are we as dedicated and committed to proclaiming this message as the prophets of the past? Are we willing to spread the gospel to every village and every home, even if it means rejection, persecution, or even death?

耶利米是犹大末期的一位先知，他的事奉历時四十多年，经历过五个王，其中除了第一个王约西亚之外，其余四个王都行神眼中看为恶的事。他被神差派向自己的民族宣告神的审判，劝喻百姓回头。但可以说，他所说的，没有人听，没有人信。换回来的是别人的凌辱、讥笑、掌掴，陷害；他被下在监里，被放在淤泥及膝的深坑；人视他为敌人，卖国贼。然而，他一直没有放弃，没有停止。为什么他可以这样坚持最少二十三年？

Jeremiah was a prophet in the last days of Judah. His ministry lasted for over forty years and spanned five kings. With the exception of the first king, Josiah, the remaining four kings acted in ways that were evil in the eyes of God. He was sent by God to proclaim God's judgment to his people and to persuade them to turn back. But it can be said that no one listened to or believed what he said. In return, he was subjected to insults, ridicule, slaps, and frame-ups. He was imprisoned and placed in a knee- deep pit of mud . People viewed him as an enemy and a traitor. Yet, he never gave up or stopped. Why was he able to persist for at least twenty-three years?

请大家查看以下耶利米书的经文：7:13、7:25~26、11:7~8、25:3~4、26:5、29:19、 32:33、35:14~15、44:4。在这些经文中，有四个重复出现的字，你能找出来吗？从早起来。耶利米事奉的特点就是从早起来传讲神的话。要从早起来传讲神的话，耶利米就需要每天从早起来先领受神的话。在领受神的话的时候，太阳渐渐升起，即使昨天的传讲没有人听，没有人信，多么令人灰心，然而当他再次领受神的话语时，他心中泛起盼望的曙光。

Please examine the following passages from Jeremiah: 7:13 , 7:25-26 , 11:7-8 , 25:3-4 , 26:5 , 29:19 , 32:33 , 35:14-15 , 44:4 . In these verses, four words appear repeatedly. Can you find them? " Rise up early." The hallmark of Jeremiah's ministry was his early morning preaching of God's word. To preach God's word, Jeremiah needed to first receive God's word each day. As he received God's word, the sun gradually rose. Even if yesterday's preaching had been met with disheartening results, as he received God's word again, a glimmer of hope dawned in his heart.

对他来说，他每天起来，首先想到的，不是事奉的艰难或者令人沮丧的情况，而是先享受神的同在，先聆听神对他说什么。与此同时，每早晨，他再次想到神诸般的慈爱、新的怜悯、极其广大的信实，以及神是他的分，他心里就有指望（参哀 3:20~24）。二十三年，四十年，他每天都是先到神面前亲近他，因此他能在种种的困难和打击中，坚持下去。

For him, the first thing he thought of when he woke up each day was not the difficulties or frustrating circumstances of his ministry, but rather enjoying God's presence and listening to what He said to him. At the same time, every morning he recalled God's many lovingkindnesses, new mercies, and immense faithfulness, as well as the fact that God was his portion. This gave him hope (see Lam. 3 : 20-24 ) . For twenty-three, then forty years, he would first draw near to God each day, enabling him to persevere through all kinds of difficulties and setbacks.

作教导同工是难的，要长期在种种打击当中，坚持作教导同工二十年、四十年就更困难。我们都是教导同工，都要传讲神的话，但愿我们像耶利米那样，每天从早起来，先领受神的话，先想到神的慈爱、怜悯、信实，那我们就能一直坚持下去。

Being a teaching co-worker is difficult. It's even more difficult to persevere in this work for twenty or forty years, even amidst various setbacks. We are all teaching co-workers, and we all need to preach God's word. May we be like Jeremiah, rising early each day to receive God's word first, and to think first of God's love, mercy, and faithfulness. Then we will be able to persevere.

The history of the Southern and Northern Kingdoms, and their decline, can be found in 1 Kings 12-22, the entire book of 2 Kings, and 2 Chronicles 10-36.

哈巴谷是其中一位南国先知，他不明白神怎么会用这样邪恶的国家去审判犹大。你可以从哈巴谷书看到神的回应。神给他的百姓很多从罪恶回转的机会，可是他们总不听从。

Habakkuk was one of the southern prophets who couldn't understand why God would use such a wicked nation to judge Judah. You can see God's response in the book of Habakkuk. God gave his people many opportunities to turn from their sins, but they refused to obey.

 

 

第 86 步  三次被掳

Step 86​   Three captivations

一、第一次被掳：  1. The First Captivity:

经文：  Scripture:

代下 36:5~7 约雅敬登基的时候年二十五岁，在耶路撒冷作王十一年，行耶和华他神眼中看为恶的事。6 巴比伦王尼布甲尼撒上来攻击他，用铜炼锁着他，要将他带到巴比伦去。7 尼布甲尼撒又将耶和华殿里的器皿带到巴比伦，放在他神的庙里。

2 Chronicles 36:5-7 Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem . He did evil in the sight of the Lord his God. 6 Then Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up against him and bound him in bronze chains, intending to take him to Babylon. 7 Nebuchadnezzar also took the vessels of the Lord's temple and brought them to Babylon and placed them in the temple of his god.

 

解释：  explain:

第一次被掳发生在约雅敬作王时。当时巴比伦王侵占犹大，进攻耶路撒冷，并 把犹大王用链子锁起来（参代下 36:6）。但基于某些原因，他决定不把约雅敬带到巴 比伦，反让他得回王位，作傀儡君王。巴比伦王从圣殿取走了一些器皿（参代下 36:5~8；但 1:1~6），把大批战利品及一些聪明的少年人带返巴比伦。

The first captivity occurred during the reign of King Jehoiakim. The king of Babylon invaded Judah, attacked Jerusalem, and imprisoned the king of Judah in chains ( see 2 Chronicles 36 : 6 ). However, for some reason, he decided not to take Jehoiakim to Babylon. Instead, he allowed him to return to the throne as a puppet king. The king of Babylon took some vessels from the temple ( see 2 Chronicles 36 :5-8 ; Daniel 1 : 1-6 ) and took back to Babylon a large amount of spoils and some talented young men.

二、第二次被掳：  2. The Second Captivity:

经文：  Scripture:

王下 24:12~16 犹大王约雅斤王和他母亲、臣仆、首领、太监一同出城，投降巴比伦王，巴比伦王便拿住他，那时比伦王第八年。13 巴比伦王将耶和华殿和王宫里的宝物都拿去了，将以色列王所罗门所造耶和华殿里的金器都毁坏了，正如耶和华说的， 14 又将耶路撒冷的众民和众首领，并所有大能的勇士，共一万人，连一切木匠、铁匠，都掳了去；除了国中极贫穷的人以外，没有剩下的。15 并将约雅斤和王母、后妃、太监，与国中的大官，都从耶路撒冷掳到巴比伦去了。16 又将一切勇士七千人和木匠、铁匠一千人，都是能上阵的勇士,全掳到巴比伦去了。

2 Kings 24:12-16 King Jehoiachin of Judah surrendered to the king of Babylon, along with his mother, his servants, his officials, and his eunuchs. He captured him in the eighth year of his reign. 13 The king of Babylon took all the treasures from the temple of the Lord and the royal palace . He also destroyed all the gold articles of the temple of the Lord that King Solomon of Israel had made, just as the Lord had promised. 14 He also took captive all the people of Jerusalem, all its officials, and all the mighty warriors—10,000 men, including all the carpenters and smiths . Only the poorest of the land remained. 15 He also took Jehoiachin captive from Jerusalem to Babylon, along with the king's mother, his concubines, his eunuchs, and all the great officials of the land. 16 He also took captive all the warriors—7,000 men—and a thousand carpenters and smiths, all capable men of war— all of them taken captive to Babylon.

The Babylonians captured Jehoiachin and took him to Babylon along with many nobles, officials, warriors, and craftsmen ( see 2 Kings 24 : 13-16 ) .

年份：主前 606 年  中国历史：春秋时代

Year: BC 606 Year   Chinese History: Spring and Autumn Period

 

经文：  Scripture:

但 1:1~4  犹大王约雅敬在位第三年，巴比伦王尼布甲尼撒来到耶路撒冷，将城围困。

Daniel 1:1-4   In the third year of the reign of King Jehoiakim of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and besieged it.

2 主将犹大王约雅敬，并神殿中器皿的几分交付他手，他就把这器皿带到示拿地，收入他神的庙里，放在他神的库中。3 王吩咐太监长亚施毗拿，从以色列人的宗室和贵胄中，带进几个人来。4 就是年少没有残疾，相貌俊美，通达各样学问，知识聪明俱备，足能侍立在王宫里的，要教他们迦勒底的文字言语。

2 The Lord gave Jehoiakim king of Judah into his hand, along with some of the vessels of the house of God. He took them to the land of Shinar and brought them to the house of his god, into the treasury of his god. 3 The king commanded Ashpenaz, the chief of his eunuchs, to bring in men from the Israelites, both royal families and nobles. 4 Young men without blemish, handsome, versed in all kinds of learning, knowledgeable and intelligent, qualified to serve in the king's palace, to teach them the language and writing of the Chaldeans.

 

问题：  question:

 谁围困耶路撒冷？

   Who besieged Jerusalem?

 神将什么交付巴比伦王？

   What did God deliver to the king of Babylon?

 巴比伦王吩咐要带进什么来？  巴比伦王要怎样对待他们？

   What did the king of Babylon command to be brought in?    How would the king of Babylon treat them?

 

解释：  explain:

第一次被掳发生在约雅敬作王时。巴比伦王从圣殿取走了一些器皿（参代下 36:5~8；但 1:1~6），把大批战利品及一些聪明的少年人带返巴比伦，目的是以巴比伦文化同化他们，好叫他们在王宫中服事。但以理是其中一个被掳走的少年人，但他决意不被同化，要继续敬拜耶和华（参但 1:5~16）。

The first captivity occurred during the reign of King Jehoiakim. The king of Babylon took some of the temple vessels (see 2 Chronicles 36:5-8 ; Daniel 1 : 1-6 ) and brought back to Babylon a large amount of spoils and some intelligent young men. His goal was to assimilate them into Babylonian culture so that they could serve in the royal palace. Daniel was one of the young men taken away, but he was determined not to be assimilated and continued to worship Jehovah ( see Daniel 1 : 5-16 ) .

没错，巴比伦把他们掳走。为什么他会被掳走？经文说，是神把犹大王和神殿中的一些东西交在巴比伦王尼布甲尼撒手中。由始至终，掌管着犹大国前途的是神。但以理和他的同伴是当时犹大的精英分子，他们被安排学习巴比伦的文化语言、饮食习惯；他们也被改了巴比伦的名字。这样的安排是要再教育他们，把他们同化，使被掳的犹大人失去反抗的力量。当年，日本侵占东北后，强迫当地的小孩子学日语，目的也是要同化新一代的中国人，使他们认同日本人的统治。可是，我们从但以理书第 1 章看见，但以理和他的朋友拒绝被同化，坚守神的律例。

Indeed, Babylon took them captive. Why was he taken captive? The Scriptures state that God delivered the king of Judah and some of the temple goods into the hands of Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon. From beginning to end, it was God who controlled the future of the nation of Judah. Daniel and his companions were the elite of Judah at the time. They were forced to learn Babylonian culture, language, and dietary habits; they were also given Babylonian names. This arrangement was intended to reeducate and assimilate them, thus rendering the captives powerless to resist. Similarly, after the Japanese invaded Manchuria, they forced local children to learn Japanese, also with the goal of assimilating the new generation of Chinese and making them accept Japanese rule. However, as we see in Daniel chapter 1 , Daniel and his companions refused to be assimilated and adhered to God's laws.

魔鬼会用世俗的文化、价值观、道德观，通过网络、电视、游戏、流行歌曲、电影等来同化基督徒，特别是教会里的青少年人。今天，网络实实在在是一个很大的战场。怎样可以对付？我们要从小把圣经的价值观教导我们的孩子。最好是每天晚上，跟他们一起读圣经，一起祷告。

The devil uses secular culture, values, and morals to assimilate Christians, especially young people in the church, through the internet, television, games, pop songs, and movies . Today, the internet is truly a vast battlefield. How can we combat this? We must instill biblical values in our children from a young age. Ideally, read the Bible and pray with them every night.

细读但以理书，我们会发现在那个黑暗的年代，但以理却为神发光，如同天上的光。纵然国破家亡，但他是一位时代的见证者：在众王面前，见证神是至高者；正如尼布甲尼撒所说的，他的神是万神之神，万王之主，超越一切的君王。在众王面前，他们都知道但以理里面有圣神的灵。就是他的老板大利乌王，也知道但以理真正的老板是神，因此称他为永生神的仆人。在神眼中，他是大蒙眷爱的人，是神高度尊重的人。

A careful reading of the book of Daniel reveals that in those dark times, Daniel shone for God like the light of heaven. Despite his nation's destruction and his family's ruin, he stood as a witness for his time: before the kings, he testified that God is the Most High; just as Nebuchadnezzar had declared, his God is the God of gods, the Lord of kings, surpassing all kings. Before the kings, they all recognized that Daniel possessed the Spirit of the Holy Spirit. Even his boss, King Darius, recognized that Daniel's true boss was God and therefore called him a servant of the living God. In God's eyes, he was a man greatly favored and highly respected.

为何但以理可以成为那个黑暗时代的见证者？成为神高度尊重的人？在但以理书中，最常提到但以理做的工作是什么？解梦以外，就是祷告！但他之所以能解梦，也是因为他祷告。即使要被狮子吞吃，他也要祷告；对他来说，祷告比生命更重要！如果我们都想在这个时代有美好的灵性，大有能力地见证主，我们就要想想：祷告在我们生命中到底有多重要？我们有多重视祷告？

Why was Daniel able to bear witness to that dark age? And why was he so highly respected by God? What is Daniel's work most often mentioned in the book of Daniel? Besides interpreting dreams, it's praying! But his ability to interpret dreams was also due to his prayers. Even when facing the threat of being devoured by lions, he prayed; to him, prayer was more important than life itself! If we all want to have a beautiful spiritual life and bear witness to the Lord with great power in this age, we must consider: How important is prayer in our lives? How much do we value prayer?

年份：主前 597 年  中国历史：春秋时代

Year: BC 597 Year   Chinese History: Spring and Autumn Period

 

经文：  Scripture:

代下 36:9~10 约雅斤登基的时候年八岁，在耶路撒冷作王三个月零十天，行耶和华眼中看为恶的事。10 过了一年，尼布甲尼撒差遣人将约雅斤和耶和华殿里各样宝贵的器皿带到巴比伦，就立约雅斤的叔叔（原文是兄）西底家作犹大和耶路撒冷的王。

2 Chronicles 36:9-10 Jehoiachin was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem for three months and ten days. He did evil in the sight of the LORD. 10 After a year, Nebuchadnezzar sent for Jehoiachin and all the valuable vessels of the LORD's temple, and took them to Babylon. Then Nebuchadnezzar made his uncle, Zedekiah , king over Judah and Jerusalem.

结 1:1~3 当三十年四月初五日，以西结在迦巴鲁河边，被掳的人中，天就开了，得见神的异象。2 正是约雅斤王被掳去第五年四月初五日。3 在迦勒底人之地，迦巴鲁河边，耶和华的话特特临到布西的儿子祭司以西结，耶和华的灵降在他身上。

Ezekiel 1:1-3 On the fifth day of the fourth month, in the thirtieth year, while Ezekiel was among the exiles by the River Chebar, the heavens opened, and he saw visions of God. 2 It was the fifth year of King Jehoiachin's exile, on the fifth day of the fourth month. 3 The word of the Lord came expressly to Ezekiel the priest, the son of Buzi, by the River Chebar, in the land of the Chaldeans. The Spirit of the Lord came upon him.

 

问题：  question:

 当神的话临到以西结的时候，以西结正身处什么地方？

   Where was Ezekiel when God’s word came to him?

 

解释：  explain:

虽然约雅敬获巴比伦王容许继续作王，但他仍背叛巴比伦王。当巴比伦大军逼近时，这个犹大王在一次宫廷政变中被弒，由儿子约雅斤继位（主前 597 年）。耶路撒冷被围困三个月后，新王约雅斤向巴比伦王投降，结果被掳到巴比伦（参王下 24:5~16）。巴比伦王班师回国之前，从耶路撒冷带走大批财物，又带走很多工匠。以西结是其中一个被掳去的人（参结 1:2）。

Although Jehoiakim was allowed to continue as king by the Babylonian king, he still rebelled against him. As the Babylonian army approached, the Judean king was assassinated in a palace coup and succeeded by his son Jehoiachin ( 597 B.C. ). After three months of siege on Jerusalem , the new king , Jehoiachin, surrendered to the Babylonian king and was taken captive to Babylon ( see 2 Kings 24 : 5-16 ) . Before returning home, the Babylonian king took a large amount of property from Jerusalem, including many craftsmen. Ezekiel was one of those taken captive ( see Ezekiel 1 : 2 ) .

以西结和一部分被掳之民被安置在巴比伦的迦巴鲁河边。当时是三十年四月初五日，那到底是从哪一年开始算的？很多圣经学者认为这三十年所指的，就是以西结三十岁的时候。

Ezekiel and some of the exiles were placed by the Chebar River in Babylon. It was the fifth day of the fourth month of the 30th year. So, when did the year begin? Many Bible scholars believe that these 30 years refer to when Ezekiel was 30 years old.

假若他没有被掳，三十岁的生日对他来说，应是一个非常特别的日子，因为那日标志着他可以成为祭司（参民 4 章）。然而，那天他身处的地方不是耶路撒冷的圣殿，而是偶像林立的巴比伦；此外，在过去五年，身处巴比伦的他根本没有接受过作祭司的训练。虽然他正踏进人生最精壮的阶段，但他身处在一个不洁净的地方，活在一个由不洁净的人管理的政权之下，他实在看不见自己可以作什么。

Had he not been taken captive, his 30th birthday would have been a very special day for him, marking his entry into priesthood ( see Numbers 4) . However, on that day, he was not in the temple of Jerusalem but in the idolatrous city of Babylon. Moreover, having spent the previous five years in Babylon, he had received no priestly training. Although he was entering the prime of his life, he was in an unclean place, living under a regime ruled by unclean people, and he saw no real role for himself.

相信，以西结不敢奢望在巴比伦会得到什么生日礼物。然而，天却为他开了，以至他得见神的异象，并且神的话特特临到他，临到身处巴比伦的他，并且神的灵降在他身上，给他力量（参结 3:14）。虽然他作不成祭司，但神要他作先知。

I believe Ezekiel didn't dare expect any birthday presents in Babylon. However, the heavens opened for him, allowing him to see visions of God. God's word came specifically to him, even in Babylon. The Spirit of God came upon him, empowering him ( see Ezekiel 3 : 1-4 ) . Although he couldn't be a priest, God wanted him to be a prophet.

以西结蒙召的处境告诉我们：不要以为神只会在一些神圣的场合向我们说话，改变我们的人生；以西结当日的心情，如同我们今天遇上失恋，孩子不听话，工作不顺利时的心情。然而，他的经历指出，当我们感到疲倦、灰心，前路茫茫之际，我们需要把我们的视线转到天上，并且相信困难可以是神改变我们，跟我们建立更深关系和使用我们的起点。与此同时，我们要谨记：纵使从环境来看，神似乎已离开我们，或我们不可能被神使用，但他总不撇下我们，也不丢弃我们（来 12:5）。

Ezekiel's calling reminds us not to assume that God speaks to us and changes our lives only in sacred settings. Ezekiel's feelings that day mirror our own today when we face a breakup, a disobedient child, or setbacks at work. However, his experience suggests that when we feel tired, discouraged, and uncertain about the future, we need to turn our gaze heavenward and believe that difficulties can be the starting point for God to change us, to build a deeper relationship with us, and to use us. At the same time, we must remember: Even if circumstances seem to indicate that God has left us or that we are unusable, He will never leave us nor forsake us ( Hebrews 12 : 5 ).

年份：主前 586 年 7 月 18 日  中国历史：春秋时代

Year: BC 586 Year 7 moon 18 day   Chinese History: Spring and Autumn Period

 

经文：  Scripture:

代下 36:16~18 他们却嬉笑神的使者，藐视他的言语，讥诮他的先知，以致耶和华的忿怒向他的百姓发作，无法可救。17 所以耶和华使迦勒底人的王来攻击他们，在他们圣殿里用刀杀了他们的壮丁，不怜恤他们的少男处女，老人白叟，耶和华将他们都交在迦勒底王手里。18 迦勒底王将神殿里的大小器皿与耶和华殿里的财宝，并王和众首领的财宝，都带到巴比伦去了。

2 Chronicles 36:16-18 But they mocked the messengers of God, despised His words, and scoffed at His prophets, until the LORD 's wrath was kindled against His people, and there was no remedy. 17 Therefore the LORD brought the king of the Chaldeans against them, who killed their young men with the sword in their temple. He had no mercy on young men or virgins, old men or the aged, and He delivered them all into the hand of the king of the Chaldeans. 18 He took all the vessels of the temple of God, both large and small, and the treasures of the temple of the LORD, as well as the treasures of the king and his officials, and took them to Babylon.

 

问题：  question:

 为什么神要把犹大人交在迦勒底王手里？

   Why did God deliver Judah into the hands of the king of the Chaldeans?

  迦勒底王把什么带到巴比伦？

   1. What did the king of the Chaldeans bring to Babylon?

 

解释：  explain:

南国将被尽行灭绝的预言（参耶 25:1~11）最终应验了！真可悲，神的审判终于临到犹大！请留意：经文强调是神使迦勒底人的王，就是巴比伦王来攻击他们；是神将犹大的百姓交在迦勒底王手里。也就是说，表面上是巴比伦王攻破犹大，但其实是神借巴比伦来管教犹大。

The prophecy of the utter destruction of the southern kingdom ( see Jeremiah 25:1-11 ) has finally been fulfilled! Tragically, God's judgment has finally come upon Judah! Note that the text emphasizes that it was God who sent the king of the Chaldeans, the king of Babylon, to attack them; it was God who delivered the people of Judah into the hands of the king of the Chaldeans. In other words, while it was the king of Babylon who ostensibly attacked Judah, in reality, God was using Babylon to discipline Judah.

耶路撒冷至终在主前 586 年被攻陷。圣殿被焚烧，耶路撒冷的城墙也被拆毁；所有值钱的东西，包括圣殿里所有的器皿，都被掳到巴比伦。

Jerusalem finally in 586 B.C. The temple was burned, the walls of Jerusalem were demolished, and all valuables, including all the temple utensils, were taken to Babylon.

这是一个悲惨的故事，一个令人伤心的结局；从中我们知道背叛和犯罪没有好下场。我们犯罪时可能会有片刻欢乐的感觉，但欢乐过后，苦楚却是深远持久。

This is a tragic story with a sad ending; it teaches us that betrayal and sin have no good consequences. We may feel a moment of joy when we sin, but the pain after the joy is profound and lasting.

当撒但引诱你犯罪时，它只会给你看到欢乐的一面，不会给你看到后果。可是，你必须知道罪一定会带来恶果；“不要自欺，神是轻慢不得的”（加 6:7）。犯罪不会有好结局，只会产生恶果。要远离罪恶，为神而活！当你被引诱犯罪时，有一个方法可以帮助你抵挡诱惑，就是想一想罪的后果：它会损害你的见证、你的家庭、你的生命，以至别人的生命──罪给我们的破坏是多么的严重啊！

When Satan tempts you to sin, he only shows you the happy side, not the consequences. However, you must know that sin always has consequences; " Do not be deceived: God is not mocked " ( Gal. 6 : 7 ). Sin has no good ending, only bad consequences. Turn away from sin and live for God! When you are tempted to sin, one way to help you resist is to think about the consequences of sin: it will damage your testimony, your family, your life, and even the lives of others — how devastating is the damage sin can cause us!

Action: Stand with fists in front of chest, then fall down

Make fists with your hands, holding your left and right forearms parallel to your chest. First, say " tear down, " while simultaneously lowering your right hand inward and saying " the temple . " Then, lower your left hand and place it on top of your right arm, saying " and the wall . " The left and right arms represent " the temple " and " the wall, " respectively . ( Say this phrase continuously, in coordination with the movements. )

Year: BC 586 Year   Chinese History: Spring and Autumn Period

 

经文：  Scripture:

问题：  question:

 他们焚烧了什么？

   What did they burn?

  他们拆毁了什么

   What did they demolish?

 

解释：  explain:

耶利米曾预言南国将被尽行灭绝（参耶 25:1~11），这预言最终应验了，真是可悲。圣殿被夷平，耶路撒冷的城墙也被拆毁。这次破坏，摧毁了以色列人信靠的两种东西：

Jeremiah had predicted the utter destruction of the southern kingdom (see Jeremiah 25:1-11), and this prophecy was tragically fulfilled. The temple was razed, and the walls of Jerusalem were demolished. This destruction destroyed two things in which the Israelites had relied:

 圣殿：神对圣殿作了非常宝贵的应许，坚定地承诺以圣殿作为他临在的地方（参代下 7:12；赛 56:7；诗 84:4）。可是百姓逐渐不再信靠圣殿里的神，转为信靠神的圣殿。他们的心扭曲到一个地步，被假先知的大谎话所骗（参耶 7:4），以为只要有了圣殿就可以毫无顾忌、为所欲为。结果连他们所信靠的圣殿都失去了！

 Temple: God made a precious promise regarding the temple, firmly committing it to be the place of His presence (see 2 Chronicles 7:12 ; Isaiah 56:7 ; Psalm 8 : 4 ) . However, the people gradually stopped trusting in the God in the temple and began to trust in God 's temple. Their hearts became so twisted that they were deceived by the false prophets' lies ( see Jeremiah 7 : 4 ), believing that as long as they had the temple, they could do whatever they wanted without restraint. In the end, they even lost the temple they had trusted in!

 耶路撒冷城：神选择在这城立他的名，也对这城作了很多宝贵的应许。神选择在这城立他的名（参代下 6:6；诗 48:1~2，135:21；赛 31:5），但百姓逐渐不再信靠耶路撒冷的神，反而信靠神的耶路撒冷。因此，神誓言要把它毁灭（参结 5:5~8）。百姓这时所犯的罪，与后来耶稣为耶路撒冷哭泣哀叹的罪大致相同（参太 23:37；路 9:41）。神对圣殿的应许是有条件的，这些条件同样适用于耶路撒冷；也就是说，神是否与他们同在，要视乎他们是否信靠神和顺服神而定。

   Jerusalem: God chose to establish his name in this city and made many precious promises to it. God chose to establish his name in this city ( see 2 Chronicles 6:6 ; Psalm 48:1-2 ; 135:21 ; Isaiah 31 : 5 ) . However, the people gradually stopped trusting in the God of Jerusalem and instead trusted in God's Jerusalem. Therefore, God vowed to destroy it ( see Ezekiel 5 : 5-8 ) . The sin the people committed at this time was largely the same sin for which Jesus later wept and lamented over Jerusalem ( see Matthew 23:37 ; Luke 9:41 ) . God's promises to the temple were conditional, and these conditions also applied to Jerusalem; that is, God's presence with them depended on their trust in and obedience to him.

神刑罚的目的是什么？是为要拯救他们，叫他们复兴过来。犹大国的故事并不是到亡国就完了。纵然圣殿和耶路撒冷都被拆毁，可是神一直掌管着一切。我们在下一课就会指出他们的故事还未完结，他们还是有盼望的，因为神是慈爱和信实的！

What was the purpose of God's punishment? It was to save them and restore them. The story of the Kingdom of Judah did not end with the destruction of the nation. Even though the temple and Jerusalem were destroyed, God remained in control. As we will see in the next lesson, their story is not over yet; there is still hope, for God is loving and faithful!

以色列人有神的城和神的殿，但他们却信靠这些，不信靠这城和殿所事奉的神。这情况今天也会发生。人们都说神在中国做了伟大的工作；这个说法大致上是对的。也许你会看见你的教会大幅增长，你应该为此感到欢欣高兴。也许你所用的方法很有效，你也应该为此而欢欣。你们当中也许发生了很多神迹奇事，那也是好的。

The Israelites had God's city and God's temple, but they trusted in these things rather than in the God whom they served. This situation can also occur today. People say that God is doing a great work in China, and this is largely true. Perhaps you are seeing your church grow significantly, and you should rejoice in this. Perhaps the methods you are using are very effective, and you should rejoice in this as well. Perhaps many miracles are happening among you, and that is also good.

然而，你不该信靠神的工作，你要信靠那位作工的神；不该信靠神赐的福，要信靠那位赐福的神。你所依赖的东西无论有多好，你若把它代替了神，就是拜偶像。别忘记希西家给我们的教训。不要把教会、神的工作或神所赐的福变成偶像，这会令神的工作停顿，也会叫神的管教临到你的身上。

However, you shouldn't trust in God's work; you should trust in the God who works. You shouldn't trust in God's blessings; you should trust in the God who blesses. No matter how good the things you rely on are, if you use them in place of God, you are worshiping idols. Don't forget the lesson Hezekiah taught us. Don't make the church, God's work, or the blessings He has given you into idols. This will cause God's work to stall and bring His discipline upon you.

猶大是個小國，耕地面積不到以色列耕地面積的四分之一（以色列耕地的面積約為8896 平方公里）；大概30 萬的人口亦只有以色列的一半。北國擁有最好的農地，雨水也較多，所以形勢較為有利。然而較強大的北國已被滅亡，反剩下南國猶大獨存。

南國不是一個可以向全世界宣揚永生神的名字的超級強國，它只是一個與列強為鄰、飽受列強威脅的小國。北面有巴比倫帝國不斷壯大，南面有埃及逐漸成為區內主要強國，弱小的猶大夾在其中，處境實在岌岌可危！

但猶大有神！它有大君王的城耶路撒冷，有聖殿和神所定立的敬拜；它繼承了聯合王國時期大衛和所羅門留下來的傳統，也延續了神特別應許的大衛王猶大朝（北國以色列卻經歷了九個朝代，先後有十九個王統治）。在耶路撒冷的聖殿有合宜的祭司制度，也有對耶和華的敬拜，這是南國不斷得到力量和保護的重要原因。

只要百姓繼續順服神和敬拜神，祂便會與他們同在。由於有神的蔭庇，所以即使南北兩面都有強敵，小國猶大仍是安全的。不過，南國的百姓一定要跟隨神，全能的神才會應許保護他們。可惜南國的百姓沒有一直跟隨神；下一步我們將看到導致猶大滅亡的其中一個主要原因。
