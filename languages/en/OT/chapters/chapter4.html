    一、历史重演：小的跟大的相争  1. History Repeats Itself: Small vs. Big

    起初，两姊妹为了抓着丈夫的心而大大相争。她们给儿子所起的名字都充满了 “争胜”的味道。最典型的例子就是拿弗他利，他名字的意思就是“相争”。

    At first, the two sisters fiercely competed for their husbands' affections. The names they gave their sons all hinted at " strife . " The most typical example was Naphtali, whose name means " strife . "

    利亚以为她为雅各生了孩子后，雅各就会爱她，与她联合，但事实并非如此。至于拉结，她并没有因为雅各宠爱她而感到满足，反而因为自己未能生育而嫉妒姐姐，担心自己在雅各心中的位置被姐姐取代。两姊妹越争越烈，甚至把自己的使女给雅各为妾也在所不惜。本来要争的是丈夫的宠爱，最后却主动把自己的使女送给丈夫为妾。一夫两妻已经够麻烦，一夫两妻两妾就更麻烦，事情的发展是多么的荒谬。

    Leah thought that if she bore Jacob a child, he would love her and unite with her, but this was not the case. As for Rachel, she was not content with Jacob's favor. Instead, she became jealous of her sister because she herself was barren and feared she would be replaced in Jacob's heart. The two sisters' quarrel intensified, even going so far as to give their maidservants to Jacob as concubines. They had originally been vying for their husband's favor, but in the end, they had offered their maidservants to him as concubines. Having two wives was troublesome enough, but having two wives and two concubines was even more problematic. What a ridiculous development !

    雅各日间放羊已经很疲累，回到家中，还要面对这样的家庭，令他更疲累。对两位妻子而言，他在家中的作用就是跟她们或她们的使女生孩子，满足她们争胜的欲望；在娶妾和给孩子起名的事上，他一直没有发言权。在整段经文，他只说过一句话；除了生气外，经文并没有提到他任何其他的感受。毕竟这些事情──小的跟大的相争，大的跟小的相争，如同一面镜反照他的本相──他之所以要投靠拉班，被拉班欺骗，被迫娶了自己不喜欢的利亚，最终弄到两姊妹相争，一切都源于他昔日作为小的，要跟大的（以扫）相争，用自己的方法去争取神要给他的福气。面对此情此景，他无话可说，但百般滋味在心头。

    Jacob was already exhausted from a day of tending the sheep, and returning home to face this family situation only further wore him out. His role in the household was to father children with either his wives or their maids, satisfying their desire for dominance. He had no say in the matter of concubines or the naming of his children. Throughout the entire passage, he uttered only one sentence ; aside from anger, the text does not mention any other feelings. After all, these events — the younger squabbling with the older, the older squabbling with the younger—served as a mirror reflecting his true self . His decision to join Laban, his deception by Laban, his forced marriage to Leah, whom he disliked, and the resulting conflict between the two sisters all stemmed from his past as the younger, his desire to compete with the older (Esau), using his own methods to secure the blessings God intended for him. Faced with this situation, he was speechless, yet a mixture of emotions filled his heart. 

    二、神的介入  II. Divine Intervention

    我们刚才所读的经文有一个一再重复的内容，是关乎神的。你能找出来吗？

    There is a recurring theme in the passage we just read that concerns God. Can you find it?

    ·        创 29:31     耶和华见利亚失宠，就使他生育…

    ·         Creation 29:31      When the Lord saw that Leah had been disgraced, he opened her womb ...

    ·        创 30:17     神应允了利亚，他就怀孕，给雅各生了第五个儿子。

    ·         Creation 30:17      God answered Leah's wish, and she conceived and gave birth to Jacob's fifth son .

    ·        创 30:22     神顾念拉结，应允了他，使他能生育。

    ·         Creation 30:22      God cared about Rachel and answered his call, allowing him to have children .

    雅各很被动，很无奈，但神却主动介入。圣经的作者在经文的开始、结尾都强调神顾念利亚和拉结，应允了她们，使她们能生育。正如雅各所说的，使他的妻子能生孩子的是神（参创 30:2）。

    Jacob was passive and helpless, but God intervened. The biblical author emphasizes at the beginning and end of the passage that God cared for Leah and Rachel and granted them the power to bear children. As Jacob said, it was God who enabled his wife to bear children ( see Genesis 30 : 2 ) .

     

    无论雅各为人有多大问题，他的婚姻有多混乱，他两个妻子有多不和，有多大的相争，她们所作的有多荒谬，神仍然掌管着一切。整段经文强调她们和她们的使女之所以能生育，是因为神顾念她们，听见和应允她们的呼求。正如雅各所说的，一切不在乎人的力量，乃在乎神自己。

    No matter how problematic Jacob's character was, how chaotic his marriage was, how much his wives disagreed, how much they argued, and how absurd their actions were, God was still in control. The entire passage emphasizes that the reason they and their maidservants could have children was because God cared for them, heard them, and answered their cries. As Jacob said, everything depends not on human power but on God himself.

    他们不知道的是，神要借这十二个儿子，实现他对亚伯拉罕所立的约的其中一个部分：后裔。他们的偏心、嫉妒、相争、混乱、荒谬，不单不能拦阻神实现他对亚伯拉罕的应许，反而在他们不知不觉中，“成就”了神对亚伯拉罕的应许，使神救赎的计划向前再迈进一步。

    What they didn't know was that God would use these twelve sons to fulfill one part of His covenant with Abraham : descendants. Their partiality, jealousy, strife, confusion, and absurdity not only failed to prevent God from fulfilling His promise to Abraham , but, unknowingly , they " fulfilled " God's promise to Abraham, moving His plan of redemption one step further.

    然而，不管我们所身处的环境，充斥着多少混乱、荒谬的事情，有多少事情是我们无力处理或解决的，我们仍要谨记：神可以借这些事情，来成就他给我们的应许和计划。下一步关乎约瑟被卖到埃及之后所发生的事情，就是一个最典型的例证。

    However, no matter how chaotic and absurd our circumstances may be, or how many things we are powerless to handle or resolve, we must remember that God can use these things to fulfill His promises and plans for us.     约瑟的人生跟梦有密切的关系，甚至他的兄弟以“那做梦的”来形容他（参创37:19）。约瑟一生的故事非常传奇，真的好像做梦一般。

    Joseph's life was so closely connected to his dreams that his brothers even described him as "the dreamer" (Genesis 37:19). The story of Joseph's life is so legendary that it truly feels like a dream.

     

    第一段经文指出，约瑟最终被卖给埃及法老的一个侍臣波提乏。第二段经文说明：打从他被卖到埃及到他坐牢，他一直认定自己是被卖到埃及的。第三段经文却指出，当他成为了埃及全地的宰相，回望过去的时候，他却从另一个角度来看整件事：他不再认为自己是被卖到埃及，却重复提到是神差他到埃及来的，并且是神使他成为法老的顾问、埃及最高的官员和埃及全地的宰相，以致在全地大饥荒的时候，可以保存他们一家人的性命。为何他的看法会有这么大的改变？现在就让我们重温他在埃及的一些人生片断。

    The first passage indicates that Joseph was eventually sold to Potiphar, a servant of Pharaoh. The second passage explains that from the time he was sold to Egypt until his imprisonment, he always believed that he had been sold there. The third passage, however, indicates that when he became the prime minister of Egypt, he looked back and saw the whole thing from a different perspective: he no longer considered himself sold into Egypt, but repeatedly stated that it was God who sent him there and made him Pharaoh's advisor, Egypt's highest official, and prime minister, thus saving his family from a severe famine. Why did his perspective change so dramatically? Let us now review some of his life in Egypt.

    约瑟一生的故事说明：不管是看似偶然发生的事，还是绝对不可能是偶然发生的事；不管是好事，还是坏事；是大错失，还是小错失；是自然气候的事，还是政治的事；是大事，还是小事；是我们能控制的，还是我们不能控制的；所有事情都在那位用他权能的命令托住万用的神的护理之下（参来 1:3）。按圣经原文，“托住”的意思不单是支撑着，还有“带着”的意思，也就是说，神能够把事情带到他所指定的地步。埃及有很多大户人家，但若不是被卖到波提乏的家，又怎会被下在法老的监里；若不是被下在法老的监里，又岂能为酒政解梦？若酒政被释放后，记起约瑟，约瑟就会被释放回家，又岂能成为埃及的宰相……？

    The story of Joseph's life illustrates that whether it seems to happen by chance or something that absolutely could not have happened by chance; whether it is good or bad; a big mistake or a small mistake; a matter of natural climate or politics ; a major matter or a minor matter; something we can control or something we cannot control; all things are under the providence of God, who upholds all things by His powerful word ( see Hebrews 1: 3 ). According to the original text of the Bible , " uphold " means not only to support but also to " carry, " that is, God can bring things to the point He has designated. There were many wealthy families in Egypt, but if they had not been sold to Potiphar's house, how could they have been thrown into Pharaoh's prison? If they had not been thrown into Pharaoh's prison, how could they have interpreted the butler's dream? If the butler had remembered Joseph after being released, Joseph would have been released and returned home, and how could he have become the prime minister of Egypt ... ?

    从雅各之所以得到十二个儿子和约瑟由于被卖到埃及而最终成为埃及的宰相，我们可以看见，不管事情多么混乱，中间夹杂了多少的嫉妒、错失、痛苦，然而神掌管一切，并且能够使万事互相效力，叫爱他的人得益处，甚至可以使苦难化成祝福，使恶事变成好事，使黑暗变为光明。

    What was the purpose of the Exodus?     Exodus is undoubtedly a book about salvation, but God's purpose in saving the Israelites was to have them serve Him. This is also God's purpose in saving us.     Church leaders should cooperate with God to bring believers into ministry. However, many leaders tend to take on all the ministry themselves. You too must serve well and become instruments of blessing to others!

    We all know that Pharaoh's heart was hard. Why did God allow Pharaoh's heart to remain so hard?
