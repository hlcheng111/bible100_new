（一）神创造天地

1. God Creates the Heaven and Earth

第一件世界大事是神创造了世界和人类。  The first great world event was God's creation of the world and mankind.

创世记第 1 章是一篇关于创造的篇章，记载神用六日创造天地万物，包括人类。神充满慈爱，他有计划地先为动植物预备环境，包括光、空气、水、陆地、洋海等，然后才依次创造植物和动物，最后才创造人。

Genesis 1 is a chapter about creation, recording how God created the heavens and earth, including humanity, in six days. Lovingly, God first planned to prepare the environment for plants and animals, including light, air, water, land, and oceans. He then created plants, animals, and finally humans.

神看着他所造的很是满意，圣经一次次说：“神看着是好的。”（创 1:4、10、12、 18、21、25）神创造人之后，圣经更加说：“神看着一切所造的都甚好。”（创 1:31）即使今天，虽然罪对万物造成了影响，但我们仍然觉察得到神的创造是美好的，宇宙和其中的万物还是可爱和美丽的。

God was very pleased with what He had created. The Bible says again and again : “ God saw that it was good. ” ( Genesis 1:17 ) 1 : 4 , 10 , 12 , 18 , 21 , 25 ) After God created man, the Bible further says: “ God saw everything he had made, and, behold, it was very good. ” ( Genesis 1 :4, 10, 12, 18, 21, 25) 1:31 ) Even today, despite the impact of sin on all things, we still perceive that God’s creation is good, and the universe and everything in it are still lovely and beautiful.

人有时会问，宇宙真的是神创造的吗？罗马书 1 章 20 节说：“自从造天地以来，神的永能和神性是明明可知的，虽是眼不能见，但借着所造之物，就可以晓得，叫人无可推诿。” 我们从宇宙和其中万物的奇妙和令人赞叹的设计，可以推断出必有一位充满智慧的设计者。当我们看到我们的身体，不同器宫、系统之间如何复杂地、紧密地远作，就可以断定那位设计者必定极有智慧。神的永能和神性实在借着所造之物就可以晓得。

People sometimes ask, did God really create the universe? Romans 1:20 says , " For since the creation of the world His invisible attributes, His eternal power and divine nature, have been clearly seen, being understood through what has been made, so that they are without excuse. " The wondrous and awe-inspiring design of the universe and everything in it can infer the existence of an intelligent Designer. When we see how our bodies, with their various organs and systems, are intricately and closely interwoven, we can conclude that that Designer must be extremely intelligent. God's eternal power and divine nature can truly be seen through what has been made.

 

看见别人手腕上名贵的手表，我们也想知道是哪里制造的。因为我们希望知道这么漂亮的手表是谁制造的。然而，当你问那人他的手表是哪里造的，他说：“是这样的，首先几百万年前，有一块金属经过风吹雨打，变成了一个小箭头的样子，一端有一个小孔。而在另一个地方，也有一块金属变成了一个比较小的箭头，上面有着相同的小孔，颜 色和阔度跟先前那个一模一样。然后，有一天突然刮起风暴，把两个箭头和一个小轴吹在一起，刚好使这三样东西连接起来，那轴漂亮地装配在两个箭头的小孔上……” 那人还继续解释表面、齿轮、表带和电池等，怎样在风雨中渐渐成形。你会相信那人的说话吗？

When we see someone wearing a fancy watch, we wonder where it was made. We want to know who made such a beautiful watch. However, when you ask them where their watch was made, they explain: "Millions of years ago, a piece of metal, weathered by wind and rain, became shaped like a small arrowhead with a small hole at one end. Elsewhere, another piece of metal was formed into a smaller arrowhead with the same hole, the same color and width. Then, one day, a storm suddenly blew together the two arrowheads and a small shaft, connecting them perfectly. The shaft fits beautifully into the holes of the two arrowheads..." The man then goes on to explain how the watch face, gears, bracelet, and battery were gradually formed by the wind and rain. Would you believe him?

你当然不会。可是，当我们观察比手表还要精密亿万倍的人类脑袋时，我们却竟然假定它只是偶然地踫撞出来的！有一位科学家这样论及人类的脑袋：

Of course not. Yet, when we look at the human brain, which is a billion times more precise than a watch, we assume it was created by chance! One scientist said this about the human brain:

“如果把人类脑袋所盛载的资料内容写出来……可以装满 2000 万册图书，等同

“ If the information stored in the human brain were written down … it would fill 20 million books, equivalent to

世上最大图书馆的藏书量。换句话说，每个人的脑袋中，都藏有 2000 万册图书的内容。脑袋是个只占很小空位的巨大空间，其神经化学活动出奇地忙碌，思路系统比任何人类设计出来的东西都要奇妙。”

The world's largest library holds as many books as it could hold. In other words, each of us holds the contents of 20 million books within our heads. The brain is a vast space occupying a tiny space, its neurochemical activity incredibly bustling, its thought processes more marvelous than anything humankind has devised .

另一位科学家计算，一个单细胞动物纯因偶然而出现的可能性是 10-40,000（也就是 1 之后加上 40,000 个 0 分之一；而复杂如人类的生物，纯因偶然出现的可能性，更是低得难以计算，因为一个成人就有 40 至 60 万亿个细胞。

Another scientist calculated that the probability of a single-celled organism arising by pure chance is 1 in 10 -40,000 (that is, 1 in Then add 40,000 0​ One in ten; and the probability of a creature as complex as humans appearing purely by chance is so low that it is difficult to calculate, because an adult human has 40 to 60 trillion cells.

神借着他所造的天地万物，彰显他的智慧、能力和慈爱，让我们可以认识他、赞美他。因此，当我们看见壮阔的河流、巍巍的高山，看见雷电交加，我们应晓得神的大能大力，并为此感谢神；当我们看见落日美景、娇花绽放，蝴舞翩翩时，我们应晓得神的温柔和慈爱，并为此感谢神；当我们感到阳光温暖、凉风送爽，听见小鸟轻歌，我们应晓得神的温柔和怜悯，并为此感谢神。

God demonstrates His wisdom, power, and lovingkindness through all things He created, allowing us to know and praise Him. Therefore, when we see majestic rivers, towering mountains, and thunder and lightning, we should know God's might and strength and thank Him for it. When we see the beauty of a sunset, the blooming of delicate flowers, and the dancing of butterflies, we should know God's gentleness and lovingkindness and thank Him for it. When we feel the warmth of the sun, the cool breeze, and hear the gentle song of birds, we should know God's tenderness and mercy and thank Him for it.

（二）人生意义跟世界的起源是分不开的

2. The meaning of life is inseparable from the origin of the world.

“起初神创造天地”是圣经第一句说话。这是一句非常重要的说话，因为他不仅指出天地是神创造的，也告诉我们，只有在这位创天造地的神那里，我们才能找到人生意义，因为人生意义跟世界的起源是分不开的。

" In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth " is the first sentence in the Bible. This is a very important statement because it not only indicates that the heavens and the earth were created by God, but also tells us that only in this God who created the heavens and the earth can we find the meaning of life, because the meaning of life is inseparable from the origin of the world.

人活着需要意义，同意吗？那么，人生有什么意义？要回答这个问题：我们首先要问：“我为何会生在这个世上？”“父母生了我。”那么父母为何会生在这个世上？父母的父母生了他们……，这样一直追问下去，最终必定要问：“这个世界和其上的一切，包括人类的始祖，究竟从何而来？”

Life needs meaning, don't you agree? So, what is the meaning of life? To answer this question, we must first ask : " Why was I born into this world? " " My parents gave birth to me. " So why were my parents born into this world? Their parents' parents gave birth to them ... If we continue this line of questioning, we must ultimately ask : " Where did this world and everything in it , including the first human ancestors, come from? "

“神创造天地”指出这个世界和其上的一切都是神所造的，都是属于神的。因此，神是这个世界的主人，是这个宇宙的大君王；他也是我们人生的主人，是我们的王。

" God created the heavens and the earth " indicates that this world and everything in it were created by God and belong to Him. Therefore, God is the master of this world and the great king of the universe; He is also the master of our lives and our king.

只有发明机器的人才知道机器存在的意义（作用）；同样，只有创造这个世界和人的神，才能告诉我们人活在世上的意义。如果我们离开神，离开我们人生的主人，我们就失去了作为人的根本意义。不是说，我们的人生完全没有意义，而是说，即使我们住得好，吃得好，我们的心仍然会觉得像有个洞，像欠缺了什么似的。正如先知耶利米所说的，神是活水的泉源，离弃神是恶事、是苦事，其他一切代替神的东西如同破裂不能存水的池子，都不能满足我们心灵最深的需要（参耶 2:13、19）。所以，不管发生了什么事，我们一辈子都要认定神是我们独一的主，我们不要离弃他

Only the person who invented the machine knows the meaning of the machine's existence (its function) ; similarly, only God, who created this world and mankind, can tell us the meaning of human life. If we leave God, the master of our lives, we will lose the fundamental meaning of being human. It is not to say that our lives are completely meaningless, but that even if we live well and eat well, our hearts will still feel like there is a hole, as if something is missing. As the prophet Jeremiah said, God is a fountain of living water. It is evil and bitter to abandon God. All other things that replace God are like broken pools that cannot hold water. They cannot meet the deepest needs of our hearts (see Jeremiah 2:13 , 19. Therefore, no matter what happens, we must recognize that God is our only Lord throughout our lives, and we must not abandon Him .

（三）人生意义一：敬拜神，与神相交、相爱

(3 ) The meaning of life: worshipping God, fellowshipping with God, and loving God

创 2:7    耶和华神用地上的尘土造人，将生气吹在他鼻孔里，他就成了有灵的活人，名叫亚当。

Genesis 2:7 The LORD God formed man of dust from the ground and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living being. His name was Adam.

神如何造人？创世记第 1 章七次提到神说要有什么，就有什么，事情就按神所说的成就了。可是神没有说：“要有亚当……”。神好像一个陶匠，特特用尘土造第一个人，因此人是神亲手造的杰作。

How did God create man? Genesis 1 Seven times in this chapter, God mentions that whatever He says will happen will happen, and things will be fulfilled as He says. However, God does not say, " Let there be Adam ..." God is like a potter, specifically forming the first man from dust . Therefore, man is a masterpiece created by God's own hands.

当神亲手造人的时候，圣经进一步描述：神把生气吹在人的鼻里，好像跟人面对面，给人轻轻一吻似的，使人成为有灵的活人。在地上，一个人之所以是活人而不是死人，是因为他的灵魂仍在他的身体里面；身体没有灵魂就是死的（参王上 17:22）。很有可能，当神把生气吹在亚当的鼻里的时候，神同时把灵给亚当。整个过程告诉我们，在神所造的万物中，唯独人是神亲手造的，并且是有灵的；人在神心中，有一个非常独特的位置；人并不是众多受造物之一而已。

When God personally created man, the Bible further describes this: God breathed into man's nostrils the breath of life, as if meeting him face to face and giving him a gentle kiss, thus making man a living being with a soul. On earth, a person is alive, not dead, because their soul remains within their body; a body without a soul is dead (see 1 Kings 17:22 ) . It is very likely that when God breathed into Adam's nostrils the breath of life, he also gave him his spirit. This entire process tells us that of all God's creations, only man was created by God himself and possesses a soul. Man holds a very unique place in God's heart; he is not just one of many creatures.

神是个灵，他赐灵给人说明了神造人的目的：他要人跟他相处交往，享受他的爱和同在，跟他建立并发展深度的关系。为什么会这样？因为神爱人，他愿意人作他的儿女，并且要赐福与人。正如我们爱我们的儿女，我们不单想跟儿女好好相处交往，还要把好东西给他们。神为什么要造人？人生有什么意义？人是“成了有灵的活人”告诉我们，神造人的目的和我们的人生意义：敬拜神，与神相交、相爱，享受神的同在。(天使也有灵，可是，在神的计划里，它们只是神的仆役，被神差来帮助我们。)

God is a Spirit, and His gift of spirit to man explains His purpose in creating humanity: He wants humans to interact with Him, enjoy His love and presence, and establish and develop a deep relationship with Him. Why is this so? Because God loves humanity, He desires humans to be His children, and He wants to bless them. Just as we love our children, we not only want to interact well with them but also give them good things. Why did God create humanity? What is the meaning of life? The fact that humans have become " living beings with spirits " tells us God's purpose in creating humanity and the meaning of our lives: to worship God, to fellowship with Him, to love Him, and to enjoy His presence. ( Angels also have spirits, but in God's plan, they are merely His servants, sent to help us. )

如何敬拜？如何与神相交？  How to worship? How to communicate with God?

诗篇第 8 篇的作者观看神创造的伟大的同时，惊讶感恩：人算什么，你竟顾念他？世人算什么，你竟眷顾他？他深深感受到：人虽然渺小，但神对人恩情是何等的独特、浩大。

Psalm 8 As the author of this passage observed the majesty of God's creation, he was filled with wonder and gratitude: "What is man, that you are mindful of him? What is the son of man, that you care for him?" He deeply felt that although man is insignificant, God's grace toward him is unique and immense.

在农业社会，人们都知道，努力耕种重要，但要好的收成，更需要适量的雨水、清风、阳光，甚至是适当和适量的昆虫（例如传播花粉的蜜蜂），不然农作物是没法健康地长起来的。收成的时候，他们特别的喜悦，因为他们感到天地的恩情。可是今天我们在超市看见一袋袋的米，我们好像没有什么感觉。

In agricultural societies, people understood that hard work was important, but a good harvest also required adequate rain, fresh air, sunlight, and even the right and appropriate number of insects (such as bees, which spread pollinators ) . Without them, crops would not grow healthily. Harvest time brought special joy, for they felt the blessings of heaven and earth. Yet, today, when we see bags of rice in the supermarket, we seem to feel nothing.

活在现代的社会，我们不要视健康、寿命为理所当然，因为我们的生活、动作、存留都在乎神（参徒 17:28)。当下这一刻就是一份礼物，每一天都是神给我们爱的礼物。主若愿意，我们就可以活着，也可以作这事、作那事。因此，我们要常常认定我们所得的一切都是出于神对我们的恩情。如果我们有这样的体会，我们参加敬拜时，就会很容易投入其中，因为我们心被恩感，就自然会歌颂神；每天灵修也不再是例行公事，而有另一番滋味。

Living in modern society, we should not take our health and longevity for granted, for our lives, our actions, and our very being depend on God ( see Acts 17:28) . This present moment is a gift; every day is God's gift of love to us. If the Lord wills, we can live and do this and that. Therefore, we should always recognize that everything we receive is due to God's grace toward us. If we have this understanding, we will easily immerse ourselves in worship, for our hearts will be moved by grace and we will naturally sing praises to God. Daily devotion will no longer be a routine, but will have a new meaning.

（四）人生意义二：按神的旨意生活，反映神的荣耀

(4 ) The Second Meaning of Life: Living According to God’s Will and Reflecting God’s Glory

创 1:26   神说：我们要照着我们的形像、按着我们的样式造人……我们是“有灵的活人”。我们的灵到底有什么特质？我们是按神的形像和样式造的。由于每一个人都是照着神的形像、按着神的样式造的，因此，每一个人，不管性别、种族、长相、身体有没有残缺，都有与生的俱来的价值和尊严，并且都是平等的。

Creation 1:26    God said: "Let Us create man in Our image, after Our likeness "… We are " living beings with spirits . " What exactly are the characteristics of our spirits? We are created in the image and likeness of God . Because every person is created in the image and likeness of God, therefore, everyone, regardless of gender , race, appearance, or physical disability, has inherent worth and dignity and is equal.

我们是按神的形像和样式所造的，也就是说，某些神拥有的特质，我们也有。圣经说，神就是爱；因此，我们里面也有爱；于是，人间有情，地上有爱。圣经也说神就是光，他所做所想的都是美的，都是善的，没有半丁点的邪恶的意念；所以，按神原本的设计，人的本质也是善的，不喜欢邪恶的事情。

God created man with a desire for fellowship and love. He also desires for man to live in obedience to His will, to live out His image and likeness, and to reflect His glory. God not only gives man free will, the ability to make choices, but also provides objective circumstances for making these choices. Just as in the " order whatever you want " event just now, where the customer had no other options except beef ramen, if objective circumstances left man with no other choice but to love and obey God, then man would not truly be able to exercise his free will to choose whether to love God or not, and his " love " and " obedience " toward God would be meaningless.

Therefore, God placed the tree of the knowledge of good and evil in the garden as a boundary, allowing Adam and Eve to exercise their free will every day to choose to love and obey God, to choose not to eat the fruit from the tree, and to stay within this boundary. This was a simple and easy task. Keeping this boundary represented Adam and Eve's love and obedience to God, and it was also their duty to love and obey God.

Genesis 3:15 “ And I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your offspring and her offspring; he will bruise your head, and you will strike his heel. ”

这是第一节提到神应许人一位救主的经文，而且神在应许了人救主之后，才宣告他对始祖惩罚，可见神对人的爱和怜悯。

This is the first verse that mentions God’s promise of a Savior to mankind. Moreover, God only declared His punishment on the first ancestors after promising them a Savior. This shows God’s love and mercy for mankind.

“女人的后裔”暗示那将要来的救主是童女所生（参赛 7:14）。“要伤你（蛇）的头”，这位救主要给魔鬼致命一击，他要败坏那掌死权的魔鬼，除灭魔鬼的作为（参来二 14，约壹 3:8）。蛇“要伤他（救主）的脚跟”暗示救主将要承受的苦难。

“ The seed of the woman ” implies that the coming Savior will be born of a virgin ( see Isaiah 7:14 . “ He will bruise your (the serpent ’s) head ” , this Savior will deal a fatal blow to the devil. He will defeat the devil who has the power of death and destroy the devil’s works (see Hebrews 2:14 , 1 John 3 : 8 ). The serpent “ will bruise his (the Savior’s) heel ” implies the suffering that the Savior will endure.

 

2.      神作皮衣给始祖穿

2.       The legendary leather jacket was given to the ancestor

创 3:21  耶和华神为亚当和他妻子用皮子作衣服给他们穿。

Genesis 3:21   The LORD God made coats of skin for Adam and his wife and clothed them.

罪带来羞耻（参创 3:7），并且令人要终身劳苦才能得吃（参创 3:7）。天起了凉风（参创 3:8），神用“皮子作衣服”给始祖穿，显出神对人无微不至的爱，皮衣可以遮羞，可以保暖，可以在劳苦工作中保护身体。

Sin brings shame (see Genesis 3 : 7 , and requires people to work hard all their lives to get food (see Genesis 3 : 7 ). A cool breeze blew (see Genesis 3 : 8 , and God made " clothes of skins " for the first ancestors to wear, showing God's meticulous love for man. Leather clothes can cover shame, keep warm, and protect the body during hard work.

另外，要作皮衣必须要有牲畜被杀，流血牺牲，暗示要遮盖罪恶的羞耻，要罪得赦免，就必须流血舍命（参来 9:22）。而人类的献祭给神大概也起源于此。

Furthermore, to make leather garments, animals had to be killed and their blood sacrificed, implying that to cover the shame of sin and to obtain forgiveness of sins , blood had to be shed and lives had to be sacrificed ( see Hebrews 9:22 ) . Human sacrifices to God probably also originated from this.

第一件大事“创造”，提醒我们：我们是按神形像造的，我们要与神相交相爱，活出荣耀神的生活；第二件大事“堕落”，提醒我们：要信任神对我们的爱，不要自以为是，以为可以不用倚靠神，而且虽然神有怜悯和拯救，但犯罪必定会有后果；如果我们以为不听神的话，离弃神，我们会生活得更自由、更幸福、更快乐，那么我们只是被罪恶的糖衣所欺骗，情况就像昔日亚当、夏娃吃了分别善恶树的果子，要面对犯罪带来的罪疚、羞愧、痛苦和管教。

The first major event, “ Creation , ” reminds us that we are created in God’s image and that we should fellowship and love God and live a life that glorifies God. The second major event , “ The Fall, ” reminds us that we must trust God’s love for us and not be self-righteous , thinking that we do not need to rely on God. Moreover, although God has mercy and salvation, sin will inevitably have consequences. If we think that by disobeying God’s words and abandoning God, we will live a freer, happier, and more joyful life, then we are simply deceived by the sugar-coating of sin. Our situation is just like when Adam and Eve ate the fruit of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil and had to face the guilt, shame, pain, and discipline that sin brings.

经文：  Scripture:

创 6:5~6   耶和华见人在地上罪恶很大，终日所思想的尽都是恶，6 耶和华就后悔造人在地上，心中忧伤。

Genesis 6:5-6 The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great on the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually . 6 The LORD was sorry that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved the LORD at his heart.

创 6:13~14   神就对挪亚说：“凡有血气的人，他的尽头已经来到我面前；因为地上满了他们的强暴，我要把他们和地一并毁灭。14 你要用歌斐木造一只方舟…

Genesis 6:13-14 Then God said to Noah, “ The end of all flesh has come before me, for the earth is filled with violence because of them. I will destroy them with the earth. 14 Build yourself an ark of gopher wood …

 

问题：  question:

 根据创世记 6 章 13 至 14 节，神说他要做什么？他为什么要这样做？他吩咐挪亚做什么？

   According to Genesis 6 Chapter 13 to 14 What did God say he would do? Why did he do it? What did he tell Noah to do?

解释：  explain:

第三件大事是“洪水”。在这件事中，神使一场洪水淹没整个大地，你可以在创世记第 6 至 9 章读到这件大事的详情。

The third great event was the " Flood . " In this event, God caused a flood to cover the entire earth. You can read the details of this event in Genesis chapters 6-9 .

自亚当、夏娃堕落后，人还是不断犯罪。到了挪亚那一代，神见人在地上的罪恶很大，终日所思想的尽都是恶，地上满了强暴，神决定要毁灭一切。而在那个罪恶滔天的世代、不敬虔的世代（彼后 2:5），唯有挪亚在神眼前蒙恩。神吩咐挪亚建造方舟，让进方舟的人可以免被洪水毁灭。挪亚因信，动了敬畏的心，按神的意思建造一艘方舟（大约长 137 米，阔 23 米，高 14 米）。

Since the fall of Adam and Eve, humanity has continued to sin. By the time of Noah's generation, God saw the great wickedness of mankind on earth . Every intention of the thoughts of the earth was only evil, and the earth was filled with violence. God decided to destroy everything. Yet, in that age of rampant sin and ungodliness (2 Peter 2 : 5 ), only Noah found favor in God's sight. God commanded Noah to build an ark to protect those who entered it from the flood. Moved by faith and reverence, Noah built an ark according to God's instructions ( approximately 137 meters long, 23 meters wide, and 14 meters high).

我们要留意圣经如何形容神当时的心情：后悔造人，心中忧伤。这里的“后悔”不是指神做错了事而后悔，而是指因为人变了，不断堕落，甚至到了终日所思想的尽都是恶的地步，令神感到既伤痛也忿怒，以致神不得不施行审判。即使如此，神最关注、最想做的是什么？

We should note how the Bible describes God's feelings at that time: He regretted having created man, and was grieved at heart. The " regret " here doesn't refer to God's regret for having done something wrong, but rather to the fact that man had changed, steadily degenerating, to the point where his thoughts were filled with evil. This pained and angered God, forcing Him to execute judgment. Even so, what was God's greatest concern and desire?

 

 

¶¶¶     分组问题    ¶¶¶

¶¶¶      Grouping Problems     ¶¶¶

请学员细阅以下四组经文，找找这四组经文有什么相同之处，经文要突显什么信息。然后，请几位学员，分享一下他们的看法。

Have students carefully review the following four groups of scriptures and identify any similarities between them. They also discuss the messages they highlight. Then, have a few students share their thoughts.

（1）创 6:18~21；

( 1 ) Genesis 6:18-21 ;

（2）创 7:1~3；

( 2 ) Genesis 7:1-3 ;

（3）创 7:6~9；

( 3 ) Genesis 7:6-9 ;

（4）创 7:13~16。

( 4 ) Genesis 7:13-16 .

 

 

创世记 6 章 11 节至 7 章 24 节，短短一章半的经文，合共 36 节，但其中 15 节，重复提到挪亚和他的全家，并一定数量的各种动物都要进入方舟。上面四组经文，意思重复，突显一个很重要的信息：虽然人罪恶很大，必须面对审判刑罚，但神还是定意施行拯救。在这四组经文中，神并没有说其他人不可以进入方舟；若动物都可以进入方舟得救，神又岂会禁止其他人进入方舟得救呢？问题是其他人愿意进入方舟吗？

Genesis 6 : 11–7 : 24 , a short chapter and a half, contains 36 verses, but 15 of them repeatedly mention Noah and his family, along with a certain number of various animals, who were to enter the ark. The repetition of these four verses underscores a crucial message: Although humanity is deeply sinful and must face judgment and punishment, God is determined to save it. In none of these four verses does God forbid others from entering the ark. If animals could be saved, why would God forbid others from entering? The question is, are others willing to enter ?

彼后 2:5 神也没有宽容上古的世代，曾叫洪水临到那不敬虔的世代，却保护了传义道的挪亚一家八口。

2 Peter 2:5 God did not spare the ancient generation and brought a flood to the ungodly generation, but protected Noah and his family of eight who preached righteousness.

从彼得后书的经文可见，挪亚一边建造方舟，一边传道，叫人悔改。他如何传道？他当然可以直接传讲因人的罪神要用洪水毁灭世界，劝他们悔改并进入方舟。另外，相信挪亚建方舟的情况，会吸引很多人从各处来围观，而且挪亚建方舟的事很可能成为所有人的笑柄：“挪亚脑子有没有问题？竟然把那么多的时间、精力花在建造一只方舟上。”而其中一些很可能会问挪亚在做什么，而挪亚也很可能会借这个机会向他们传道。挪亚至少用了几十年建造方舟，也就是说，神其实给当时的人至少几十年的时间，让他们有悔改，进方舟得救的机会。

As the passage in 2 Peter shows, while Noah was building the ark, he was also preaching, calling people to repent. How did he preach? He could, of course, simply preach that God would destroy the world with a flood because of man's sin, urging them to repent and enter the ark. Furthermore, Noah's construction of the ark would likely have attracted crowds of onlookers, and it would likely have become a source of ridicule : " Is Noah crazy? He's wasting so much time and energy on building an ark! " Some of them would likely have questioned Noah's work, and he might have used this opportunity to preach to them. Noah spent at least several decades building the ark, meaning that God gave the people of his time at least several decades to repent and enter the ark for salvation.

最后，当挪亚一家和各类动物，包括飞鸟，有秩序地进入方舟的时候，场面一定非常壮观，这是一个奇迹，一个浩大的拯救行动，告诉其他人，挪亚所说的是真的，大家快点进方舟吧！进入方舟的行动历时七天。在这七天中，四周的人若愿意，相信还是有足够的时间进入方舟的，只是没有人相信洪水会来，也没有人愿意进入方舟。结果，“不知不觉洪水来了，把他们全都冲去。”（太 24:39）

Finally, when Noah and his family, along with all the animals, including the birds, entered the ark in an orderly manner, the scene must have been truly spectacular. It was a miracle, a tremendous act of salvation, a message to the others that Noah's words were true and that everyone should hurry and enter the ark! The entry process took seven days. During those seven days, there would have been ample time for those around to enter the ark if they had wanted to. However, no one believed in the flood, and no one was willing to enter . As a result, “ they knew not until the flood came and swept them all away ” ( Matthew 24:39 ).

洪水之后，神跟挪亚立约：无条件地承诺不再以洪水来毁灭世界，神还以彩虹作为立约的记号（参创 9:11~17)。

After the flood, God made a covenant with Noah: He unconditionally promised not to destroy the world with a flood again. God also used a rainbow as a sign of the covenant ( see Genesis 9:11-17) .

相比之下，关乎洪水浩大的经文只有几节，而指出神愿意施行拯救的经文，却一再重复。可见，神愿意万人得救，不愿意有一人沉沦。

In contrast, there are only a few verses about the magnitude of the flood, but the verses that point out God's willingness to save are repeated again and again. This shows that God wants everyone to be saved and does not want anyone to perish.

弟兄姊妹们，到了将来，今天的事物不会是同一样子，世界也不会无休止地一直运行。耶稣快来了！但世界却漠视这一点。在挪亚的时代，世人漠视洪水的警告，生活如常，一点也没有受到影响，直到挪亚进入方舟的那日。耶稣来临的时候也是如此（太 24:38; 路 17:27）；世人漠视耶稣快将回来，以为他的来临无关紧要。他一天没有真正来到，世人也不知道他要来！

Brothers and sisters, in the future, things will not be the same as they are today, and the world will not continue indefinitely . Jesus is coming soon! Yet the world is oblivious to this fact. In Noah's day, the people ignored the warnings about the flood and continued their lives as usual, unaffected until the day Noah entered the ark. The same will be true when Jesus comes ( Matthew 24:38 ; Luke 17:27 ) . The world will ignore Jesus' imminent return, regarding His coming as insignificant. As long as He does not truly arrive, the world will not know He is coming!

不要因你为神所做的工作而灰心（加 6:9）。也许你已经事奉了很多年，感到疲乏。但请想起挪亚，他辛劳工作几十年而毫无成果，但最后，神成就了他所说的一切！因此，要忠心！你的劳苦在主里面不是徒然的（林前 15:58）。从方舟的建造、洪水期间的保护，及至其后的彩虹，挪亚处处经历神的信实。睁开你的眼睛，在目前的景况中看看他的信实吧！你必会感到惊讶！

Do not be discouraged in your work for God (Gal. 6 : 9 . Perhaps you've served for many years and feel weary. But remember Noah, who toiled for decades with no results, yet in the end, God fulfilled everything he had promised! Therefore, be faithful! Your labor is not in vain in the Lord (1 Corinthians 15 : 5-8 ). From the building of the ark, to the protection during the flood, to the rainbow afterward, Noah experienced God's faithfulness at every turn. Open your eyes and see His faithfulness in your current situation! You will be amazed!

盼望我们继续努力传扬福音，世上多很多人尚未认识主，还没得到这个救恩。弟兄姊妹，我们要努力传福音，因为这是神的心意，也是神给我们的大使命。愿我们都像挪亚那样，既然传福音是神所吩咐的，不管要付出多少代价，我们也要照着神的话而行，努力传福音（参创 6:22，7:5、9）。

I hope we will continue to diligently spread the gospel. There are still many people in the world who have not yet come to know the Lord and received salvation. Brothers and sisters, we must diligently spread the gospel, for this is God's will and the great commission He has given us. May we all be like Noah. Since preaching the gospel is God's command, no matter the cost, we must follow God's word and diligently spread the gospel ( see Genesis 6:22 ; 7:5 , 9 ).

经文：  Scripture:

创 10:5   这些人的后裔将各国的地土、海岛分开居住，各随各的方言、宗族立国。

Creation 10:5    The descendants of these people settled in different lands and islands of different countries, each establishing their own nation according to their own language and clan .

创 10:20   这就是含的后裔，各随他们的宗族、方言，所住的地土、邦国。

Creation 10:20    These are the descendants of Ham, according to their families, according to their languages, according to their lands and nations .

创 10:31   这就是闪的子孙，各随他们的宗族、方言，所住的地土、邦国。

Creation 10:31    These are the children of Shem, according to their families, according to their languages, according to their lands, and according to their nations .

 

问题：  question:

 根据以上三段经文，挪亚三个儿子的后裔是不是住在一起，并且有共同的语言？

   Based on the above three passages, did the descendants of Noah’s three sons live together and have a common language?

 

解释：  explain:

挪亚的三个儿子：雅弗、含和闪，都说一样的语言，但创世记 10 章 5 节、20 节

Noah's three sons: Japheth, Ham, and Shem, all spoke the same language, but Genesis 10 : 5 , 20

和 31 节分别提到雅弗、含和闪的后裔，各在自己的国家居住，使用各自的语言。为

Verses 1 and 31 mention the descendants of Japheth, Ham, and Shem, each living in his own country and using his own language.

什么一个家庭会发展出不同的国家和语言？创世记第 11 章给我们说明了原因。

Why did one family develop into different nations and languages? Genesis 11 explains why.

创 11:1、4   那时，天下人的口音、言语都是一样。4 他们说：“来吧！我们要建造一座城和一座塔，塔顶通天，为要传扬我们的名，免得我们分散在全地上。”

Genesis 11:1 , 4 In those days the whole earth had one speech and one language. 4 They said, " Come, let us build ourselves a city and a tower with its top in the heavens, and let us make a name for ourselves, lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth. "

创 11:7~9   我们下去，在那里变乱他们的口音，使他们的言语彼此不通。”8 于是，耶和华使他们从那里分散在全地上；他们就停工不造那城了。9 因为耶和华在那里变乱天下人的言语，使众人分散在全地上，所以那城名叫巴别（就是“变乱”的意思）。

Genesis 11:7-9 "Let us go down and there confuse their language, so that they will not be able to understand one another's speech. " 8 So the LORD scattered them abroad from there over all the earth; and they stopped building the city. 9 Therefore its name was called Babel (which means " confusion " ) because there the LORD confused the language of all the earth and scattered them abroad over all the earth .

洪水之后，人蒙神赐福，人口渐渐增多。创世记 11 章 1 节提到，当时，天下的人口音、言语都是一样的。然而，当人去到巴比伦（也就是示拿）附近的一个大平原时，他们想做两件事情：建一座城，以及建一座塔，而且塔顶通天。他们建城的目的是，以这座城为中心，把所有人集中起来，免得人分散在全地上；至于建一座通天的塔的原因是，要传扬他们的名，显示他们有非凡智慧、能力，能够建一座塔通往神那里。

After the Flood, God blessed humanity, and the population gradually increased. Genesis 11 : 1 states that everyone under heaven had a common accent and language. However, when the people arrived at a large plain near Babylon (also known as Shinar), they wanted to do two things: build a city and a tower with its top reaching to the heavens. Their purpose in building the city was to gather everyone together, preventing them from being scattered across the earth. Their purpose in building a tower reaching to the heavens was to spread their name, demonstrating their extraordinary wisdom and ability to build a tower that reached God.

 

这两个想法显示了他们的骄傲：他们要集结力量，造一座通天的塔，跟神平起平坐，这跟当年亚当和夏娃吃分别善恶树果子的根本原因是一样的：想自己作主，不要神作他们的主。

These two ideas revealed their pride: they wanted to gather their strength and build a tower that reached the sky, so that they could be on an equal footing with God. This was the same fundamental reason why Adam and Eve ate the fruit of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil: they wanted to be their own masters and did not want God to be their master.

神回应的方法是：变乱了他们的口音。结果，他们因没法彼此沟通而停工，不能再造那城和那塔。而由于口音变乱了，就形成了列国。什么是骄傲？当人自以为可以当家作主，不用再顺服神的安排和旨意，这就是骄傲和叛逆，这也是罪的本质。在我们生命里，特别是教会事奉，我们一定要提防自己变得骄傲，以及跟骄傲有密切关系的试探——扬名。什么时候，我们要传扬自己的名，什么时候大家要争名，什么时候教会就会变乱，大家就没法沟通，最终就会各自为政，如同分裂为列国一样。只有神的名才配得我们称扬。弟兄姊妹，求神帮助，让我们努力建造教会，而不是建造自己的名声、位置。

吾珥位于迦勒底，而迦勒底就是巴比伦。吾珥是当时的商业中心和重要港口，考古发现城内有两层的民房、商业楼房、宽阔的街道，以及贯穿全城的运河和水沟。这座城还拥有非常健全的学校体制，因此当地人精通数学、天文、建筑。可惜，它是一个拜偶像（月神）的中心。亚伯兰就是在这种拜偶像的环境中居住和成长，所以神必须把亚伯兰一家从吾珥领出来，借他建立一个新的民族。正如约书亚记 24 章2 至 3 节所说：“古时你们的列祖，就是亚伯拉罕和拿鹤的父亲他拉，住在大河那边事奉别神，我将你们的祖宗亚伯拉罕，从大河那边带来，领他走遍迦南全地，又使他的子孙众多……”

Only by leaving the land of idolatry could Abram establish a deeper relationship with God. As we will later learn from the history of Israel's fall, the consequences of not abandoning idols are grave and painful, and their impact can be far-reaching.

Genesis from chapter 12 The chapter begins by focusing on how God unfolds His work of redemption through Abram and his descendants. This passage is the covenant between God and Abram.
