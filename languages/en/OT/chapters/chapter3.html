第 24 步  因信而行

Step 24​   Act by faith

动作：右手包左拳，再往西北跨步  

Action: Wrap your left fist with your right hand, then step northwest.

左手握拳，用右手包住它，说：“因信”。接着伸出左脚夸张地往前跨一步，然后右脚再跨一步，同时说：“而行”。我们做这个动作的时候，要从吾珥这个地方，往西北的方向跨步。(朝西北的方向走，并且要顺着肥沃月弯的地形走。)

Make a fist with your left hand, cover it with your right hand, and say, " By faith . " Then, extend your left foot and dramatically step forward, then take another step with your right foot , saying, " And walk . " As we do this, we should step northwest from Ur ( walking northwest, following the terrain of the Fertile Crescent ) .

经文：  Scripture:

创 12:4~5 亚伯兰就照着耶和华的吩咐去了；罗得也和他同去。亚伯兰出哈兰的时候年七十五岁。亚伯兰将他妻子撒莱和侄儿罗得，连他们在哈兰所积蓄的财物、所得的人口，都带往迦南地去。

Genesis 12:4-5 Abram went as the Lord commanded, and Lot went with him. Abram was seventy-five years old when he left Haran. He took his wife Sarai, his nephew Lot, and all the possessions they had accumulated and the people they had acquired in Haran, and they set out for the land of Canaan.

来 11:8 亚伯拉罕因着信，蒙召的时候，就遵命出去，往将来要得为业的地方去；出去的时候，还不知往哪里去。

Hebrews 11:8 By faith Abraham obeyed when he was called to go out to a place which he would after receive as an inheritance. And he went out, not knowing where he was going.

问题：  question:

 根据创世记 12 章 4 至 5 节，亚伯兰对神的吩咐有什么回应？

   According to Genesis 12 : 4-5 , how did Abram respond to God's command?

 根据希伯来书 11 章 8 节，当神叫亚伯兰出去时，他怎样做？他是否知道要往哪里去？

 According to Hebrews 11:8, what did Abram do when God told him to go out? Did he know where he was going?  

 

解释：  explain:

一、因信而行，面对不明朗和陌生的未来  
1. Acting by faith in the face of an uncertain and unfamiliar future

神吩咐亚伯兰离开本地、本族和父家，往他要亚伯兰去的地方；简单来说，神要他移民。神吩咐亚伯兰之前，并没有把所有实际情况告诉亚伯兰，亚伯兰也没有要求必须先让他知道所有实际情况才起行。亚伯兰不知道那个地方是怎样的，不认识那里任何一个人，也没有人在他出发前承诺会照应、接待他。面对不明朗和陌生的未来，为什么他还要去？因为他是因信而行。

God commanded Abram to leave his country, his people, and his father's household and go to the land He wanted him to go to; in short, God told him to migrate. God did not tell Abram all the details before giving this command, nor did Abram require that he be fully informed before setting out. Abram did not know what the land was like, did not know anyone there, and no one had promised to care for him or receive him before he set out. Faced with an uncertain and unfamiliar future, why did he continue to go? Because he acted on faith.

二、因信而行，面对旅途的艰辛和危险  
2. Facing the hardships and dangers of the journey by faith

请想象，在古代没有汽车，也没有空调的情况下，亚伯兰的旅程会是怎样的？很辛苦？还是很舒服？

Imagine Abram’s journey in those days, when there were no cars or air conditioning. Was it arduous? Or was it comfortable?

他的旅程十分辛苦，不仅居无定所，雨淋日晒，还要面对强盗的危险和很多陌生的环境，又没有朋友，难免有时会感到孤单。最辛苦的是旅程十分漫长，不是一两个星期就可以走完。面对长途旅程的艰辛和危险，为什么他还要去？因为他是因信而行。

His journey was arduous. Not only was he homeless, exposed to rain and sun, but he also faced the danger of bandits and unfamiliar surroundings. Without friends, he inevitably felt lonely at times. Worst of all, the journey was long; it wouldn't take just a week or two. Despite the hardships and dangers of such a long journey, why did he continue? Because he acted on faith.

三、因信而行：面对没有证据的呼召  
3. Acting by Faith: Facing a Call Without Evidence

在出发前，他要面对許多很大的难处——不光离开自己的家、离开自己的家族的人，放下在吾珥所有的工作、地位等等；他最大的难处很有可能是：如果有人问他：他怎么知道这个决定是对的？他有没有搞错？他根本拿不出任何理据来。因为神只给他应许，没有给他任何理据。面对没有理据的呼召，为什么他还要去？因为他是因信而行。他相信神，而这个因信而行的行动亦成了新约论到信心生活时所引用的榜样（参来 11:8~12; 罗 4:18~21）。

Before setting out, he faced many great difficulties — not only leaving his home and family, but also giving up his job and position in Ur. His greatest difficulty was probably this: If someone asked him: How did he know this decision was right? Was he mistaken? He would have no reason at all. Because God only gave him a promise, not any reason. Faced with a call without reason, why did he still go? Because he acted by faith. He believed in God, and this act of faith became the model cited in the New Testament when discussing the life of faith ( see Hebrews 11:8-12; Romans 4 : 18-21 .​​

信心建基于两件事：神是信实的，并且他的应许都是真的；信心必定会带来相应的行动。希伯来书第 11 章列举一众信心英雄时，常常这样描述他们：……因着信……就……。有信心，就必定会有相应的行动。亚伯兰因信就前行。你因着信，曾经采取过什么行动？

Faith is based on two things: God is faithful and his promises are true; and faith inevitably leads to corresponding action. Hebrews 11 , when listing heroes of faith, often describes them this way: … by faith …… Faith inevitably leads to corresponding action. Abram moved forward by faith. What actions have you taken based on faith ?

今天，你害怕神的带领和引导吗？你是否想在顺服之前，先从神那里得到多一些理据呢？很多时候，当神带领我们迈出第一步时，我们仍然不知道跟着要怎样走。然而，我们要因信而行，信赖神的带领，踏出信心的第一步；对于以后未知的每一步，我们同样要信靠神的带领！只要你跟从他，他必定会带领你（参出 15:13；诗 48:14）。

Are you afraid of God's guidance and direction today? Do you want more reassurance from God before you obey ? Often, when God leads us to take the first step, we still don't know how to follow. However, we must act by faith, trusting God's guidance in taking that first step of faith. And for every subsequent, unpredictable step , we must trust in God's guidance as well! As long as you follow Him, He will surely lead you (see Exodus 15:13 ; Psalm 48:14 ).

你当初蒙召，遵照神的吩咐，走上事奉的路时，你有没有想过：你因着什么走下去？当你想放弃的时候，你认为你应该怎样做？

When you were first called and followed God's instructions to embark on the path of ministry, did you ever think about why you kept going? What do you think you should do when you want to give up?

一、为神筑坛  1. Building an Altar for God

经文：  Scripture:

创 12:5~8 亚伯兰将他妻子撒莱和侄儿罗得，连他们在哈兰所积蓄的财物、所得的人口，都带往迦南地去。他们就到了迦南地。6 亚伯兰经过那地，到了示剑地方、摩利橡树那里。那时迦南人住在那地。7 耶和华向亚伯兰显现，说：我要把这地赐给你的后裔。亚伯兰就在那里为向他显现的耶和华筑了一座坛。8 从那里他又迁到伯特利

Genesis 12:5-8 Abram took his wife Sarai and his nephew Lot, along with all the possessions they had accumulated and the people they had acquired in Haran, and they set out for the land of Canaan. They arrived in the land of Canaan. 6 Abram traveled through the land to Shechem, to the oak of Moreh. At that time, the Canaanites were living in the land. 7 The Lord appeared to Abram and said, "I will give this land to your descendants." There, Abram built an altar to the Lord, who had appeared to him. 8 From there, he moved to Bethel.

东边的山，支搭帐棚；西边是伯特利，东边是艾。他在那里又为耶和华筑了一座坛，求告耶和华的名

He pitched his tent on the hill to the east, with Bethel to the west and Ai to the east. He also built an altar there to the Lord and called on the name of the Lord.

创 13:18   亚伯兰就搬了帐棚，来到希伯仑、幔利的橡树那里居住，在那里为耶和华筑了一座坛。

Genesis 13:18 So Abram moved his tent and came and dwelt by the oaks of Mamre, which are in Hebron, and built an altar there to the LORD.

问题：  question:

 这两段经文提到亚伯兰为神筑什么？  你认为他为什么要这样做？

   What did Abram build for God as mentioned in these two passages?    Why do you think he did that? 

解释：  explain: 

他们从吾珥走到哈兰，那是另一座敬拜月亮神的城。圣经说他们住在哈兰，直到亚伯兰的父亲他拉死了（参徒 7:4），然后才进入迦南。迦南是以色列地的另一个名称；在改称以色列之前，那地叫迦南。

They traveled from Ur to Haran, another city that worshipped a moon god. The Bible says they stayed in Haran until the death of Abram's father Terah (see Acts 7 : 4 ), and then they entered Canaan. Canaan is another name for the land of Israel ; before it was renamed Israel, it was called Canaan.

使命开始时可能很令人沮丧。亚伯兰在神应许给他的土地上，是个异乡人。他更发现那里已经有很多强盛的部族聚居，他们早已占据着这个地方的平原，他和他的家人只能住在山上。现在亚伯兰因神的呼召来到迦南，却没有力量从那些占据者手上把地夺过来；然而，他没有因此灰心或埋怨神，反而为神筑坛，在那里敬拜那位呼召他到这里来的神，表明他还是信靠神（参创 12:6~8）。

The beginning of a mission can be frustrating. Abram was a stranger in the land God had promised him. He discovered that many powerful tribes already lived there, occupying the plains. He and his family were forced to live in the mountains. Now, Abram had arrived in Canaan at God's call, but he lacked the power to seize the land from those who occupied it. Yet, rather than becoming discouraged or blaming God, he built an altar and worshipped the God who had called him there, demonstrating his continued trust in Him ( see Genesis 12 : 6-8 ) .

亚伯兰有一个特点──为神筑坛。他一直住在帐棚里，帐棚不太坚固，并且是临时的建筑物。可是，他每到一个新的地方，似乎都会为神筑一座坛。坛是用石头建造，相对于帐棚来说，坛是坚固和长久的建筑物。由此可见，亚伯兰非常认真看待他跟神的关系。

Abram had one characteristic : he built altars for God. He had always lived in tents, which were not very sturdy and were temporary structures. Yet, whenever he arrived in a new place, he seemed to build an altar for God. Altars were made of stone, and compared to tents, they were sturdy and permanent structures. This shows that Abram took his relationship with God very seriously.

你怎样看待你跟神的关系？每次开始一个新的服事，你一定会做的是,认真为这个服事祷告，让神可以通过你赐福你所服事的人。每一天，就是新的一天，起来的时候，我们要先祷告，把我们当天要作的事情交托神。

How do you view your relationship with God? Every time you begin a new ministry, you must pray earnestly for it , allowing God to bless those you serve through you. Every day is a new day. When we wake up, we should first pray and entrust our work to God.

 

二、被神称义  2. Justified by God

Look at the sky and count the stars, if you are able to number them. ” And he said to him, “ So will your descendants be. ” 6 Abram believed the LORD, and he counted it to him for righteousness.

亚伯兰在迦南住了一段日子。一个晚上，神告诉亚伯兰他必大大赐福他。可是，亚伯兰表示他没有儿子，神赐福给他有啥用。神就请亚伯兰看一看天上的繁星，并请他数一数它们的数目。亚伯兰当然数不过来。于是神向他表示，他一定会有自己的后裔，而且他后裔的数目将要如天上的星那样众多，那样数不过来。

Abram lived in Canaan for some time. One night, God told Abram that He would greatly bless him. However, Abram said that since he had no children, God's blessing was useless. God then asked Abram to look at the stars in the sky and count them. Abram couldn't count them. Then God promised Abram that he would have descendants, and that their number would be as numerous as the stars in the sky, so numerous that they could not be counted.

假若你是亚伯兰，你和你的太太年纪已经很大了，过了可以生育的岁数(参罗 4:19),但神一直跟你说你会有后裔，可是你一直都没有孩子。现在神再次跟你说，你的后裔将要如天上的星那样众多，你会有什么反应？

If you were Abram, you and your wife were already very old, past childbearing age ( see Romans 4:19 ), yet God had repeatedly told you that you would have offspring, yet you remained childless. Now God tells you again that your descendants will be as numerous as the stars in the sky. How would you react?

虽然亚伯兰到这刻仍然没有孩子，但听见神这番话，他没有以为神在跟他开玩笑。他相信神。这是圣经第一次出现“信”这个字，这是一个很重要和宝贵的字。亚伯兰不再注视自己的年纪有多大，而是全心全意地信任神，因而得到神对他完全的悦纳(被称为义)，可以跟神建立很深的关系，甚至成为神的朋友(参雅 2:23)！纵使从环境来看，神的应许似乎不可能实现，可是他仍是信任神。

Although Abram still had no children at this point, upon hearing God's words, he didn't think God was joking. He trusted God. This is the first time the word " faith " appears in the Bible , a very important and precious word. Abram no longer focused on his age, but placed his wholehearted trust in God. As a result, he received God's complete acceptance ( being declared righteous ) , was able to establish a deep relationship with God, and even became God's friend ( see James 2:23 ) ! Even though the circumstances made God's promises seem impossible to fulfill, he still trusted in God.

称义的意思是：一个人满足了法律的要求，就被称为义人。就神跟人的关系来说，神要求的是，我们完全信任他。而亚伯兰的信心达到了神的要求，就被神称为义。因为人非有信，就不能得神的喜悦。

Justification means that a person is declared righteous when they meet the legal requirements. In the relationship between God and man, God requires complete trust in Him. Abram's faith met God's requirements, and he was declared righteous. Without faith, it is impossible to please God.

亚伯兰的信心到底是怎样的一种信心？亚伯兰的信心是一种不靠自己，不靠别人，单单倚靠神的信心。亚伯兰深知不能靠自己或妻子，因为他们都过了生育的年龄。与此同时，他所要作的不是改变自己的身体状况，或吃什么特别的食物，让自己可以有孩子，而是全然信靠神。

What kind of faith did Abram have? It was a faith that relied not on himself or others but solely on God. Abram knew he couldn't rely on himself or his wife, as they were both past childbearing age. At the same time, he didn't need to change his physical condition or eat any special foods to conceive, but rather rely completely on God.

我们在基督里被称为义，也像亚伯兰一样，不是靠自己，也不是因为我们作了什么好人好事，一切都是因为神的恩典；只有靠着主，我们才能得救。

We are justified in Christ, just like Abram, not by ourselves, nor because we have done any good deeds. It is all due to God's grace. Only by relying on the Lord can we be saved.

 

 

第 26 步  以外的以实玛利

Step 26​   Ishmael

( See Genesis 36 : 9 . The Book of Obadiah is a prophecy about the Edomites. Later, God’s judgment came upon the Edomites ( See Ezekiel 25 : 12-13 . Today , the Edomite race no longer exists.

为什么以扫轻视长子的名分，只为一顿食物就把它出卖了？这件事情很严重。为什么？希伯来书的作者形容以扫“贪恋世俗”（ 参来 12:16）。世俗是什么意思？它是圣洁的相反。圣洁就是分别出来，归神使用。贪恋世俗的意思就是，不敬虔、不圣洁。他的祖父亚伯拉罕、父亲以撒必定曾向孩子们讲述神与他们相遇的经过、神与他们所立的约，以及神所赐的应许；但以扫却漠不关心。以扫是一个不敬虔的人，他根本不重视自己跟神的关系，因而轻视神，也就轻看长子的名分。

Why did Esau despise his birthright and sell it for a single meal? This was a serious matter. Why ? The author of Hebrews describes Esau as " worldly " ( see Hebrews 12:16 ) . What does worldliness mean? It is the opposite of holiness. Holiness means being set apart for God's use. Worldliness means being ungodly and unholy. His grandfather Abraham and father Isaac must have told their children about God's encounter with them, His covenant with them, and the promises He had given them; but Esau paid no attention. Esau was an ungodly man who had no regard for his relationship with God. As a result, he despised God and, by extension, his birthright.

此外，长子的名分关系到神对后裔的应许，这方面的应许是神与亚伯拉罕所立的约的主要条文。因为以扫轻看这个约的价值，所以也视长子名分为毫无价值。

Furthermore, the birthright was related to God's promise of descendants, a key provision of the covenant between God and Abraham. Because Esau despised the value of this covenant, he also considered the birthright worthless.

我们可以从圣经所记有关以扫的反面例子中学得教训。以扫好比那些轻看与主的关系、不懂珍惜这个关系的人。神的约极宝贵，而今天我们所得的应许，比亚伯拉罕、以撒或雅各所得的更大（来 8:6）。我们岂不应加倍珍惜在神面前的身份吗！

We can learn from the negative example of Esau in the Bible. Esau exemplifies those who neglect and fail to cherish their relationship with the Lord. God's covenant is extremely precious, and the promises we receive today are greater than those of Abraham, Isaac, or Jacob (Hebrews 8:6). Shouldn't we cherish our standing before God even more?

 

些在基督里的身份说明一件事：我们的神给我们的恩典是丰丰富富的，并且极其荣耀。因此，不要为了即时、眼前的满足，而放弃了将来那更大的福分。神为爱他的人所预备的，是眼睛未曾看见、耳朵未曾听见、人心也未曾想到的(林前2:9)。不要忘记神给你尊贵的身份，以及神在基督里给你的应许。

Our identity in Christ demonstrates one thing: the grace of our God is abundant and glorious. Therefore, don't forgo the greater blessings of the future for the sake of immediate, present gratification. Eyes have not seen, ear has not heard, and no human mind has conceived what God has prepared for those who love him ( 1 Corinthians 2:9) . Never forget the honorable identity God has given you and the promises he has made to you in Christ.

你是否珍惜你与神的关系？你是否高度重视你在基督里的身份？还是你觉得这些都微不足道？你靠基督与神建立的关系，绝对是你所拥有的最无价、最宝贵的东西，没有其他东西的价值可以相比。你要开始珍惜这个关系！你要开始重视那些应许（参太 13:45~46）！它们是又宝贵又极大的应许（参彼后 1:4）！它们是我们的生命（参诗 119:50），我们既然得了这些应许，就该过纯全圣洁的生活（参林后 7:1）。

Do you treasure your relationship with God? Do you hold your identity in Christ in high regard? Or do you consider it insignificant? Your relationship with God through Christ is the most priceless and precious thing you possess. Nothing else can compare in value. Start cherishing it! Start valuing the promises ( see Matthew 13 : 45-46 ) ! They are precious and exceeding great promises ( see 2 Peter 1 : 4 ) ! They are our life ( see Psalm 119 : 50 ) , and since we have received these promises, we should live pure and holy lives ( see 2 Corinthians 7 : 1 ) .

解释：  explain:

雅各从母腹出生时抓住哥哥的脚跟，这个动作象征他会争取和抓紧他认为属于自己的东西。雅各不像哥哥以扫；他皮肤非常光滑，喜欢住在帐棚里，帮助母亲煮食。雅各是个牧羊人。

When Jacob was born, he grasped his brother's heel. This gesture symbolized his willingness to strive for and hold on to what he considered his own. Unlike his brother Esau, Jacob had very smooth skin, enjoyed living in a tent, and helped his mother cook. Jacob was a shepherd.

神把那约的应许给予雅各，雅各也很珍惜。雅各在伯特利睡觉时，神与他坚定了那些应许（参创1:17) .

God gave the covenant promises to Jacob, and Jacob treasured them. God confirmed those promises with Jacob while he was sleeping in Bethel ( see Genesis 1:17) .

28:10~19）。可是雅各却误以为可以用欺诈的手段和诡计来抓紧那些福分。为此，神要教导雅各，让雅各在他的岳父手下，经历了很多艰苦和困难，让他明白神会以自己的方法和大能来成就神对他的应许，而不是以他的方法和能力。不过神始终没有放弃雅各。后来，他离开他的岳父，要回到迦南，打算请他的哥哥以扫原谅他。

28 : 10-19 . However, Jacob mistakenly believed he could grasp those blessings through deception and cunning. Therefore, God wanted to teach Jacob, allowing him to experience many hardships and difficulties under his father- in - law , so that he would understand that God would fulfill His promises to him through His own methods and power, not through his own methods and abilities. However, God never gave up on Jacob. Later, he left his father-in-law and returned to Canaan, intending to ask his brother Esau for forgiveness.

在快要过河与哥哥重聚的那个晚上，他有一个很特别的经历。有一个人来跟他摔跤。他后来发现，那个不是普通人，而是神。一直以来，他要抓住的是神的福气，而不是神。直到他与永生神角力，才开始明白人生最重要的不是抓住神要赐给他的福气，而是要抓住那位赐福给他的神，全心倚靠神，然后他才得到神所赐的福。

On the night before crossing the river to reunite with his brother, he had a remarkable experience. A man wrestled with him. He later discovered that this man was no ordinary person, but God. Until then, he had been grasping for God's blessings, not God himself. It was only after wrestling with the living God that he began to understand that the most important thing in life was not grasping the blessings God offered, but grasping the God who blessed him. Only by relying wholeheartedly on God could he receive the blessings God bestowed.

直到他与永生神角力，他才开始明白神的方法。神以人的形像与他摔跤直到黎明（参创 32:22~32）；当他跟神角力完毕离开时，虽然扭了大腿窝，心灵却治好了。他的名字改为以色列 ──与神较力得胜（参创 32:28），但他的能力是从信靠神而来的，并非从他自己而来的。

It was not until he wrestled with the living God that he began to understand God's ways. God, in human form, wrestled with him until dawn ( see Genesis 32 : 22-32 ). When he left after wrestling with God, his hip was dislocated, but his spirit was healed. His name was changed to Israel — the victorious wrestler with God ( see Genesis 32 :22-32). 3 2 : 2 8 , but his power came from trust in God, not from himself.

圣经常提到神是亚伯拉罕、以撒、雅各的神，而他们三人的性格、生命的遭遇都不一样。但相同的是，神是他们的神（参太 22:32），他们都在人生历程中经历到神。活人跟死人最大的分别，就是死人的生命都是一样的──不可能再有什么改变，可是活人就不同，生命还可以改变，因此都不是一样的。

The Bible often mentions God as the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. These three men had different personalities and life experiences . But what they all shared was that God was their God (see Matthew 22:32 ) . They all experienced God in their lives . The greatest difference between the living and the dead is that the lives of the dead are the same — they cannot change. However, this is different for the living; their lives can change, and therefore they are all different.

掌管我们人生的是神，不是什么命运，因此，不管你现在面对的困难有多大，神是你的神，你的生命是可以改变过来的。谈到改变，我们有时很羡慕能有某某人的职业、才干、恩赐、背景；假若我的工作像某某那样入息又高又稳定就好了，假若我的儿女像某某的儿女就好了，假若我像某某这样那样就好了。当我们有这样想的时候，我们就如同雅各，只想得到神给某某的福气，而不是那位赐福了某某工作、家庭、事奉的神。神是某某的神，也是你的神，你抓住他，你必定会看见神给你的福气。神欢喜施恩与某某，神也欢喜施恩与你。神是活人的神，不是“复制人”的神，我们各人的生命故事没有翻版。然而，他是活神，并且要赐福我们，我们要抓住他。

It is God, not fate, that controls our lives. Therefore, no matter how great the difficulties you face now, God is your God, and your life can be changed. When it comes to change, we sometimes envy someone's career, talents, gifts, or background. We wonder if only I had someone's high-paying, stable job, if only my children were like someone's children, if only I were like someone else. When we harbor such thoughts , we become like Jacob, seeking only the blessings God bestows upon someone, not the God who has blessed someone's job, family, and ministry. God is someone's God, and he is also your God. If you hold on to him, you will surely see his blessings. God delights in showing grace to someone, and he also delights in showing grace to you. God is a living God, not a " clone God. " Our life stories are not identical. However, he is a living God who wants to bless us. We must hold on to him.

神赐福给我们，有一个目的，就像他赐福给亚伯拉罕、以撒和雅各一样，要我们成为别人的祝福。我们要去，把神的福气带给更多的人。

God blesses us for a purpose, just as He blessed Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He wants us to be a blessing to others. We should go out and bring God's blessings to more people.
