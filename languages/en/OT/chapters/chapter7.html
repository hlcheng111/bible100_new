解释：  explain:

在死海北面，摩西三次（也可能是五次）向新一代的以色列人讲道。他提醒百姓过去曾发生的事情，然后再次把神的律法告诉他们。摩西的讲道是打气的话，为要鼓励新一代的领袖和百姓尽心尽性跟从神、敬畏神和爱神（参申 10:12~22）。申命记(“申命”的意思就是向新一代重申神的命令。)记载了全部的讲章。第一篇是第 1

North of the Dead Sea, Moses preached three times (or possibly five times) to the new generation of Israelites. He reminded the people of past events and then reiterated God's law to them. Moses' sermons were words of encouragement, meant to encourage the new generation of leaders and people to follow God with all their heart, soul, and spirit, fear Him, and love Him (see Deuteronomy 10 : 12-22 ) . The book of Deuteronomy ( " Deuteronomy " means to reiterate God's commands to the new generation ) records all of these sermons. The first is Chapter 1 .

至 4 章；第二篇是第 5 至 26 章；第三篇是第 27 至 30 章。

to 4 Chapter 5 to 26 Chapter 27 to 30 chapter.

摩西不仅指出上一代的以色列人因为不信而未能进入迦南地，还为新一代打气，语重心长地鼓励、提醒他们，而其中三个重点也是我们今天要彼此提醒的：

Moses not only pointed out that the previous generation of Israelites failed to enter the land of Canaan because of their unbelief, but he also encouraged the new generation with earnest words of encouragement and reminder. Three of these key points are also what we should remind each other today:

一、独一的主  1. The One Lord

摩西提醒以色列人，神是全地独一的主，也是他们独一的主。自有永有的神是创天造地的主，也是持续管理天地的主；除他以外，别无他神。因此，对以色列人来说，要敬拜和信靠神，就只有一个选择，就是事奉这位独一的真神。人若以别的代替神，他们的愁苦必然加增；离开神这位独一的主，就是苦事和恶事。

Moses reminded the Israelites that God is the sole Lord of the earth and their only Lord. The eternal God is the Creator of heaven and earth and the One who continually governs them; there is no other. Therefore, for the Israelites, worship and trust in God left them with only one choice: to serve this one true God. If people replaced God with others, their sorrow would inevitably increase; departing from God, the only Lord, would be bitterness and evil.

二、全然的爱  2. Total Love

申命记虽然向新一代的以色列人重申神的律例，然而，整卷书重复强调：以色列人要因为爱神而遵守神的命令。正如我们先前所提到的，神要跟人建立爱的关系，因此，顺服的大前提是我们相信神爱我们，因此，他赐给我们遵守的命令必定是要叫我们得到最大的益处。那么，以色列人要怎样回应神的爱呢？他们要尽心、尽性、尽意爱神，意思就是，他们要毫无保留地爱神。跟从神的路诚然是一条顺服的路，然而，我们不是背负着一大堆规条来跟从他，而是因着爱全然奉上一切，喜乐地跟从他。

While Deuteronomy reiterates God's laws to a new generation of Israelites, the book repeatedly emphasizes that Israelites were to obey God's commands out of love for Him. As we mentioned earlier, God desires a loving relationship with humanity. Therefore, obedience is premised on our belief in God's love for us. Therefore, the commands He gives us to obey must be for our greatest benefit. So, how were the Israelites to respond to God's love? They were to love Him with all their heart, soul, and mind—meaning, they were to love Him without reservation. Following God is undoubtedly a path of obedience, but we do not follow Him burdened by a multitude of rules. Instead, we follow Him joyfully, giving our all out of love .

三、谨守一切  3. Keep everything in mind

跟以上两个重点有密切关系的是，摩西多番提醒他们要他谨守顺从神的一切命令。摩西强调要谨守神一切的命令；不是百分之九十，而是百分之一百。若人真的承认神是独一的主，并且愿意尽心、尽性、尽意爱神，那么，人就该谨守神一切的吩咐，而不是让自己作主：自行决定哪些可以遵守，哪些不用遵守；在遵守神的命令上，绝不跟自己或任何人妥协。在这方面，但以理的三个朋友为我们树立了很美好的榜样：即使神不施行拯救，要牺牲性命，也绝不妥协。

Closely related to the above two key points is Moses' repeated reminders to them to carefully observe and obey all of God's commandments. Moses emphasized the need to observe all of God's commandments; not 90 percent, but 100 percent. If one truly acknowledges God as the only Lord and is willing to love Him with all their heart, soul, and mind, then one should carefully observe all of God's commandments, not allowing oneself to decide what to obey and what not to. In obeying God's commandments, one must never compromise with oneself or anyone else. In this regard, Daniel's three friends set a wonderful example for us: even if God did not deliver them and it cost them their lives, they would not compromise.

现在就让我们每一个人在神面前安静下来反思我们最近的生活：神在我们的生活中是独一的主吗？还是我们在神以外，在好些事上如金钱，有别的主？我们跟神的关系怎样？我们在每一件事上，不管是大事还是小事，是全然爱他吗？在遵守他的命令方面，我们有没有跟自己或别人妥协，因而没有谨守遵行某些命令？

Now let each of us take a moment to reflect on our recent lives before God: Is God the only Lord in our lives? Or do we have other Lords besides Him, such as money, in matters like ours? What is our relationship with God like? Do we love Him completely in everything, big and small? Have we compromised with ourselves or others in obeying His commandments, failing to keep or obey certain of them?

摩西的讲道是打气的话，为要鼓励新一代的领袖和信徒尽心尽性跟从神、敬畏神和爱神。

Moses' sermons were words of encouragement to inspire a new generation of leaders and believers to follow, fear, and love God with all their heart and soul.

你是否在鼓励新一代的领袖和信徒全心全意地爱神、敬畏神和顺服神呢？要以摩西为榜样，花时间去训练、鼓励和装备下一代！现在就停下来，问问自己：“下一代之中，是否有哪一位我应该去鼓励他，并将棒交给他？”

Are you encouraging a new generation of leaders and believers to love, fear, and obey God with all their heart? Follow Moses' example and take the time to train, encourage, and equip the next generation! Stop now and ask yourself , " Is there someone in the next generation I should be encouraging and handing the baton to? "

 神把什么指给摩西看？  之后发生了什么事？

解释：  explain:

神终于把应许地指给摩西看。摩西在尼波山上，虽能远远地观看，却不得进去。为什么？因为这位以色列的领袖没有尊神为圣。这件事情记载在民数记 20 章 1 至 13 节。神对这件事情这样说：“因为你们在寻的旷野，加低斯的米利巴水，在以色列人中没有尊我为圣，得罪了我。”（申 32:51；另参民 20:12, 27:14）

God finally showed Moses the Promised Land. From Mount Nebo, Moses could view it from a distance, but he could not enter. Why? Because this Israelite leader had failed to sanctify God . This incident is recorded in Numbers 20: 1-13 . God explained this incident this way : “ Because you sinned against me at the waters of Meribah at Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin, by not sanctifying me among the Israelites ” ( Deuteronomy 32:51 ; see also Numbers 20:12 , 27:14 ) .

为什么神说摩西没有尊他为圣？神指示摩西吩咐石头发出水来，并没有叫他斥责百姓或用杖击打盘石。可是，摩西却斥责百姓，并且表示：是他使水从盘石流出来。经文更提到他举手撃

Why did God say that Moses did not sanctify Him? God instructed Moses to command the stone to give forth water, but He did not tell him to rebuke the people or to strike the rock with his rod. However, Moses rebuked the people and said that it was He who caused the water to flow out of the rock. The scripture also mentions that He raised His hand and struck the rock.

打盘石两下，可见，他不是轻轻敲打，而是用力地打了两下，给人的感觉是他用自己的能力使水发出来。

He struck the rock twice, which shows that he did not strike it lightly, but struck it with great force, giving the impression that he was using his own power to make the water come out.

The task God gave Joshua was a formidable one. The Canaanite army was a seasoned warrior, equipped with chariots and the latest technological advances of the time, and their fortresses were impregnable ( compare Numbers 13:28 , 32ff .). Furthermore, they were accustomed to forming alliances to jointly resist foreign enemies, and Joshua anticipated the Canaanites would do the same against the Israelites.
