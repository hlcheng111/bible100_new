Our God is a consuming fire! But He is also our friend! Remember, God's power will soon be revealed in an even more terrifying form. One day He will not only shake the earth (as He did at Sinai) , He will also shake the heavens!

信徒们，要清楚知道，他爱你！但你不可把他的怜悯和恩典视为理所当然！与神所立的约和跟他建立的关系，是你拥有的东西中最重要的，也是宇宙中最宝贵的！不要轻忽它，要像

The Bible says, “ Let us worship God acceptably with thanksgiving and reverence and awe. ” ( Hebrews 12:28 ) Worship that is acceptable to God must have these key elements: thanksgiving (and the joy , reverence, and awe that come with it .) You can have beautiful music, good instruments, excellent leaders, great emotions, and all the good things that help you worship. But if there is no thanksgiving, reverence, and awe, it is not worship that is acceptable to God! Does your church have worship that is acceptable to God?

 神在颁布律法时，怎样介绍他跟色列人的关系？

   When God gave the law, how did he describe his relationship with the Israelites?

每个国家的人民都要遵守自己国家的法律。既然神是以色列人的王，以色列人就是他的子民，那么，以色列人行事为人，就必须遵照神给他们的律法。十诫就是神所颂布的律法的十条总纲。有一点值得留意，在颁布十诫前，神先指出他们该遵守十诫的原因：神是他们的神，曾把他们从埃及为奴之家领出来。既然神爱他们，在他们面前施展大能拯救他们，他们理当心悦诚服地按神的吩吋去生活。

The people of every nation must obey the laws of their own land. Since God was the King of the Israelites and the Israelites were His people, the Israelites were bound to act according to the laws He had given them. The Ten Commandments were the ten general provisions of God's law. It is noteworthy that before promulgating the Ten Commandments, God first explained the reason for their obedience: He was their God, having brought them out of slavery in Egypt. Since He loved them and had demonstrated His mighty power to save them, they should wholeheartedly live according to His instructions.

 

 

神借摩西将律法颁给以色列人。律法以神赐给摩西的约为基础，摩西的约则以神赐给亚伯拉罕的约为基础。对顺服的人，这约会带 来祝福，但对叛逆的人却会带来诅咒。

God gave the Law to the Israelites through Moses. The Law was based on the covenant God gave to Moses, which in turn was based on the covenant God gave to Abraham. This covenant brought blessings to those who obeyed but curses to those who disobeyed.

十诫是摩西律法的重心，而整套诫命至少有 613 条，分别记载在出埃及记第 20 至 40 章、整卷利未记（除了第 8 至 10 章某些部分）、民数记第 3 至 6 章、第 15 章、第 18 至 19 章、第 28 至 30 章和第 35 章；涉及生活上很多层面，包括：敬拜、社群、战争、社会、家庭、饮食、公义和审判等。律法内容全备，它的设计是要使以色列人成为特别的民族，让其他国家可以看见以色列民的生活和文化不但独特，而且是被神分别出来的。

The Ten Commandments are the core of the Mosaic Law, and the entire set of commandments consists of at least 613 items, which are recorded in Exodus 20, to 40 Chapter, the entire book of Leviticus ( except chapter 8 to 10 Parts of chapter 3 to 6 Chapter 15 Chapter 18 to 19 Chapter 28 to 30 Chapter and Section 35 The Law covers many aspects of life, including worship, community, warfare, society, family, diet, justice, and judgment. The Law is comprehensive and designed to distinguish the Israelites as a unique people, allowing other nations to see that their life and culture were not only unique but also set apart by God.

 

昔日的以色列人要从迦南人的文化风俗中分别出来，甚至不可以跟他们交往。因为当日迦南人的道德非常败坏。同样，今天我们也要从这个世界败坏的价值观、婚姻观、人生观等等分别出来，不要效法这个世界。以弗所书提醒我们要小心今世的风俗。自古以来，未信的人一切败坏的观念离不开四样的事情：钱、性、名和权。因钱和性而产生的问题多的是，很多人因钱或性跌倒得很厉害，这些事情大家都十分清楚。然而，较少人注意到名和权。人喜欢美名，更喜欢扬名。保罗在腓立比书提到有些人为了想超越保罗，于是趁保罗坐牢时努力传福音，保罗形容这些人的事奉是出于嫉妒分争。人也喜欢争权，个个都想做第一，于是门徒争大，弟兄姊妹也彼此争竞。与名和权有关的问题就是，不肯认错和不肯饶恕。大家要小心，要从这些事情中分别出来。

The Israelites of old had to separate themselves from the Canaanite culture and customs, even forbidding them to associate with them. This was because the Canaanites were extremely morally corrupt. Similarly, today, we must separate ourselves from the world's corrupt values, views on marriage, and life, and avoid imitating them. The book of Ephesians reminds us to be wary of worldly customs. Since ancient times, all corrupt ideas among unbelievers have been tied to four things: money, sex, fame, and power. Money and sex have caused countless problems, and many have fallen seriously because of them. These things are well known to everyone. However, less attention is paid to fame and power. People crave a good reputation, and even more so, fame. In Philippians, Paul mentioned some who, hoping to surpass Paul, diligently preached the gospel while he was imprisoned. Paul described their ministry as motivated by jealousy and strife. People also crave power, each vying for the top spot, leading to strife among disciples and among brothers and sisters. Problems related to fame and power include an unwillingness to admit mistakes and an unforgiveness. We must be careful and separate ourselves from these things.

从个人来说，我们的态度、生活的优先次序，以及在生命中表现出来的属灵果子，都能显出我们与世人不同。从整体来说，我们的独特之处在于我们有合一的心，且彼此相爱。这些都是荣耀神的事情。因此，对我们基督徒来说，无论是个人还是教会，这些事情都是极为重要的。

Individually, our attitudes, priorities, and the spiritual fruit we manifest in our lives distinguish us from the world. Collectively, we are unique in our unity and love for one another. These are things that glorify God. Therefore, they are of paramount importance to us Christians, both individually and as a church.

God not only gave the law but also commanded the Israelites to build a tabernacle for Him, a sanctuary, because He wanted to dwell among them. Why didn't He wait until they entered Canaan to build the tabernacle? Carrying the various components of the tabernacle would have been inconvenient in the wilderness! There was only one reason: God wanted to dwell among humanity, to be with them, and to allow them to associate with Him.

Before this arrangement, the Israelites could easily have the misconception that only certain people designated by God, such as Abraham, Jacob, Joseph, or Moses, could have a relationship with God. However, the tabernacle taught the Israelites that this was not the case. God was willing to live among people and interact with everyone.

会幕由三部分组成：外院、圣所和至圣所。一般来说，外院用来准备和宰杀祭牲；圣所的日常运作包括补充油和香、更换陈设饼和处理祭牲的血；至圣所则是大祭司每年只能进入一次、为全体百姓献赎罪祭的地方。至圣所内有约柜，里面放了亚伦的杖、一罐吗哪和两块法版。

The tabernacle consisted of three parts: the Outer Court, the Holy Place, and the Most Holy Place. The Outer Court was generally used for the preparation and slaughter of animal sacrifices. The Holy Place's daily operations included replenishing oil and incense, replacing the showbread, and handling the blood of sacrifices. The Most Holy Place was where the high priest could enter only once a year to offer the sin offering for the entire people. Within the Most Holy Place was the Ark of the Covenant, which contained Aaron's rod, a jar of manna, and the two tablets of the Testimony.

The tabernacle was the place where God met with humanity. God lived among humanity! Therefore, the people were to regard it as extremely holy. But when Jesus was born, God did not dwell among us in the form of a pillar of fire and smoke, but became a person of flesh and blood, living with us face to face! (See John 1:14 and 1 John 1:1). When the Holy Spirit came at Pentecost, God in Jesus and through the Holy Spirit lived with us in an even more intimate way, that is, in every believer! Ultimately, God will live with humanity personally. For Revelation 21

From Genesis to Revelation, God's will for humanity remains the same: He wants to dwell with us and establish a close relationship with us. Do you understand God's will today? 會幕共有 7 件聖物，形成了“十字”形狀。Có bảy thánh tích thiêng liêng trong nhà tạm tạo thành hình dạng của một konjac với một cây thánh giá nhân sâm

特別是約翰福音有明顯的指涉。

Tin Mừng Gioan nói riêng có những tài liệu tham khảo rõ ràng.

 

1．約翰福音書的顯示
（1）銅祭壇（約 1：29）：        神的羔羊為贖罪祭。銅祭壇（約 12：32－33）：耶穌要在十字架上被舉起來。
（2）洗濯盆（約 2 章）：                    潔淨聖殿。洗濯盆（約 13 章）：耶穌為門徒洗腳，表明聖潔。
（3）陳設餅桌子（約 4－6 章）：有吃有喝，生命糧和活水。
（4）金燈檯（約 8－9 章）：   有兩次說“我（耶穌）是世界的光”（8：12，9：5）；瞎子得見光（約 9：）。
（5）金香爐（約 14－16 章）：          奉耶穌的名禱告，金香爐表明禱告。
（6）約櫃（約 17 章）。
（7）施恩座（約 17 章）：        大祭司代求。至聖所（約 17 章）：內有約櫃和施恩座。

 

1. Trưng bày Tin Mừng Gioan

(1) Bàn thờ bằng đồng (Giăng 1:29): Chiên Con của Đức Chúa Trời là của lễ chuộc tội. Bàn Thờ Đồng (Ga 12:32-33): Chúa Giêsu sẽ bị treo trên thập giá.

(2) Laver (Ga 2): Dọn dẹp đền thờ. Laver (Ga 13): Chúa Giêsu rửa chân cho các môn đệ như một dấu hiệu thánh thiện.

(3) Bàn Bánh Trình Diễn (Giăng 4-6): Có đồ ăn và đồ uống, bánh sự sống và nước sống.

(4) Chân Đèn Vàng (Ga 8-9): Người ta nói hai lần rằng “Ta (Chúa Giêsu) là ánh sáng thế gian” (8:12, 9:5);

(5) Lư hương bằng vàng (Ga 14-16): Hãy cầu nguyện nhân danh Chúa Giêsu. Lư hương bằng vàng biểu thị sự cầu nguyện.

(6) Hòm Giao Ước (Giăng 17).

(7) Ngôi thương xót (Ga 17): Vị thượng tế cầu thay. Nơi Chí Thánh (Giăng 17): Chứa Hòm Giao ước và Nắp thi ân.
