1. 犹大支派不赶出的原因  1. The reason why the tribe of Judah was not driven out

士师记 1 章 19 节告诉我们：他们之所以不能赶出，是因为当地人有铁车。然而，约书亚早已预言，虽然迦南人有铁车，以色列也能把他们赶出去。事实证明，纵然迦南王耶宾有铁车 900 辆，以色列人靠着神的帮助，也能杀败他们。那么，为什么犹大支派不能赶出平原的居民？原因很有可能是他们不想赶出。因为要赶出这些有铁车的迦南人要花很大的力气；他们压根儿不想彻底遵行神的命令，不愿意为此付代价。相信他们会自我安慰说：我们已经赶出那些山地的居民，很不错了。

Judges 1:19 tells us that they were unable to drive out the Canaanites because they had iron chariots. However, Joshua had already predicted that Israel would be able to drive them out despite their iron chariots. As it turned out, even with King Jabin of Canaan possessing 900 iron chariots, the Israelites, with God's help, were able to defeat them. So why couldn't the tribe of Judah drive out the inhabitants of the plains? Most likely, they didn't want to. Expelling these Canaanites with their iron chariots would have required enormous effort; they simply didn't want to fully obey God's command and weren't willing to pay the price. They likely consoled themselves by saying, "We've already driven out the inhabitants of the hill country; that's a good thing."

 

2. 其他支派不赶出的原因  2. The reason why the other tribes were not driven out

 

士 1:28  及至以色列强盛了，就使迦南人做苦工，没有把他们全然赶出。

Judges 1:28   When Israel grew strong, they forced the Canaanites to do hard labor but did not drive them out completely.

其他支派也没有全然赶出所有的迦南人，原因是他们把部分迦南人留下来为他们作苦工；正如士师记第 1 章重复提到的，迦南人住在以色列人中间，成为了服苦的人。以色列人以为生活会因而轻松多了，但事实刚好相反。

The other tribes did not drive out all the Canaanites because they left some of them to do hard labor for them ; as in Judges 1: 1 As mentioned repeatedly in the chapter, the Canaanites lived among the Israelites and became forced laborers. The Israelites thought that this would make their lives easier, but in fact the opposite was true.

总的来说，以色列人没有全然赶出迦南地的外邦人，是因为他们在遵守神的命令上先跟自己妥协：赶出了一些就够了；继而再跟迦南人妥协：只要他们愿意做苦工，就可以留下来。这样的妥协，带来进一步的妥协：容许迦南人留下来，就会容许迦南人继续拜他们的偶像，向他们的偶像献祭；也就没有遵照神的吩咐，拆毁迦南人一切的偶像和祭坛。

In short, the Israelites didn't completely expel the Gentiles from Canaan because they first compromised with themselves in obeying God's command: driving out some was enough. Then they compromised with the Canaanites: as long as they were willing to do hard labor, they could stay. This compromise led to further compromise: allowing the Canaanites to stay meant allowing them to continue worshiping their idols and offering sacrifices to them. Consequently, they failed to obey God's command to tear down all of the Canaanite idols and altars.

Simply put, they were ungrateful and did not remember or believe in Jehovah and his deeds. Sadly, they went on to serve the gods of the Canaanites. One thing worth noting is that the "evil deeds" mentioned in the passage refer to the Israelites' abandonment of God and their service to other gods. Since they had abandoned God, they disregarded His commands and recklessly engaged in all kinds of acts that displeased Him. Following ingratitude comes rebellion.

有没有听过“温水煮蛙”的故事？就是一只青蛙被慢慢煮死的故事。如果把一只青蛙放进沸水中，它会跳出来。但如果把它放进冷水中慢慢加热，它会察觉不到危险而慢慢被煮死。这也是魔鬼经常用来对付我们的方法。在属灵的事上，小小的妥协，可能不会马上对你的灵命、服事带来很大的影响。然而，若你跟罪恶妥协，你的生命就有一个漏洞，如果你还是不悔改，这个洞就会越来越大，最终你的灵命就会破烂不堪。

Have you ever heard the story of the " boiling frog " ? It's about a frog being slowly boiled to death. If you put a frog in boiling water, it will jump out. Small compromises in spiritual matters may not immediately have a significant impact on your spiritual life or ministry. However, if you compromise with sin, a hole is created in your life. If you don't repent, this hole will grow larger and larger, and ultimately your spiritual life will be in tatters.

A certain church had a young senior pastor who managed the church well and was publicly praised by other churches. However, he later began counseling a married woman individually. This wasn't because his church lacked female staff. He knew that as a male staff member, he shouldn't counsel a woman individually. Yet, he didn't. His relationship with the woman grew increasingly close, eventually leading to an extramarital affair and being suspended from the church.

在遵行神的命令上，我们绝对不要跟自己妥协。我们要百分之百坚持遵行神一切的命令，千万不要自行决定要遵守什么和遵守到什么程度。昔日夏娃之所以堕进魔鬼的圈套，就是因为她没有认真看待神的话。妥协的人，也是一样，不会认真看待神的话语。以色列人没有按神的吩咐，全然赶出外邦人，结果使自己和他们的后代陷在罪里，继而落在一个恶性循环当中。

When it comes to obeying God's commands, we must never compromise. We must be 100% committed to obeying all of God's commands, never deciding for ourselves what to obey and to what extent. Eve fell into the devil's trap because she didn't take God's words seriously. Compromising people, likewise, don't take God's words seriously . The Israelites didn't completely drive out the Gentiles as God commanded, resulting in them and their descendants falling into sin and a vicious cycle.

你是否觉得你的教会正处于恶性循环之中？教会不但没有增长和进步，还看似有很多问题——这里有罪的问题，那里亦有；这里要对付罪恶，那里又要处理人的不顺服。你的教会是否一直往下走，没有提升？这里所指的并非人数，而是指顺服和纯洁的程度。也许这是因为领袖未能树立好的榜样。你教会的领袖与神的关系是否密切？他们是否顺服神的话，并信靠祂？他们对神的工作有没有异象？

Does your church feel like it's in a downward spiral? Instead of growth and progress, it seems to have numerous problems — sin here, disobedience there, sin here, disobedience there . Is your church constantly declining, not improving? This isn't referring to numbers, but to levels of obedience and purity. Perhaps this is because the leaders aren't setting a good example. Do your church leaders have a strong relationship with God? Are they obedient to His Word and trusting in Him? Do they have a vision for God's work?

初期他们事奉诸巴力，接着他们事奉诸巴力和亚舍拉，不仅是男的偶像巴力，还有女的偶像亚舍拉。不管是男偶像，或是女偶像，它们的庙里面都有妓女，拜偶像跟淫行是混在一起的。最后，以色列人所拜的偶像越来越多，迦南人拜什么，他们就拜什么，他们的灵命越来越不像样，他们所行的恶也越来越多，每况越下。他们拜偶像一段时间后，忘记了神，最后，离弃神，不再事奉他。

Initially, they worshipped Baal, then Baal and Asherah. This included not only male Baal idols but also female Asherah. Temples for both male and female idols were populated by prostitutes, and idolatry and sexual immorality were intertwined. Eventually , the Israelites' idolatry grew in number, adopting whatever the Canaanites worshipped. Their spiritual lives deteriorated, and their evil deeds continued to increase. After a period of idolatry, they forgot God and eventually abandoned Him, no longer serving Him.

今天我们不会拜民间信仰的偶像，可是，这并不表示我们不会犯拜偶像的罪。人所犯的罪的背后往往都有一个更严重的罪，就是拜偶像——以别的代替神，在神以外寻求满足或保障。昔日以色列人拜众多的巴力和迦南人的神，只是想生活、家庭得到保障。我们要注意，也要提醒自己：若不是以神为我们生活最大的保障，我们一定会以别的作为我们的保障。我们的信心就会从神转到那些我们以为能给我们保障的事上，那些事情就会成为我们的偶像，我们就会渐渐忘记神，甚至离弃神，不再事奉他。金钱就是现在不少信徒的偶像。还有别的偶像吗？

Today, we don't worship idols from folk religions, but that doesn't mean we won't commit the sin of idolatry. Behind every sin, there's often a more serious sin: idolatry — replacing God with something else, seeking satisfaction or security outside of God . The Israelites worshipped numerous Baal and Canaanite gods simply to secure their lives and families. We must be mindful and remind ourselves: If we don't rely on God as the greatest security in our lives, we will inevitably rely on something else. Our faith will shift from God to things we believe can provide us with security. These things will become our idols, and we will gradually forget God, even abandoning Him and ceasing to serve Him. Money is an idol for many believers today. Are there other idols?

警告：中国教会不断受逼迫和苦难的试炼的同时，还要面对富足的考验。很多时候，富足和安逸比逼迫更难对付，如温水煮蛙般能无声无息地把灵魂诱去，也能使最热心的战士打瞌睡。这样的事情已经在不少教会发生过。要慎防这样的事情发生在你的教会和你身上！

Warning: While the Chinese church continues to endure the trials of persecution and suffering, it also faces the test of prosperity. Often, prosperity and comfort are more difficult to deal with than persecution. Like a frog in boiling water, they can silently lure away souls and lull even the most ardent warriors into sleep. This has already happened in many churches. Be careful to prevent this from happening to your church and yourself!

What should leaders do when sin occurs in the church? Let's discuss this now. What are the consequences if leaders do nothing?

罪一定要对付，不可拖延！  Sin must be dealt with without delay!

We must never underestimate God's discipline. When faced with God's discipline, we must quickly repent and turn away from our evil ways, or we will suffer even greater hardship. At the same time, we must remember that God loves us; we are His precious children, and that is why He disciplines us.

鲍维均博士是美籍华人，是美国三一神学院新约系的系主任，也是研究路加福音的专家。大概五十多岁的时候，他去检查身体，发现胆固醇很低，心脏好像一个年青人一样，他就很高兴。有一次，他坐了十多个小时的长途飞机到另外一个国家讲道。他在飞机上睡了十个小时。由于睡眠充足，他就更高兴，认为自己的讲道应该不会有什么问题。可是，当飞机快要降落的时候，他突然发现他张不开嘴巴，但是他来这里就是要讲道啊。在惊慌之中，他勉强用力张开口，却把关节弄坏了。现在他的嘴巴还是不能像以前那样，完全张开。神管教他，他以后就不敢再为自己的健康等等而骄傲。

神的管教有时的确很严厉，有时的确很痛苦，但这是他对我们任性刚愎的恩慈回应。他施行惩罚是因为他爱我们，要叫我们离开罪恶，把我们重新带回他那里。神容许降下苦难可以有不同原因；管教我们，使我们离开罪恶是其中之一。神的管教是可怕的。然而，若神不管教，容让我们一直错下去，情况就更可怕；单就是今生，我们将要面对很多很大的亏损。

God's discipline can be severe and painful at times, but it is a gracious response to our waywardness. He punishes us because he loves us and wants to turn us from our sins and bring us back to himself. God allows suffering for many reasons; disciplining us to turn us from our sins is one of them. God's discipline is terrible. However, if God does not discipline us and allows us to continue in our sins, the situation is even more terrible. In this life alone, we will face many and great losses.

你最近有没有被神管教？他是否正管教你？如果是的话，请记住神不是因为不爱你而管教你，而是因为他爱你！不要灰心，要聆听神对你说的话。圣灵在叫你脱离什么罪？他叫你为什么罪悔改？你要立即对付这罪，并归回神的道路，不要拖延！要忍受管教，并全心全意跟随神（参来 12:5~12）。

Don't lose heart; listen to what God is saying to you. What sin is the Holy Spirit calling you to deliver from ? What sin is He calling you to repent of? Deal with this sin immediately and return to God's ways. Do n't delay! Endure discipline and follow God wholeheartedly ( see Hebrews 12 : 5-12 ) .

赛 57:15   因为那至高至上、永远长存、名为圣者的如此说：我住在至高至圣所在,也与心灵痛悔谦卑的人同居，要使谦卑人的灵苏醒，也使痛悔人的心苏醒。

Isa 57:15 For thus says the high and lofty One who inhabits eternity, whose name is Holy: I dwell in the high and holy place, also with him who is of a contrite and humble spirit, to revive the spirit of the humble, and to revive the heart of the contrite ones.

神是至高至上，一直活到永永远远的。而他住的地方是至高至圣的，试问谁有资格可以跟他同住？但以赛亚书这节经文说：他与心灵痛悔谦卑的人同居。这是何等大的应许！为什么神要这样做？因为他愿意我们苏醒，复兴过来。这真是个好消息！当我们因罪受苦时，我们可以向神呼求！我们可以在痛苦中奔向神，可以飞到神的宝座前向他恳求！神不但准许我们这样做，还欢迎我们向他呼求！他喜欢我们这样来到他面前。神也利用我们生命中要忍受的困难和痛苦，使我们以合宜的心态向神呼求。什么是合宜的心态？就是以谦卑、痛悔的心态呼求神，神必垂听！

God also uses the difficulties and pain we endure in life to help us cry out to Him with the right attitude. What is the right attitude? It means crying out to God with a humble and contrite heart, and He will hear!

所以，不管我们犯了什么罪，我们要尽快悔改，离开罪恶，转向神，呼求神赦免我们所犯的罪。我们要谨记希伯来书 4 章 16 节的应许：“所以，我们只管坦然无惧的来到施恩的宝座前，为要得怜恤，蒙恩惠，作随时的帮助。”这是何等宝贵的应许！上文提到我们会有软弱、胜不过试探，但既然说要坦然无惧，那我们就不用转弯抹角，只管坦诚地认罪。大卫犯了很严重的罪，可是，他认识神的大慈爱，于是他真诚悔改，坦然向神认罪。诗篇第 32 和 51 篇都是很典型的例子。

Therefore, no matter what sin we have committed, we must repent as soon as possible, turn away from sin, turn to God, and ask God to forgive our sins. We must remember Hebrews 4 Chapter 16 The promise in verse 16: “ Let us therefore draw boldly to the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy and find grace to help in time of need. ” What a precious promise! The passage above mentioned that we may be weak and unable to overcome temptation, but since we are told to be bold, we don’t need to beat around the bush; simply confess our sins openly. David committed a serious sin, but he recognized God’s great love, so he sincerely repented and confessed his sins openly. Psalms 32 and 51 are classic examples.

经文：  Scripture:

士 2:18   耶和华为他们兴起士师，就与那士师同在。士师在世的一切日子，耶和华拯救他们脱离仇敌的手。

Judges 2:18 And when the LORD raised up a judge for them, he was with the judge: and the LORD saved them out of the hand of their enemies all the days of the judge.

 

问题：  question:

 神兴起什么人？

   Who does God raise up?

 神要通过他们做什么？

   What does God want to do through them?

 神为什么要这样做？百姓受苦，神觉得怎样？

   Why did God do this? How did God feel about the suffering of his people?

 

解释：  explain:

我们要让全世界知道：神是圣洁的神，他憎恶罪恶；也要让全世界知道：神是有恩典的神，他喜欢施恩。神是圣洁的神，同时也是有恩典的神。以色列民向神呼求，神就以他的怜悯和恩典回应他们。他不一定要怜悯他们，律法也说明罪人要死。以色列民不配得怜悯，也不配得他的恩典，然而神却拯救了他们。这就是神的怜悯和恩典。以色列民原本不配得拯救，但神却以他的怜悯拯救了他们。

We must let the whole world know that God is holy and hates sin; we must also let the whole world know that God is gracious and loves to show mercy. God is both holy and gracious. When the Israelites cried out to God, He responded with mercy and grace. He did not have to show them mercy; the law also stated that sinners must die. The Israelites did not deserve mercy or grace, yet God saved them. This is God's mercy and grace. The Israelites did not deserve salvation, but God saved them through His mercy.

对我们来说也是如此。神拯救了我们，并以圣召呼召我们。他救我们，并不是因为我们有什么了不起，这完全是他给我们的恩典。他已经怜悯我们，且要继续把怜悯和恩典加给我们。我们的神真伟大！

The same is true for us. God saved us and called us with a holy calling. He saved us not because we were anything special, but entirely out of grace. He has shown mercy to us and will continue to extend mercy and grace to us. Our God is truly great!

请注意神用来拯救以色列民的士师，他们都不是完全的人。亚伯兰不用神给他什么证据就因信而行；可是，基甸却多番要求神给他证据才敢去。有些士师的出身的背景很不好，例如耶弗他，他是妓女的儿子，他的兄弟都看不起他。可是，神用他。这些性格、出身背景都不好的人，都能被神用来拯救整个民族。但请留意关于这些领袖的两件事情：

Please note that the judges God used to save the Israelites were not perfect people. Abram acted on faith without needing any proof from God; however, Gideon repeatedly asked God for proof before he dared to go. Some of the judges came from poor backgrounds, such as Jephthah, the son of a prostitute, whose brothers looked down on him. Yet, God used him. Even these people with poor personalities and backgrounds could be used by God to save the entire nation. But please note two things about these leaders:

 

 神为了他自己的荣耀使用他们。因此，他也可以使用像你一样不完美的器皿。那么，不管你认为自己有多软弱，你的背景多么不好，你要知道：神不会因为这些而决定不用你；神当年能用那些士师，他今天也可以用你。有一件事是你要相信和接受的：神要用你，你是有君尊的祭司；神拯救你只是一个开始，你还要事奉神，专心跟从他。

God used them for His own glory. Therefore, He can also use imperfect vessels like you. So, no matter how weak you think you are or how unfavorable your background may be, know this: God will not decide not to use you because of these things. God was able to use those judges then, and He can use you today. One thing you must believe and accept: God wants to use you; you are a royal priest. God's salvation is only the beginning; you still need to serve Him and follow Him wholeheartedly.

 

 他们虽然是领袖，但还是要为自己的罪受苦。虽然他们被放在特别的岗位上，但还是要像其他信徒般承担自己犯罪的后果。虽然他们是领袖，神又使用他们，但若他们任意犯罪，也必定会被罪火烧伤。我们要特别提到参孙。参孙有很大的失败，更因失败而落到很可怜的光景。然而，他的生命故事并没有就此完结。最后他呼求神，求神给他最后一次力量。神听了他的呼求，应允了他，借他施行拯救。

 Though they were leaders, they still had to suffer for their sins. Although they were placed in special positions, they still had to bear the consequences of their sins like other believers. Although they were leaders and God used them, if they sinned willfully, they would inevitably be burned by the fire of sin. We should especially mention Samson. Samson suffered a great failure, and because of this failure, he fell into a pitiful state. However, his life story did not end there. Finally, he cried out to God, asking Him to give him one last burst of strength. God heard his cry, answered him, and brought salvation through him.

 

总结：  Summarize:

士师记告诉我们：跟自己和罪恶的一点妥协，可以带来很长久的痛苦，连下一代也会受到影响。本来以色列人在应许之地是要承受福气，在万民中见证神。可是，一点的妥协，不仅令他们失去了福气，更因而受尽痛苦。士师记的结尾告诉我们：他们最后堕落到一个地步，互相残杀，非常可怕！

The book of Judges teaches us that even a small compromise with oneself and sin can bring long-lasting suffering, even affecting the next generation . The Israelites were originally destined to inherit blessings in the Promised Land and bear witness to God among all nations. However, a small compromise not only cost them these blessings but also led to untold suffering. The book of Judges concludes with us: they ultimately degenerated to the point of slaughtering one another. It was truly horrific!

士师记的最后一节经文是：“那时，以色列中没有王，各人任意而行。”“各人任意而行”在士师记重复出现了好几遍(17:6，18:1，19:1)。什么是任意而行？当各人以自己为王的时候，各人就任意而行。我们跟自己或罪恶妥协的背后，也是同样的

The last verse of the Book of Judges is : " In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes. " The phrase " everyone did what was right in his own eyes " appears several times in the Book of Judges ( 17:6 , 18:1 , 19:1 ) . What does "do what is right in his own eyes" mean? When everyone considers himself king, they do what is right in their own eyes. The same thing happens when we compromise with ourselves or with sin.

心态，我们以自己为王：自行决定什么要遵行，什么不用遵行，或遵行到什么程度。我们成了自己的王、自己的偶像，这正正是罪恶的本质。

In this mindset, we make ourselves kings: we decide for ourselves what to obey, what not to obey, and to what extent to obey. We become our own kings and our own idols, which is the essence of sin.

今天，有没有一些事情，是神要你从你的生命中赶出去的呢？你要赶出去吗？你若完全赶出，就必定会经历到从神而来的福气。相反，若你不赶出或没有全然赶出，这些事情必成为你眼中的刺，肋下的荆棘。

Are there things today that God wants you to remove from your life? Do you want to remove them? If you do, you will experience blessings from God. On the other hand, if you don't remove them, or if you don't remove them completely, they will become thorns in your eyes and thorns in your side.

我们的生命应该如同香膏一样，为主发出香气。可是，一点的妥协，就如同一只死苍蝇，会使香油发出臭气（参传 10:1），会败坏我们的生命。因此，我们不要一错再错。错了以后，要尽快彻底离开罪恶。不然的话，我们就要承受更多、更大的痛苦。

Our lives should be like ointment, emitting a fragrant aroma for the Lord. However, even the slightest compromise is like a dead fly, which makes the ointment smell foul (see Ecclesiastes 10 : 1 ) and corrupts our lives. Therefore, we must not repeat our mistakes . After we make a mistake, we must quickly and completely turn away from sin. Otherwise, we will endure even greater pain.

第四次循环

 米甸人

基甸

士6:1-8:28

基甸如何打仗？ 

  1. 神所揀選的三百人跟隨基甸，為以色列擊敗米甸人。
  2. 基甸將三百人分作三隊，把號角和空瓶交在各人手裏，瓶內都藏著火把。
  3. 他們就吹號，打破瓶子。
  4. 他們左手拿著火把，右手拿著號角，喊叫說，『有刀為著耶和華，為著基甸！』
  5. 米甸全營的人都亂竄。
  6. 在他們的勝利之後，基甸求以色列人將所奪的金子和紫色衣服給他。基甸製造了一個以弗得，設立在他的城。以色列人到那裏拜那以弗得，如同淫婦；這以弗得就成了基甸和他全家的網羅。

基甸成功的秘訣：

  1. 他仔細聽神的話，這在當時以色列人中間是少有的。
  2. 基甸服從神的話，照著神的話而行。
  3. 他拆毀巴力祭壇，砍下亞舍拉，這摸著神的心。
  4. 藉著拆毀父親的巴力祭壇，砍下亞舍拉，基甸犧牲了他與父親的關係，以及他對社會的享受。因著他所做的，俄弗拉城的人就與他爭辯，甚至要殺他。他的犧牲是他成功的有力因素。七章二節揀選三百人的事，也強調為著神的定旨犧牲我們個人的權益和享受。

基甸失敗的關鍵：

  1. 基甸不仁慈。他殺了那些不支持他的同胞（八16-17），干犯了神的第六條誡命。
  2. 他放縱肉體的情慾，對肉體的情慾毫不約束。士師記八章三十節告訴我們，基甸有七十個兒子，因為他有許多的妻，藉此他干犯了第七條誡命。
  3. 雖然他在拒絕管理百姓的事上作得很好（八22-23），但他貪戀百姓所奪的掠物百姓就將掠物交給他。
  4. 基甸放縱性慾並貪戀金子，就導致拜偶像。貪婪就是拜偶像（西三5），淫亂與貪婪都連於拜偶像（弗五5）。
  5. 基甸用他從百姓取得的金子製造一個以弗得，這以弗得成了以色列人的偶像。

补充：

  • 基甸起初拆毀巴力祭壇及其偶像，但在他成功之後，卻建立另一個敬拜的東西。這失敗抵銷了他一切的成功。
  • 基甸死後，有慘烈的殺害弟兄、奪位與內戰。陀拉與睚珥作士師(八33~十5)。
