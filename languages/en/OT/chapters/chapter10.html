这一课讲述以色列史上王国分裂的时期。这时，以色列已分裂成为南北两国，国力和灵性均慢慢步向衰落。不过，南北两国衰落的速度和途径不同；北国以色列衰落的速度，比南国犹大快。这一课，我们将着重讲以色列的衰落和被掳。

This lesson explores the period of Israel's divided kingdom. By this time, Israel, divided into two kingdoms, was slowly declining both physically and spiritually. However, the speed and path of decline differed between the two kingdoms; the northern kingdom of Israel declined more rapidly than the southern kingdom of Judah. In this lesson, we will focus on Israel's decline and exile.

研读这段衰落时期的历史，也许会令人忧伤慨叹；然而，从中我们仍可看到两个闪烁耀目的亮点。首先，虽然百姓一再令神失望，但他仍然信实地看顾自己的百姓；第二，在这衰落当中，仍有极少数人完全跟随神和全心爱神。

Studying the history of this period of decline may be a cause for sorrow and lamentation, yet we can also see two shining bright spots. First, God remained faithful to His people despite their repeated disappointments. Second, amidst this period of decline, a tiny minority still followed Him completely and loved Him wholeheartedly.

Such cruel and disgusting methods were the policy adopted by the Assyrian Empire in dealing with occupied territories. Its purpose was to undermine the morale of neighboring countries, causing them to fall into a state of complete terror and forcing them to surrender as soon as possible.

亚述的另一个政策，是把占领地的所有人口放逐到其他占领区。这样会较容易控制他们，使们没法在原居地重建国家，那就只留乖乖地留在亚述境内。

Another Assyrian policy was to deport all the people of the occupied territories to other occupied territories. This made it easier to control them, preventing them from rebuilding their own country in their original place of residence, and forcing them to stay within Assyria.

Nineveh, the capital of Assyria, was located on the Tigris River. It was a very ancient city, founded at the dawn of human history (see Genesis 10:8-11). During the Assyrian Empire (circa 800 B.C.), Nineveh's walls were high and thick, a magnificent sight. This empire was undoubtedly powerful, but also wicked.

怒、满有怜悯。一个极其残暴的外邦国家，神也怜悯；一个背叛神的先知，神也忍耐，神又岂会不怜悯他的百姓呢？关键是他们是否愿意悔改归向神？

God is both wrathful and merciful. He showed mercy to an extremely brutal pagan nation and was patient with a prophet who betrayed Him. How could He not show mercy to His people? The key is whether they are willing to repent and turn to God?

解釋：

除了亞述之外，我們的“地圖”上還有另一個地方，那是什麼？

是巴比倫。巴比倫是個非常古老的城（創 10:10），於主前700 年左右成了一座非常宏偉的大城。它的四周築起城牆，由東至西約寬18 公里；據一位歷史學家說，這些牆高逾100 公尺，厚約30 公尺，有100 個實心的青銅閘門，以及250 個比城牆還要高出30 公尺的守望台。巴比倫統治的地方包括今天的伊拉克和伊朗。在伊拉克首都巴格達以南大約80 公里的地方，現在人們仍然可以看到巴比倫城的廢墟。

這個巴比倫帝國並非第一個以巴比倫城為中心的帝國；早於主前1500 年左右，即以色列人出埃及時，首個以此城為中心的帝國便已存在。但之後亞述帝國興起，巴比倫城兩次遭亞述王攻擊和掠奪，所以有一段長時間，巴比倫受到亞述帝國控制。可是，後來巴比倫逐漸強大起來，直至……
