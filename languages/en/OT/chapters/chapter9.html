 以色列国首三个国王是谁？

   Who were the first three kings of Israel?

 

解释：  explain:

为什么以色列人要求立王？撒母耳的两个儿子是以色列最后的两位士师。可是他们腐败，百姓就厌弃他们。此外，当时以色列的景况一团糟。百姓厌倦了恶性循环，但他们对如何终止恶性循环却有错误的想法。他们没有回转归向耶和华，反而认为应该像周围的国家一般立王（参撒上 8:4~9），这样整个民族就能强大起来。神是他们的王，多番为他们施行拯救，他们要另立王，可见百姓根本就厌弃神。因此神对撒母耳说：“他们不是厌弃你，乃是厌弃我……”（撒上 8:5~7）。百姓厌弃以色列真正的大君王，立他们想要的王（参撒上 12:12~13）。虽然百姓因为错误的动机立王，但神却施恩使这个国家在这时期兴盛富强起来。联合王国时期就是这样展开了。

Why did the Israelites demand a king? Samuel's two sons were Israel's last two judges. However, they were corrupt, and the people rejected them. Furthermore, Israel's situation was in turmoil. The people were tired of the downward spiral , but they had misguided ideas about how to end it. Instead of turning to Jehovah, they believed they should establish a king, as the surrounding nations had done (see 1 Samuel 8 : 4-9 ) , to strengthen the nation. God was their King, having repeatedly delivered them, but they wanted another king, demonstrating their fundamental rejection of Him. Therefore, God said to Samuel, " They have not rejected you, but they have rejected Me ..." ( 1 Samuel 8 : 5-7 ) . The people rejected Israel's true great king and established a king of their own choosing ( see 1 Samuel 12 : 12-13 ) . Although the people had wrong motives in establishing a king, God showed His grace and enabled the nation to prosper and become powerful during this period. This is how the period of the United Kingdom began.

以色列在分裂成为南北两国前有三个王，分别是扫罗、大卫和所罗门。你可以怎样记住这三个名字？《附录七：三个王》的故事能帮助你记住他们的名字。

扫罗是以色列第一位君王；他身材高大，可是灵命却不成熟。

Saul was Israel's first king; he was physically tall but spiritually immature.

1.      例如：  For example:

2.      私自献祭。当撒母耳没有在约定的时间回来，又眼见百姓开始散去，扫罗承受的压力愈来愈大。最后他屈服于压力之下，违背了神的吩咐（参撒上 13:1~13）。

3.      Saul offered a sacrifice secretly. When Samuel did not return at the appointed time and the people began to disperse, the pressure on Saul grew. Finally, he succumbed to the pressure and disobeyed God's command (see 1 Samuel 13:1-13).

4.       鲁莽顽固。扫罗起了一个愚蠢的誓，禁止士兵进食。他不但没有谦卑下来承认过错，反而为了表现自己的权威而变得顽固起来，甚至不惜要把作战时吃了一点蜜的儿子杀掉（参撒上14:24~46）。

5.      Reckless and stubborn. Saul made a foolish oath, forbidding his soldiers to eat. Instead of humbled and admitting his mistake, he became stubborn in his pursuit of authority, even going so far as to kill his son for eating a little honey during battle (see 1 Samuel 14:24-46).

6.      厌弃神的命令。在攻打亚玛力人一役，神原本要求扫罗灭尽城里的一切，但他违背神的命令，把城里上好的牛羊及亚玛力王的性命保留下来（参撒上 15）。当撒母耳指出他的问题后，他假意悔改，把责任推卸给其他人。

7.      He disregarded God's command. During the battle against the Amalekites, God originally commanded Saul to destroy everything in the city, but he disobeyed God's command and spared the city's best sheep and cattle, as well as the life of the Amalekite king (see 1 Samuel 15). When Samuel pointed out his problem, he feigned repentance and shifted the blame to others.

8.       

 嫉妒杀人。虽然自知不是一个好君王，也知道神要把王位传给别人，然而扫罗不但没有顺服神的旨意，为继任人作准备，反而嫉妒起来，多次企图杀死除掉大卫这个继任人。在自己生命快到尽头的时候，扫罗仍像捕捉猎物般追杀大卫（参撒上 18~26）。

 Jealousy led to murder. Although Saul knew he was not a good king and that God would pass the throne to someone else, he not only failed to obey God's will and prepare for a successor, but instead became jealous and repeatedly attempted to kill David as his successor. Near the end of his life, Saul continued to hunt David like prey (see 1 Samuel 18-26 ) .

 求问交鬼的妇人。强敌当前，扫罗惧怕起来，加上神不与他说话，没有给他指引，扫罗竟然违反了神的禁令，求问交鬼的妇人，而这禁令他是清楚知道的（参撒上 28:3；申 18:10~11）。

 Consulting a medium. Faced with a powerful enemy, Saul grew fearful. Furthermore, God refused to speak to him or provide him with guidance. Saul actually violated God 's prohibition and consulted a medium, a prohibition he was well aware of (see 1 Samuel 28 : 3 ; Deuteronomy 18 : 10-11 ) .

这些都是属灵生命不成熟的征状。扫罗虽然身材高大，但内心和灵性却不行。结果，他的生命在羞耻中结束了。

These are all signs of spiritual immaturity. Saul may have been physically immature, but his heart and spirit were not. As a result, his life ended in disgrace.

为什么我们说扫罗对神无心无意？从表面来看，扫罗失败的原因是因为惧怕。因为惧怕军心动摇而私自献祭；因为惧怕百姓不听他，于是留下上好的牛羊来讨好百姓；因为怕大卫比自己更受人拥戴，并且怕失去自己的王位而追杀大卫；因为怕撒母耳而假意悔改；因为怕打败仗而求问交鬼的妇人……。

Why do we say Saul was indifferent to God? On the surface, Saul's defeat was due to fear. He offered sacrifices secretly, fearing the army would lose its morale. He withheld his best cattle and sheep to appease the people, fearing they would disobey him. He pursued David, fearing he would gain more favor than himself and lose his throne. He feigned repentance out of fear of Samuel. He sought counsel from a medium, fearing defeat ....

他最重视的压根儿就是自己的王位和面子；他不信任神，并且厌弃神的命令，于是用自己的方法来保住自己的王位。可见，他所做的都是以自己为中心，不是以神为中心；他对神无心无意，因此，正如历代志上 10 章 13 至 14 节所说的，他不遵守神的命令，也没有寻求神，反而寻求交鬼的妇人，而他知道这是神憎恶的。

His greatest concern was his throne and reputation. He distrusted God and despised His commands, so he resorted to his own methods to preserve his throne. Clearly, everything he did was centered on himself, not God . He had no heart or regard for God. Therefore, as 1 Chronicles 10 : 13-14 says , he did not obey God's commands or seek Him. Instead, he sought out mediums, whom he knew were abominable to God.

警告：扫罗是个鲜明例子，说明我们为何不可以把未成熟的初信者，放在领导的位置。他们也许有恩赐，也许很愿意，也许有时间，也许可以即时解决领袖不足的问题。但如果你在他们尚未受过试验前，就把他们放在领袖岗位上，这样恐怕最终会摧毁他们。你应该先让他们事奉一段日子，先让他们处理较容易的事情，然后才慢慢领他们进入领导的角色，同时也可以观察他们内心所重视的是什么。过早把领导的责任加给别人，也许正是你过去遇上麻烦的原因。你的教会确实很需要领袖，但切勿将过早把大量责任放在有潜质当领袖的人身上！

Warning: Saul is a stark example of why we shouldn't place immature new believers in leadership positions. They may have the gifts, they may be willing, they may have the time, and they may be able to address any leadership deficiencies immediately. But if you place them in leadership positions before they've been tested, you risk destroying them. Let them serve for a while, handling the easier tasks first, before gradually introducing them to leadership roles and observing what they value. Imposing leadership responsibilities prematurely may have been the cause of your past troubles. Your church certainly needs leaders, but don't place too much responsibility on potential leaders too soon!

经文：  Scripture:

撒上 16:7、13    耶和华却对撒母耳说：不要看他的外貌和他身材高大，我不拣选他。因为，耶和华不像人看人：人是看外貌；耶和华是看内心。13 撒母耳就用角里的膏

1 Samuel 16:7 , 13 But the LORD said to Samuel , “Do not consider his appearance or his height , for I have rejected him. For the LORD does not see as man sees: man looks on the outward appearance, but the LORD looks on the heart.” 13 Then Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him.

anointed him in the presence of his brothers. And the Spirit of the LORD came mightily upon David from that day forward.

1 Samuel 30:6-8 David was greatly distressed, for the people were vexed because of their sons and daughters, and they were saying they would stone him. But David trusted in the Lord his God and strengthened his heart. 7 David said to Abiathar the priest, son of Ahimelech, "Please bring the ephod here." Abiathar brought the ephod to David. 8 David inquired of the Lord, saying, "If I pursue the enemy, will I be able to overtake them?" The Lord replied, "You may pursue them; you will surely overtake them and rescue them all."

 

问题：  question:

 根据撒母耳记上 16 章 7 节，人看的是什么？神看的又是什么？我们寻找教会领袖时，要考虑什么？

   According to 1 Samuel 16 : 7 , what do people see? What does God see? What should we consider when seeking church leaders ?

 根据撒母耳记上 30 章 6 至 8 节，当大卫面对众人的压力，甚是焦急的时候，他向谁求问？

   According to 1 Samuel 30 : 6-8 , when David was facing pressure from the crowd and was very anxious, who did he turn to for help?

解释：  explain:

神按他的心意，拣选了大卫大代替扫罗。大卫诚然有严重的失败，但整体上，他对神是全心全意的。他常常祷告，是众多诗篇的作者。撒母耳记的作者刻意拿大卫跟扫罗作对比。扫罗面对非利士时，神不回答他，那时，他其实他可以在神面前自卑、认罪，再向神呼求，这是士师时代给我们的教训之一。可是，他没有这样做，反而去求问交鬼的妇人。正如我们先前所指出的，其实他由始至终都没有真诚地悔改。反观大卫，当他的妻子和跟随他的人的妻儿都被亚玛力人掳走，他同样面对军心不稳，并且不知敌人在什么地方，妻子是生是死，但他却坚定地求问神他们能否追得上。大卫作王后，他最关心的，不是如何保住自己的王位，而是把约柜带进耶路撒冷，其后，更打算为神建殿。

According to God's will, he chose David to replace Saul. While David certainly had his share of serious failures, overall, he was wholeheartedly devoted to God. He prayed frequently and was the author of numerous Psalms. The author of Samuel deliberately contrasted David with Saul. When Saul faced the Philistines and God didn't answer him, he could have humbled himself before God, confessed his sin, and then cried out to Him—a lesson from the period of the Judges. However, he didn't do so, instead consulting a medium. As we've previously pointed out, he never truly repented. In contrast, when David's wife and the wives and children of his followers were taken captive by the Amalekites, he faced a similarly unstable army, uncertain where the enemy was, and whether his wife was alive or dead. Yet, he steadfastly prayed to God whether they could catch up. After David became king, his primary concern wasn't securing his throne, but bringing the Ark of the Covenant to Jerusalem and, later, planning to build a temple for God.

旧约圣经用了最长的篇幅来描述哪个圣经人物的生命故事？大卫。他的名字的意思就是蒙爱的。大卫的生命故事并不是不吃人间烟火，而是非常有血有肉。有非常有情有义的时候，有被人出卖的时候；有非常荣耀的时候，也有受尽羞辱的时候；他有非常敬畏神的时候，也有非常得罪神的时候。他尝尽人生的甜酸苦辣。但他的生命故事跟神却是紧紧相连，他所写的众多诗篇就是最好的证明。不管他失败得有多严重，他仍然没有放弃神，因而没有放弃自己。因为他知道他是大卫，是蒙爱的人。

Which biblical figure's life story is described at length in the Old Testament? David. His name means "beloved." David's life story was not detached from the mundane world, but rather deeply lived. There were times of great affection and loyalty, as well as betrayal; times of great glory, as well as humiliation; times of deep reverence for God, as well as times of profound offense. He experienced the ups and downs of life. Yet, his life story was closely connected to God, as evidenced by the many psalms he wrote. No matter how grievously he failed, he never gave up on God, and therefore never gave up on himself. Because he knew he was David, the beloved one.

当我们把他的名字和他的一生连贯起来的时候，我们会得着很大安慰、盼望和鼓励。在人生的一切甜酸苦辣中，大卫知道他仍然是神所爱的，不是因为他配得，而是因为神丰盛的慈爱。因此，从悔罪方面来说，他对神是全心全意的，当神因为他数点百姓而降瘟疫，大卫为百姓代求，情愿自己和自己的家人受罚（参代上 21:17）；以为神建殿来说，他也是全心全意的，虽然他是王，他却把自己私人积蓄的金银献上，就是三千他连得（一百多公吨）的上好金子和七千他连得（二百四十公吨）上好的银子（参代上 29:4~5）。

When we connect his name with his life, we find great comfort, hope, and encouragement. Amidst all the ups and downs of life, David knew he was still loved by God, not because he deserved it, but because of God's abundant love. Therefore, in terms of repentance, he was wholeheartedly devoted to God. When God sent a plague because he numbered the people, David interceded for the people, willingly accepting the punishment for himself and his family ( see 1 Chronicles 1:17) . 21:17 ; He was wholehearted in building the temple for God. Although he was a king, he offered his own personal savings of gold and silver, namely, 3,000 talents (over 100 metric tons ) of fine gold and 7,000 talents ( 240 metric tons ) of fine silver ( see 1 Chronicles 21:17 ). 2 9 : 4~ 5 .

今天，你我都是蒙爱的人。没有什么可以使我们与基督的爱隔绝。因此，不管发生什么事，不要放弃自己，也不要放弃神。如何回应神的大爱？我们要全心全意地爱他。现在就让我们一起赞美神的慈爱。

Today, you and I are both beloved. Nothing can separate us from the love of Christ. Therefore, no matter what happens, never give up on yourself, and never give up on God. How do we respond to God's great love? We must love Him with all our heart and soul. Let us now praise God's lovingkindness together.

 

附：大卫的一生可以分为以下几个阶段  

Appendix: David's life can be divided into the following stages

（一）牧羊人大卫（撒上 16:22; 诗 23 篇）

(1 ) David the Shepherd ( 1 Samuel 16:22 ; Psalm 23) (articles )

这是大卫的预备阶段，神用这段时候来训练他。神的训练方法可以用四个词语来概括：

This was David's preparation period, a time God used to train him. God's training method can be summarized in four words:

 孤独：神把大卫从群众的喧嚷和诱惑中领出来，给他反省和默想的时间，以此训练这个年轻的牧羊人。

   Solitude: God trained the young shepherd by taking David away from the noise and temptations of the crowds and giving him time for reflection and meditation .

 卑微：神把他放在一个不被人看见和称赞的地方，使他可以在无人注意下学做一个好工人。他学会了即使没有人在身边，仍然要做诚实可靠的人。

   Humble: God placed him in a place where he would not be seen or praised by others so that he could learn to be a good worker without being noticed . He learned to be an honest and reliable person even when no one is around.

 单调：神让大卫长期埋首于琐碎和规律化的工作。虽然不知道要做到什么时候，但他仍然在小事上忠心。

   Monotony: God allowed David to be immersed in trivial and routine work for a long time. Although he did not know when he would finish his work, he remained faithful in the small things.

 受苦：大卫遇过危险和苦难。活在野地的他，要与野兽搏斗，也要忍受天气的变化，但大卫从中学会了凭勇气和信心应付这些挑战。

   Suffering: David encountered danger and hardship. Living in the wilderness, he had to contend with wild beasts and endure the changing weather . However, David learned to face these challenges with courage and faith.

（二）战士大卫（撒上 16-18 章; 诗 68 篇）

(2 ) David the Warrior ( 1 Samuel 16-18) Chapter ; Psalm 68 (articles )

很快大卫就显示出他是一个信靠神的英勇战士。他杀死巨人歌利亚之后（参撒上 17），在王的军队作高级将领（参撒上 18:5），广受爱戴（参撒上 18:7）。为求娶得扫罗王的女儿米甲，他杀了 200 个非利士人。他与扫罗的儿子约拿单也建立了深厚的友谊。但其后扫罗开始对他非常嫉妒，于是大卫的生命进入另一个阶段……

David quickly demonstrated his prowess as a brave warrior who trusted in God. After slaying the giant Goliath (see 1 Samuel 17), he became a high-ranking commander in the king's army (see 1 Samuel 18:5) and earned widespread favor (see 1 Samuel 18:7). To marry King Saul's daughter Michal, he killed 200 Philistines. He also developed a close friendship with Saul's son Jonathan. But Saul soon became deeply jealous of David, and David's life entered a new phase...

(3 ) David the Fugitive ( 1 Samuel 19-30) Chapter ; Psalm 34 Chapter , 57 articles , 59 articles , 142 articles)

嫉妒驱使扫罗追杀大卫，大卫只得逃亡。接着十年，为了逃避扫罗的怒气，大卫大部分时间住在犹大的旷野过流亡的生活。他失去了高级军官的身份（参撒上 19:8~10），失去了妻子米甲（参撒上 19:10~17），失去了导师撒母耳（参撒上 19:18~19,

Jealousy drove Saul to pursue David, forcing him to flee. For the next ten years, David lived largely in exile in the wilderness of Judah, fleeing Saul's wrath . He lost his high-ranking military officer ( see 1 Samuel 19 : 8-10 ) , his wife Michal ( see 1 Samuel 19 : 10-17 ) , and his mentor Samuel ( see 1 Samuel 19:18-19 ) .

20:1），也失去了挚友约拿单（参撒上 20:1~42）。多么悲惨！但神正在这时候作工，把大卫炼净。神把这些宝贵东西拿走，好让大卫单单倚靠他。虽然大卫失去了这一切，但他没有失去神的应许，也没有失去神的爱。

20 : 1 , and also lost his dear friend Jonathan ( see 1 Samuel 20 : 1-42 . How tragic! But God was working at this time to purify David. God took away these precious things so that David could rely solely on Him. Although David lost all of this , he did not lose God's promises or God's love.

 

（四） 胜利中的君王大卫（撒下 1~10 章; 诗 2 篇）

(4) King David in Victory (2 Samuel 1-10; Psalm 2)

神的器皿终于预备好了，神可以提升大卫的地位，让他先作犹大王，7 年后更让他作整个以色列的王。大卫要把神的约柜带进国都，并要为神建造圣殿；这两件事情足以显出他对神的心意。为此，神给他战无不胜的赏赐。神又向大卫应许：他的儿子要建造圣殿，他的朝代永无终结！

God's vessel was finally ready. God could elevate David's status, first making him king of Judah and, seven years later, king of all Israel. David would bring the Ark of the Covenant into the capital and build a temple for God. These two actions clearly demonstrated his heart for God. For this, God rewarded him with unparalleled victory. God also promised David that his son would build the temple, and that his dynasty would have no end!

 

（五）危机中的君王大卫（撒下 11-24 章; 诗 32 篇, 51 篇）

(5 ) King David in Crisis ( 2 Samuel 11-24) Chapter ; Psalm 32 Chapter , 51 (articles )

大卫正处于成功的顶峰！但对领袖来说，兴盛的时候往往是最危险的时候。大卫犯了奸淫和杀人的罪，他开始有麻烦。虽然大卫被先知拿单责备之后，立刻认罪悔改并获得神的宽恕，但他的罪使他要在余生中不断面对家族的斗争与不和。最大的打击莫过于他英伟多才的儿子押沙龙叛变，迫使他流亡。结果，押沙龙在一场战役中被杀，大卫为此大大悲痛。大卫死时年 70 岁。

David was at the height of his success! But for a leader, times of prosperity are often the most dangerous. Troubles began to arise when David committed adultery and murder. Although David repented immediately after being rebuked by the prophet Nathan and received God's forgiveness, his sins would cause him to face family strife and discord for the rest of his life. The greatest blow came when his brilliant and talented son, Absalom, rebelled and forced David into exile. Absalom was subsequently killed in battle , leaving David devastated. David died at the age of 70 .

Solomon's life is recorded in 1 Kings chapters 1–11 and 2 Chronicles chapters 1–9 . His life can be divided into two phases. His early reign began well. He sought wisdom from God, and God granted him unparalleled wisdom and judgment, as well as abundant wealth. He possessed a humble heart, inherited his father's piety, and earned the loyalty of the nation. He also built a temple and, at its dedication, prayed for the people.

可惜他后来犯了大错，做了一些国王应该禁戒不作的事情。他变得心高气傲（参申 17:20）；他倚重与邻国立约来巩固以色列国的地位和势力，因而接受列国送来的公主，并且立他们作妻子或妃嫔（700 个妻子，300 个妃嫔）（参申 17:17）；他蓄养马匹（参申 17:16），并且开始倚靠物质（参申 17:16）。这一切都叫他的心离开神（参王上 10:23~29）。他年老的时候，开始供奉妻子们所拜的神祇，甚至拜那可憎的偶像米勒公（参王上 11:5，与代下 6:14 作比较）。(编按：米勒公又叫摩洛，它的信徒会用火烧死自己的孩子当作祭物献上。)

Unfortunately , he later made grave mistakes, doing things a king should refrain from. He became proud and arrogant ( see Deuteronomy 17:20 ) . He relied heavily on covenants with neighboring nations to consolidate Israel's position and power, accepting princesses from these nations as wives or concubines ( 700 wives and 300 concubines ) ( see Deuteronomy 17:17 ) . He kept horses ( see Deuteronomy 17:16 ) and began to rely on material things ( see Deuteronomy 17:16 ) . All of this caused his heart to turn away from God (see 1 Kings 10 : 23-29 ) . In his old age, he began to worship the gods worshipped by his wives, even the detestable idol of Milcom ( see 1 Kings 11 : 5 , compare 2 Chronicles 6:14 ). ( Editor's note: Milcom was also called Molech, whose followers would burn their own children as sacrifices. )

所罗门的人生路程开始得很好，可惜虎头蛇尾。起初他全心全意敬拜神，但最后却做了神眼中看为恶的事。因此，我们说所罗门只是半心半意地跟随神。权力、财富和美色足以使大有智慧的所罗门离弃神，我们就更要小心了。

Solomon's life began well, but unfortunately ended poorly. He worshipped God wholeheartedly at first, but ultimately committed acts that were evil in God's eyes. Therefore, we can say that Solomon followed God only half-heartedly. If power, wealth, and beauty could cause even the wise Solomon to abandon God, we should be even more cautious.

所以，我们要提醒自己：我们的人生要有美好的开始，也要有美好的结束。一位研究圣经领袖和教会领袖的学者指出：按他的统计，10 个领袖当中，只有 3 位可以有美好的结束。我们年纪越大，越要步步为营，不要倚靠自己的经历、经验、地位、名望，因为一不小心，就会像所罗王那样，晚节不保。我们要谨慎，诚诚实实地倚靠主，一直跟主到底。

Therefore, we must remind ourselves: Our lives must begin well and end well. A scholar who studies biblical and church leadership noted that, according to his statistics, only 3 out of 10 leaders can have a happy ending. The older we get, the more cautious we must be. We must not rely on our own experience, status, or reputation. If we are not careful, we could end up like King Solomon, whose integrity was ruined in his later years. We must be cautious, sincerely rely on the Lord, and follow Him to the end.

好的开始很重要，你们很多人的事工都有很好的开始，可是好的开始不保证有好的结束。你必须每天都全心全意、彻彻底底地跟随神，属灵生命亦不可随波逐流。今天要追求神！明天也要追求神！保罗说：“当跑的路我已经跑尽了”（提后 4:7），我们要学效他。愿我们都能漂亮地完成人生的赛事！希伯来书 12 章 1 至 3 节教导我们该如何跑好这场赛事。

A good start is crucial. Many of you have had a strong start in your ministry, but a good start doesn't guarantee a good finish. You must follow God wholeheartedly and thoroughly every day, and your spiritual life cannot drift. Pursue God today! Pursue God tomorrow! Paul said, " I have finished my race " ( 2 Timothy 4 : 7 ). We should follow his example. May we all finish life's race with flying colors! Hebrews 12 Verses 1 to 3 of this chapter teach us how to run this race well.

对有意结婚的人，这里还有一个明确的警告！若连世上最有智慧的人，他的心也被不信主的配偶引离正途，那么，若我们与不信者结婚，又会被牵引到痛苦多少倍的道路上呢？

Here's a stark warning for those considering marriage! If even the wisest people in the world can be led astray by their unbelieving spouses, how much more painful will our lives be if we marry unbelievers?

解释：  explain:

以色列历史中的王国分裂时期开始了。这段历史记载在列王纪上第12 至22 章、

The period of division of the kingdom in Israel's history began. This period is recorded in 1 Kings 12. to 22 chapter,

列王纪下全卷，以及历代志下第 10 至 36 章。

All of 2 Kings, and 2 Chronicles 10 to 36 chapter.

为什么以色列会分裂呢？有两个原因。第一个原因是神命定的。神这样命定是因为所罗门没有全心全意地跟随他，除了敬拜耶和华，他还拜别的神祇──那些可憎的神祇。神曾将这个分裂的局面预先告诉所罗门和耶罗波安（王上 11:11~33）。

Why was Israel divided? There are two reasons. The first was divinely ordained. God ordained this because Solomon did not follow Him wholeheartedly. In addition to worshipping Jehovah, he worshipped other gods — hateful gods. God had foretold this division between Solomon and Jeroboam ( 1 Kings 11 : 11-33 ) .

第二个原因可以从历史发展中看出来，就是所罗门的继任人，年轻的罗波安王不肯听从老年人的主意（参王上 12:8），没有减少所罗门定下的重税、苦工。他年少登位，没有经验；他身边本有善良又负责任的老年人给他出了明智的主意，但他却偏要听从与他同样缺少经验和智慧的少年人，向百姓收取更多的税款，令他们负担更重！这是何等的愚昧！何等的骄傲！直至大部分以色列民（十个支派）背叛离去，只留下两个支派给他，他才在沉痛的教训中明白自己的愚昧。

The second reason can be seen from historical developments. Solomon's successor, the young King Rehoboam, refused to listen to the advice of the older men ( see 1 Kings 12 : 8 ) and did not reduce the heavy taxes and forced labor imposed by Solomon. He came to the throne young and inexperienced. He had kind and responsible older men around him who gave him wise advice, but he chose to listen to young men who were just as inexperienced and lacked wisdom, and collected more taxes from the people, burdening them even more! What foolishness! What pride! It was not until most of the Israelites (the ten tribes) rebelled and left, leaving only two tribes to him, that he learned the hard way about his own folly.

（留意：王上 11:13 所说“还留一个支派”的意思是，在罗波安所属的犹大支派以外，还留下一个支派给他，也就是便雅悯支派。）

(Note: 1 Kings 11:13 says “one tribe was left” meaning that in addition to the tribe of Judah to which Rehoboam belonged, another tribe was left for him, namely, the tribe of Benjamin.)

我们可以从这件事学习到一个原则：神会赐福给全心全意跟随他的人，但如果这个人后来不是全心全意跟随他，就不能久享神所赐的福。神通常不会马上撤回他的恩典。有些时候，一个偏离正路的领袖可能会有一段时间仍能在岗位上和传道工作上得到神的赐福，但这情况不会持久。神这样容许，是因为他有怜悯和忍耐，但假如这个领袖仍不悔改，神的管教终会临到他身上，也会临到他的事工上。如果他继续犯罪，神所赐的福气和荣耀就会离开他。神不会再给他稳固的根基，他就会跌倒。

We can learn a principle from this incident: God blesses those who follow Him wholeheartedly, but if this person later stops following Him wholeheartedly, they will not be able to enjoy God's blessings for long. God usually does not withdraw His grace immediately. Sometimes, a leader who has strayed from the right path may still receive God's blessings in his position and preaching work for a period of time, but this situation will not last. God allows this because of His mercy and patience, but if the leader still does not repent, God's discipline will eventually come upon him and his ministry. If he continues to sin, the blessing and glory given by God will leave him. God will no longer give him a solid foundation, and he will fall.

 

你或许会以为罗波安会吸取父亲的教训，回转过来全心全意地归向神。可惜他没有，他反而离开神的道，结果，因为神没有为以色列争战，埃及王前来掠夺耶路撒冷（参代下 12:1~5），连所罗门打造的荣耀的金盾牌也被夺去（参代下 12:9~11）。罗波安后来以铜盾牌代替；铜可以擦亮，令它看起来好像金光闪闪，但它始终不是金，只是一种廉价的代替品。请注意，每次用完这些盾牌之后，卫兵总都会把盾牌收起来，可能是不想给人看出那不是金盾牌，只是次等的铜制货色。

You might think Rehoboam would have learned from his father's lesson and returned to God wholeheartedly. But he didn't. Instead, he turned away from God's ways. Consequently, because God didn't fight for Israel, the king of Egypt plundered Jerusalem (see 2 Chronicles 12 : 1-5 ) , and even the glorious golden shields Solomon had crafted were taken away ( see 2 Chronicles 12 : 9-11 ) . Rehoboam later replaced them with bronze shields . Bronze can be polished to appear shiny, but it's not gold; it's just a cheap substitute. Note that after each use, the guards always put the shields away, perhaps to avoid the appearance of inferior bronze.

同样，当神所赐的福气离我们而去，我们理应悔改回转，但我们往往却把一切粉饰得像从前一样，以次等货色替代所失去的。无论外表多么相似，实际上却是两回事。

Likewise, when the blessings God has bestowed on us depart, we should repent and turn back, but we often try to make everything look as it was before, replacing what we have lost with inferior goods. However similar they may appear, they are actually two different things .

我们以无生命的组织和无用的传统来代替活的信心，又以追求成功的心（或类似的东西）来取代渴慕神的心，以致无论自己多忙碌，在属灵上也结不出果子，事奉也没有果效。我们是否没有彼此相爱，反而暗中诽谤和中伤别人，与他人争竞，更运用手段取得地位和好处？教会啊！不要甘于接受次等的东西！不要满足于代替品。让我们回到起初的爱心，回到全心全意顺服的荣耀！

We have replaced living faith with lifeless organizations and useless traditions, and replaced a thirst for God with a pursuit of success (or something similar). As a result, no matter how busy we are, we bear no spiritual fruit and our ministry is ineffective. Instead of loving one another, do we secretly slander and backbite others, compete with others, and resort to tactics to gain status and advantage? Church! Do not settle for second best! Do not settle for substitutes. Let us return to our first love and the glory of wholehearted obedience!

（注意：这不是说组织或传统都是属肉体的、邪恶的或次等的。只要加上活的爱心和对神无伪的信心，组织和传统就是有用的、活泼的！但假如没有这份无伪的信心和活的爱心，任何东西都只是劣质的代替品。）

此外，罗波安因为不听百姓的意见，也不听老年人的忠告，却听从朋辈的建议，以为加重百姓的负担就能展示自己比所罗门更有权威，结果导致王国分裂。

今天，作为教会的同工或领袖，我们必须学习做一个好的聆听者。面对重大的事情，我们不要只听那些跟我们关系要好的人的意见，而是要多聆听不同人的意见，并且要放下个人的荣辱得失，把神的旨意和教会的福祉放在首位。我们一定要谨记：偏听必定会破坏教会的合一。

你是否尊重教会中的老人和智者？当然，年长的人不一定比我们有智慧。但教会中监督我们的长老，他们拥有我们或许缺乏的智慧和经验；若忽略他们给我们的智慧忠告，就是愚蠢至极。还有，我们必须提防那些分裂教会和教导虚假教义的人，不管他们是年长还是年轻。

北國與南國  Miền Bắc và miền Nam

北國與南國的關係則為時戰時和，端看當時國際情勢與國王施政政策。

南國與亞述的關係亦是如此，有時成為臣屬國、納貢屈服（如亞哈斯王與瑪拿西王時期）；有時則國力較強，收復失地（西希家王時期）。此種關係一直持續到南國眼見亞述衰亡，為巴比倫帝國所取代。

 

南北兩國均滅亡之後，以色列人經歷巴比倫與波斯帝國之興衰，被擄與回歸，以及聖殿和耶路撒冷崇建的漫長歷程。

隨著希臘的興起，在亞歷山大大帝時期，巴勒斯坦地區開始納入希臘勢力範圍，由希臘軍事將領實施軍事統治，開始希臘化過程（Hellenization）。

此時期稱為「約間時期」－新舊兩約之間的時代。

 

西元前第二世紀，因為設立大祭司的爭議與政治腐敗的問題，造成了約間時期最重要的事件－「瑪加比革命」（瑪喀比革命，Maccabean Revolt ）。

這次革命是因鄉下祭司Mattathias與其子Judah Maccabee起而反叛塞流士帝國（Seleucid Empire），形成哈斯曼王朝（Hasmonean Kingdom of Israel，140–37BC），是為以色列復興運動最成功的ㄧ次。

撒馬利亞人  Samaritan

撒馬利亞人的祖先原為舊約時期的北國以色列人。北國以色列的第 6 任皇帝暗利在撒馬利亞山上興建首都，並稱首都為撒馬利亞城 (880 BC；王上16:24)。自此，撒馬利亞城成為以色列的首都，直至 722 BC 敗於亞述帝國為止 (王下17:5-6)。

當時亞述稱霸，不單把以色列吞噬，還把周邊國家一一吞併 (除了南國猶大)，參圖一。亞述控制戰敗國的其中一個政治手腕，就是刻意遷移戰敗的民族，要同族人分散居住，以減弱、淡化他們的文化及凝聚力。因此當以色列被滅後，亞述把部份以色列人遷至帝國版圖內不同的區域 (王下17:6)，而留在原居地的以色列人則與被遷入的外族人同住 (王下17:24)，令後裔夾雜異族血統及信仰文化。慢慢地，住在這地區的人被統稱為撒馬利亞人。

 

到了羅馬帝國年代，撒馬利亞地區 (大約就是舊約北國以色列的版圖) 及猶太地區 (大約就是舊約南國猶大的版圖) 同被劃分為猶太省，在它們上面的是加利利省，參圖二。猶太省的猶太地區及加利利省同為猶太人的聚居處；夾在中間的撒馬利亞地區則是撒馬利亞人居住的地方。圖二同時標示了數過撒馬利亞地方，包括：撒馬利亞城、基利心山、以巴路山、敍加等。

撒玛利亚人（The Samaritan）是一个非常古老的民族，据称他们是在3000多年前迁居到以色列帝国北部的一个部族的后裔，属于以法莲支派和玛拿西支派。[1]仅存的650名撒玛利亚人中有一半居住在纳布卢斯的基利心山上的祖先留下来的村落里，他们和犹太人一样庆祝逾越节，[2]但他们也讲阿拉伯语，并且同时拥有以色列和巴勒斯坦两国的国籍

 

與猶太人的關係  Quan hệ với người Do Thái

羅馬時期的猶太地區大約就是舊約南國猶大的版圖，居民主要為猶大人的後裔，擁有純正的以色列人血統，統稱為猶太人。雖然撒馬利亞及猶太地區同被劃分為猶太省，但猶太人普遍藐視撒馬利亞人 (約4:9)，原因包括：

  • 自北國以色列立國以來，撒馬利亞是拜各類偶像的地方 (王下17:7-18)
  • 以色列亡國後，當地拜偶像的風俗變得更厲害 (王下17:24-41)
  • 在亞述的政治手段下，留在當地的人與遷入的外族人通婚 (王下17:24)，失去了種族的純正，被猶太人視為不可饒恕的罪
  • 在波斯帝國年代 (538 BC)，撒馬利亞人曾阻礙回歸的南國猶大人重建聖殿 (拉4:1-5)
  • 撒馬利亞人被視為叛道者，因為他們只相信摩西五經，又在基利心山上自建聖殿 (約4:20)。這也解釋了為何哈斯摩尼王朝的猶太人要摧毀他們的殿 (129 BC)

基於猶太人認為撒馬利亞人在各方的不潔 (血統、信仰、文化等)，再加上種種的歷史糾結，他們與撒馬利亞人沒有甚麼來往 (約4:9)。一般猶太人若要往返他們的聚居地猶太和加利利時，寧願繞道，也不願穿過撒馬利亞範圍。由此可見，耶穌取道撒馬利亞，是一個刻意選取的路徑。
