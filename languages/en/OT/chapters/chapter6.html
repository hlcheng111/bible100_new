Step 43​   priest

Action: With your palm facing up, wiggle your crossed right fingers.

接续会幕的动作，把拇指分开，掌心反过来向上，摆动交叉着的右手手指，代表“祭司”。

Continuing with the movement from the Tabernacle, separate your thumbs, turn your palms upward, and move your crossed right fingers to represent " priest . "

 

经文：  Scripture:

出 29:44   我要使会幕和坛成圣，也要使亚伦和他的儿子成圣，给我供祭司的职分。

out 29:44    I will sanctify the tent of meeting and the altar; I will sanctify Aaron and his sons, to serve me as priests . 

问题：  question:

 根据出埃及记 29 章 44 节，神使什么东西成圣？他又使什么人成圣？神使他们成圣，要他们做什么？

   According to Exodus 29 Chapter 44 What does God sanctify? Who does He sanctify? What does He sanctify them for? 

解释：  explain:

会幕就是神与人会面的帐幕。究竟什么人在里面工作的呢？他们负责什么工作呢？

The tabernacle was the tent where God met with mankind. Who worked there? What were their responsibilities?

“司”的意思是负责管理。负责管理某政府部门财政的是该部门的财政司；负责管理献祭的人就是祭司。这节经文提到神使亚伦和他的儿子成圣，为神执行祭司的职分。“成圣”的意思，是成为圣洁，而成为圣洁的意思是：分别出来，为神所用。有一点值得留意，经文也提到神使会幕和祭坛成圣，意思是它们也被分别出来，为神所用；除了神所指定的用途外，它们不可以用作其他的用途。既然人和物件都可以成圣，那么，成圣的基本意思就跟道德没有关系，它着重的是：有没有分别出来，为神所用？所以当一个城被分别出来，为神所用，那就是一个圣城；当一个殿宇被分别出来，为神所用，那就是圣殿。当身为信徒的你和我，被分别出来，为神所用，我们就是圣徒。不犯罪，就等同生活圣洁吗？不是的！若没有分别出来，为神所用，就不圣洁。

" Si " means to be in charge of management. The person in charge of a government department's finances is the department's treasurer; the person in charge of offering sacrifices is the priest. This verse mentions that God sanctified Aaron and his sons to perform priestly duties for God . " Sanctify " means to become holy, and to be holy means to be set apart for God's use. It is worth noting that the verse also mentions that God sanctified the tabernacle and the altar, meaning that they were also set apart for God's use; they could not be used for anything other than what God had designated. Since both people and objects can be sanctified, the fundamental meaning of sanctification has nothing to do with morality; it emphasizes whether something is set apart for God's use. Therefore, when a city is set apart for God's use, it is a holy city; when a temple is set apart for God's use, it is a holy temple. When believers like you and me are set apart for God's use, we are saints. Does not sin equate to a holy life? No! If we are not separated and used by God, we are not holy.

为什么神要设立祭司？要回答这个问题，我们要先回答一个根本的问题：人为什么要献祭？若不用献祭，就不用设立祭司来管理。顾名思义，会幕就是神跟人相会的帐幕。神建立会幕的原因是，他表明他要住在以色列人中间，向人说话，并且与人相会。然而，有罪的人又如何可以跟全然圣洁公义的神相会？这是人要面对的大难题。为了解决这个问题，神设立了献祭制度。这个制度包括两方面：

Why did God establish priests? To answer this question, we must first answer a fundamental question: Why do people offer sacrifices? If sacrifices were not necessary, there would be no need for priests to manage it. As the name suggests, the tabernacle is the tent where God meets with mankind. God established the tabernacle to show that He would dwell among the Israelites, speak to them, and meet with them. However, how could sinful people meet with the completely holy and righteous God? This was a major problem that mankind faced. To solve this problem, God established the sacrificial system. This system includes two aspects:

 有罪的人要借献祭来到神面前，因为若不流血，罪就不得赦免。献祭的人是要表明他把他的罪归在祭物身上，让祭物代替他受罚，叫罪得到赦免。

   Sinners must come before God through sacrifices, because without the shedding of blood, sins cannot be forgiven. The person offering the sacrifice is indicating that he places his sins on the sacrifice, allowing the sacrifice to bear the punishment on his behalf, so that his sins can be forgiven.

 人一定要按神的方法献上祭物，包括要献上什么和怎样献。神把一些特定的人分别出来，设立他们作祭司，让他们专门在献祭的事上服事神，协助人按照神的规定把祭物献上。也就是说，祭司成为圣洁的神，跟有罪的人之间的中间人。

   Sacrifices must be offered according to God's method, including the content and manner of offering. God set apart certain individuals and appointed them as priests, specifically to serve God in sacrificial offerings and to assist people in offering sacrifices according to God's regulations. In other words, priests served as intermediaries between the holy God and sinful humanity.

因此，祭司有两重的角色：（1）在人前代表神，收纳人所献上的祭物；当人要把祭物呈献给神，他就把祭物交祭司处理，或者根据祭司的指示来处理。（2）在神前代表人，把祭物献上；当祭司把献祭的人的祭物献上，他其实是代表那个人把祭物呈献给神，因为祭物不是他的，而是来自献祭的人。

Therefore, the priest has a twofold role: ( 1 ) Representing God before men and accepting the offerings made by men; when men wish to present their offerings to God, they hand them over to the priests for processing, or they process them according to the priests’ instructions. ( 2 ) Representing men before God and offering their offerings; when the priest presents the offering of the man who made the sacrifice, he is actually presenting the offering to God on that man’s behalf, for the offering is not his own but comes from the man who made the sacrifice.

总的来说：祭司负管理献祭的事宜；在神前代表人，在人前代表神。祭司必须是利未人亚伦的后裔；然而，不是每个利未人都能成为祭司，只有亚伦的后裔才可以。

In general, priests were responsible for managing sacrifices; they represented humanity before God and God before humanity. Priests had to be descendants of Aaron, a Levite. However, not every Levite could become a priest; only descendants of Aaron could .

 

基督是我们的大祭司，也是那更美的祭物  Christ is our High Priest and the better sacrifice

来 10:10   我们凭这旨意，靠耶稣基督，只一次献上他的身体，就得以成圣。

Come 10:10    By this will we have been sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all .

来 10:12   但基督献了一次永远的赎罪祭，就在神的右边坐下了。

Come 10:12    But Christ offered one sacrifice for sins forever and sat down at the right hand of God .

今天，我们去到神面前，还要献上赎罪祭吗？不用。为什么？希伯来书第 10 章指出：当耶稣基督把他自己当作祭物一次献上，就使我们永远完全，因此，我们不用再献上任何的祭物，就可以去到神面前。基督是祭物，那把他当作祭物献上的祭司是谁？希伯来书告诉我们，他既是祭物，也是大祭司，他代表我们把他自己献上。

Do we still need to offer a sin offering when we approach God today? No. Why? Hebrews 10 states : When Jesus Christ offered himself once for all as a sacrifice, he perfected us forever. Therefore, we no longer need to offer any sacrifices to approach God. Christ is the sacrifice, but who is the priest who offers him as a sacrifice ? Hebrews tells us that he is both the sacrifice and the High Priest, offering himself on our behalf.

我们在祷告结束的时候，都会说奉耶稣的名祈祷。奉耶稣的名祈祷的一个主要意思是，向自己、会众、灵界宣告：除了耶稣以外，别无其他倚靠，可以让我们直接向父神献上祷告；耶稣是神与人之间唯一的中间人。

At the end of our prayers, we always say "in the name of Jesus." One of the main meanings of praying in the name of Jesus is to declare to ourselves, the congregation, and the spiritual world that there is no other person to whom we can rely directly except Jesus , who is the only intermediary between God and humanity.

我们都是有君尊的祭司  We are all royal priests

彼前 2:9 唯有你们是被拣选的族类，是有君尊的祭司，是圣洁的国度，是属神的子民，要叫你们宣扬那召你们出黑暗入奇妙光明者的美德。

1 Peter 2:9 But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for his own possession, that you may proclaim the praises of him who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light.

今天，在新约里，我们都成了祭司，不是普通的祭司，乃是有君尊的祭司，乃是主耶稣基督这位大君王的祭司，我们在人面前代表他。如何代表他？我们要宣扬这位召我们出黑暗入光明者的美德。

Today, in the New Testament, we have all become priests—not just ordinary priests, but royal priests, priests of the great King, the Lord Jesus Christ. We represent Him before men. How do we represent Him? We proclaim the virtues of the One who called us out of darkness into light.

·        用口宣扬：传福音、作见证；

·         Proclaim with your mouth: preach the gospel and bear witness;

·        用我们的生活见证宣扬，从世俗错谬的价值观、人生观分别出来。

·         Use our lives to testify and spread the word, separating ourselves from the world’s erroneous values and outlook on life.

大祭司的冠冕上有一块金牌，上面刻着六个字：归耶和华为圣(參出 28:36， 39:30)。大家不妨想象一下：若你我的前额上，都纹了这六个字，你在家庭、工作间、社区、教会的行事为人会否要有所改变？为什么？

The high priest's crown contained a gold plate inscribed with the words: "Holy to the Lord" ( see Exodus 28:36 , 39:30 ). Imagine this: If you and I had these six words tattooed on our foreheads, would our behavior at home, at work, in our community, and at church change? Why?

在南方一个城市，有一位弟兄，他的学历很低，小学还没有毕业就去打工，最终成为一个搭棚工人。地盘工人大都粗言秽语，爱赌博，可是，弟兄为人端正，并且很关心同事的安全。每年的鲁班诞，全城的各大小地盘的搭棚工人都会以烧猪来拜祭鲁班。可是，有一年，他的老板没有遵照传统，举行任何拜祭仪式，却请这位弟兄为食物祝谢。弟兄当下感到很愕然，因为他只是一个小管工而已。然而，地盘上上下下的人都知道耶稣在他的身上大有能力。为什么他们会知道？他把自己归神为圣，分别出来，被主使用，在地盘里活出了基督的形像。

In a southern city, there was a brother with a low level of education. He started working as a laborer before even graduating from elementary school, eventually becoming a scaffolding worker. While most construction workers were foul-mouthed and fond of gambling, this brother was a decent person and deeply concerned about the safety of his colleagues. Every year on Lu Ban's birthday, scaffolding workers from construction sites across the city would offer roasted pigs in honor of Lu Ban. However, one year, his boss, rather than following the traditional rituals, asked this brother to bless the food. The brother was stunned, as he was only a small foreman. However, everyone on the construction site knew that Jesus had great power in him. How did they know this? He had sanctified himself to God, set himself apart to be used by the Lord, and lived out the image of Christ on the construction site.

It is noteworthy that their visit coincided with the first ripening of grapes, in the seventh month, allowing them to immediately witness the truth of God's promise and the abundance of His provision. They described the land as flowing with milk and honey ( see Numbers 13:27 ) and mentioned the various Canaanite tribes they encountered. Clearly, what they saw was consistent with God's promise and what He had spoken of all along. Yet, their report made no mention of God or His promises. Why? The next step will provide the answer.

解释：  explain:

然而，在整个报告里，十个探子并没有提到神，连一次都没有；他们没有跟大家说：“神所说的都是真的，那地的确是流奶如蜜之地，这挂葡萄就是证据；我们也看见神预先所告诉我们的迦南族人。”他们作出的结论是：不能上去攻击当地的人，因为那地的人身材高大；相比之下，以色列人如同蚱蜢；并且他们认为当地的人亦觉得他们是蚱蜢。

However, in their entire report, the ten spies did not mention God even once. They did not tell everyone, "What God said is true. The land is indeed a land flowing with milk and honey, and these bunches of grapes are the proof. We also saw the Canaanites that God had told us about in advance." They concluded that they could not attack the local people because the people there were tall. In comparison, the Israelites were like grasshoppers. And they believed that the local people also thought they were grasshoppers.

 

关于那十个探子带回来的报告，请留意下列三点：  Note the following three points about the report brought back by the ten spies:

 他们被恐惧主宰：他们所说的话如“吞吃居民之地”、“伟人”(巨人) 、据我们看自己就如蚱蜢一样，据他们看我们也是如此”，反映出他们已经被恐惧支配。神是他们的王、他们的主，可是，现在却是恐惧成为了他们的王、他们的主。

 They are dominated by fear: their words, such as " the land that devours its inhabitants , " " the giants , " and "we are like grasshoppers in our own eyes , and so are we in their eyes, " reflect that they are controlled by fear. God is their King and Lord, but now fear has become their King and Lord.

 他们夸大了困难：他们认为在迦南人眼中，他们只不过是蚱蜢。然而，他们根本未曾跟迦南人打过交道，又怎会知道在迦南人眼中，他们只不过是蚱蜢？那些迦南人真的会吞吃他们吗？纵然他们身材高大，然而，他们真的都是巨人吗？他们夸大了困难，作出失实的报告。

 They exaggerated their difficulties: They thought the Canaanites saw them as mere grasshoppers. However, they had never interacted with the Canaanites, so how could they know that they were mere grasshoppers? Would the Canaanites really devour them? Even though they were tall, were they really giants? They exaggerated their difficulties and misrepresented their situation.

 他们否定神的能力、同在和信实：他们承认那是流奶与蜜之地，并且有一挂葡萄作为证据，然而，在他们的报告当中，却没有提到神。为什么？他们因为惧怕而不相信神能使他们胜过这些迦南人，否定神的能力、同在和信实。因此，在报告中，连神也不提了。

   They denied God's power, presence, and faithfulness. They acknowledged the land flowing with milk and honey, and even a cluster of grapes served as evidence. Yet, they made no mention of God in their reports. Why? Because they feared God's ability to defeat the Canaanites, denying His power, presence, and faithfulness. Therefore, they omitted even a mention of God in their reports.

当我们面对经济、健康或家人方面的一些重大改变时，我们心中难免会有恐惧和不安。然而，我们要谨记：对神来说，这些生活上的困难，没有一个是太大的，是他不能背负和应付的。更重要的是，我们的神常用他权能的命令托住万有，使万事都互相效力，叫我们得益处；并且没有任何难处，不管是现在的，或是将来的，可以使我们与基督的爱隔绝。

When we face major changes in our finances, health, or family, fear and anxiety are inevitable. However, we must remember that none of life's challenges are too great for God to bear and handle. More importantly, our God upholds all things by His sovereign word, enabling all things to work together for our good. No difficulty, present or future, can separate us from the love of Christ.

Year: BC 1450 Year   Chinese History: Shang Dynasty

 根据民数记 14 章 6 至 9 节，约书亚和迦勒怎样说那地？他们怎样说神？他们说以色列人不应该做什么？为什么？

   According to Numbers 14: 6-9 , what did Joshua and Caleb say about the land? What did they say about God? What did they say the Israelites should not do? Why?

 

解释：  explain:

十个探子被恐惧主宰，报恶信，认为不可以去攻取那地，然而，另外两个探子约书亚和迦勒却持相反的看法。他们的报告和结论是：

The ten spies, driven by fear, reported a bad news, believing that the land should not be conquered. However, the other two spies, Joshua and Caleb, held a different view. Their report and conclusion were:

 那是极美之地，真的是流奶与蜜之地；神所说的是真的；

   It is a very beautiful land, truly a land flowing with milk and honey; what God says is true;

 他们不是靠自己进去；只要讨神的喜悦，听从神的吩咐，神必定领他们进去，把那地赐给他们；

   They could not enter by themselves; as long as they pleased God and obeyed His command, God would surely lead them in and give them the land.

 不上去，就是背叛神

   If you don't go up, you are betraying God.

 不要怕那地的居民，因耶和华与他们同在。

   Do not be afraid of the inhabitants of the land, for the LORD is with them.

十个探子的焦点是当地的人；约书亚和迦勒两人的焦点却是耶和华和他的同在。为什么他们两人会有如此的反应？他们所见的，跟十个探子所见的一样，但结论却很不一样。为什么？神认为他们二人专心跟从他。在原文，“专心”这个词在创世记第一章曾经出现过，一次被翻成“遍满”，另一次被翻成“充满”。他们两人的内心充满了和遍满了一件事，只有一件事：跟从神；除此以外，没有其他的意念。

The ten spies focused on the local people; Joshua and Caleb focused on Jehovah and His presence. Why did they react this way? They saw the same things as the ten spies, but their conclusions were very different . Why? God considered them to be wholeheartedly following Him. The word " wholehearted " appears once in Genesis 1, translated once as " full " and another time as " filled . " Their hearts were filled and overflowing with one thing and one thing only: following God. They had no other thoughts.

当我们遇到困难或问题时，我们也要这样。我们是以自己的不足和限制来衡量困难，还是以神无限的拯救大能来衡量困难呢？大卫见到巨人歌利亚时，就是用了后者的观点（参撒上 17:20-26、31-37）。

We should do the same when we encounter difficulties or problems. Do we measure them against the backdrop of our own shortcomings and limitations, or against the backdrop of God's infinite power to save us? David used the latter perspective when he encountered the giant Goliath ( see 1 Samuel 17:20-26 , 31-37 ) .

大部分人都以眼目去判断，然后作出行动，约书亚和迦勒却是凭着信心而行；大部分人只看见障碍，约书亚和迦勒却是看到机会；大部分人只见到问题，约书亚和迦勒却是望见远景；百姓只关心自己的安全，约书亚和迦勒却是关心神的荣耀和应许。你是在哪一个阵营中？你希望属于哪一个阵营？

Most people judge and act based on their sight, but Joshua and Caleb acted by faith. Most see only obstacles, but Joshua and Caleb saw opportunities. Most see only problems, but Joshua and Caleb saw the vision. The people cared only about their own safety, but Joshua and Caleb cared about God's glory and promises. Which camp are you in? Which camp do you want to belong to?

今天你和我内心充满了什么？但愿我们内心充满一个心志，也只有一个志，就是不管发生什么事情，都要跟从神到底。还是我们三心两意？若是这样，我们的生活就没法进到更丰富的地步，如同那些不专心跟从神的以色列人。

What fills your heart and mine today? May our hearts be filled with one resolve, and one resolve only: to follow God to the end, no matter what happens. Or are we divided? If so, our lives will be unable to reach a richer state, like those Israelites who were not wholehearted in following God.

The report of the ten spies frightened the Israelites. Their unbelief spread like fire. They questioned and complained about why God had led them here to kill them and cause their wives and children to be taken captive by the Canaanites. They all said they did not want to go in, but wanted to return to Egypt because:

 他们不相信神的应许：虽然他们知道那是流奶与蜜之地，可是，他们不想信神的应许，不认为神会把那地赐给他们。

   They did not believe in God’s promise: Although they knew that it was a land flowing with milk and honey, they did not want to believe in God’s promise and did not think that God would give them that land.

   They didn't know God's lovingkindness. If God really wanted them dead, He would have sent a plague, and they would have all died. If He wanted to harm them, He wouldn't have led them out of Egypt or provided them with food and water every day.

他们要推翻摩西和耶和华的带领，要走回头路。他们的怨言跟过去的没有什么分别。之前，他们发怨言，很怀念埃及的食物。虽然以色列人见过神在埃及和旷野所行的神迹，但从出埃及开始，以色列多次向神发怨言，连同这一次，就是第十次了。纵然他们在旷野这样的地方天天经历神迹，他们还是不断发怨言，现在根本不相信神会实现他的应许，甚至表示宁愿死在那个旷野，也不要进入应许之地。因此，神的审判临到他们，这六十万大军，除了约书亚和迦勒外，所有人都要死在旷野。

They were about to overturn Moses and Jehovah's guidance and return to their former ways. Their complaints were no different from before. Previously, they had complained and yearned for the food of Egypt. Although the Israelites had witnessed God's miracles in Egypt and the wilderness, they had complained against Him many times since the Exodus, and this was the tenth time. Even though they experienced miracles daily in the wilderness, they continued to complain. 那一代的以色列人已经去到迦南地的边缘，但最终却因为不信而享受不到迦南地的丰富，并且继续在旷野过着漂流的日子，最终死在旷野。希伯来书的作者认为这些不信的以色列人的遭遇是我们的鉴戒，我们不要重蹈他们的覆辙。请想想：你希望你的人生有一个怎样的终结？我们不要像他们那样，来到迦南美地的边界，却功亏一篑，最终漂流，死在旷野；相反，我们要切切保守我们的心，像约书亚和迦勒那样，一生专心跟从神，走完我们人生的路。

That generation of Israelites had reached the edge of Canaan , but ultimately, due to their unbelief, they were unable to enjoy its bounty and continued to wander in the wilderness, eventually perishing there. The author of Hebrews considers the fate of these unbelieving Israelites a warning for us, so we should not repeat their mistakes. Consider: How do you want your life to end? We don't want to be like them, reaching the border of Canaan only to fall short, ultimately wandering and dying in the wilderness. Instead, we should guard our hearts diligently, like Joshua and Caleb , wholeheartedly following God throughout our lives, and completing our lives.
