如何学习（和教导）旧约历史 100 步

How to Study (and Teach) Old Testament History 100 step

旧约历史 100 步是学习旧约历史进程的有效方

100 Steps to Old Testament History is an effective way to learn the historical process of the Old Testament.

法。下面的建议能帮助你在学习 100 步和教导

The following suggestions can help you learn 100 steps and teach

100 步时，得到最大的果效。

The maximum effect is achieved at 100 steps.

 

壹. 课堂上要注意的事情

1. Things to pay attention to in class

甲. 上课时，你要集中学习每一步。

A. During class, you should focus on learning each step.

不要期望你能记住所有的事实资料。只需在导师作灵修分享时，让神亲自对你说话就行了。请记着，圣经的教导是全人性的（“知道”、“活道”和“行道”），而最重要的是“活道”。神会对你说有关你生命的一些事情，请小心聆听在你心中的微小声音。

Don't expect to memorize all the facts. Simply allow God to speak to you during your mentor's spiritual sharing. Remember, the Bible's teachings are holistic ("knowing the Word," "living the Word," and "doing the Word"), but the most important aspect is "living the Word." God will speak to you about your life. Listen carefully to the still, small voice within you.

乙. 你要积极、用心地学习 100 步，务求把它学好。

Second . You must study actively and attentively 100 Step by step, make sure to learn it well.

1.         背诵每一步的步名──即要“说”的部分，明白当中每个词句的意思。同时，用一句话写出每一步所代表的意思，就像你要向别人讲解一样。

1.          Memorize the name of each step — the " talking " part—and understand the meaning of each word. Also, write out the meaning of each step in a sentence, as if you were explaining it to someone else.

2.         细心留意每一步的动作，务求做到跟导师所教的一样。你绝对不能掉以轻心，因为如果你在理解上有一点点模糊，你所教的下一代就会更感困惑难明。你要非常清楚每一步的动作，要将动作做得夸张一点。也许初时你会感到有点不习惯，但即使是这样，你也要坚持下去，务求把动作做得清楚明白。对你的学员来说，你清楚的动作不会显得太夸张。只要你继续练习，你的动作会做得越来越自然，你也会越来越有信心。

2.          Pay close attention to each step, ensuring it's performed exactly as your instructor instructed. Never let your guard down, as even the slightest ambiguity in your understanding will only confuse the next generation you're teaching. Be absolutely clear about each step, and exaggerate your movements. It may feel a little awkward at first, but persist, ensuring your movements are clear and understandable. Your clear movements won't seem exaggerated to your students. With continued practice, your movements will become more and more natural, and your confidence will grow.

3.         细心留意导师在“地图”中所指出的不同地方和山川。找一张中东的地图，看看这些地方在哪里。把导师在教室所作的“地图”跟一般的地图作比较。

3.          Pay close attention to the different places and mountains indicated on the “ map ” your instructor creates. Find a map of the Middle East and identify these places. Compare the “ map ” your instructor created in class with a standard map.

4.         进行温习时，尝试不要看笔记；要尝试一边望着导师，一边背诵资料。

4.          When reviewing, try not to look at your notes; instead, try to recite the material while looking at your instructor.

5.         要清楚明白这 100 步。你需要找一个伙伴，在你们有空时一起温习这 100步。你可以在饭后、黄昏或早晨培训开始之前，找一些人和你一起温习这 100 步，直至你能够掌握，不用停下来想也能说出这 100 步为止。

5.          To clearly understand these 100 steps, you need to find a partner to review these 100 steps with you when you have free time. You can find someone to review these 100 steps with you after dinner, in the evening, or before the morning training, until you can master them and can recite these 100 steps without stopping to think .

 

贰. 课程完结后要注意的事情

2. Things to note after the course

你需要加深自己对这 100 步的认识，以便能够教导别人。你需要深入了解这 100 步的各个部分，包括动作、步名和解释这三个部分。我们给你的材料应该能帮助你。然而，当你向别人讲解或作灵修分享时，圣经才是你寻找资料的首要来源。

You need to deepen your understanding of these 100 steps in order to be able to teach them to others. You need to have a thorough understanding of each component of the 100 steps, including the movements, step names, and explanations. The materials we have provided should help you. However, when you are explaining these steps to others or sharing them in devotionals, the Bible should be your primary source of information.

甲. 动作和步名

First . Action and step names

1.         不断温习这 100 步，直至你能够完全掌握为止。要教得好，你必须对这 100

1.          Keep reviewing these 100 steps until you can fully master them. To teach well , you must

步非常熟识，这 100 步要变成你生命的一部分。

These 100 steps should become part of your life.

在家里的时候，不管有没有伙伴跟你一起，也请经常温习这些动作。假如你是自己一个人温习，你需要一个房间；想象自己就是培训课程的导师，然后把 100 步做出来，边做边说步名，就像前面有人在听你讲一样。

Practice these exercises regularly at home, whether or not you're with a partner. If you're practicing alone, you'll need a room. Imagine you're the instructor and practice the 100 steps, saying the names as you go, as if someone is listening.

练习是很重要的。你也不希望把 100 步忘掉了，然后等到要预备教别人时，才匆匆看看笔记簿的笔记吧！若是这样，你必会感到困扰，因为这样做的果效不大呢！

Practice is important. You don't want to If you forget the first step and then quickly look at your notes in your notebook when you are about to teach others, you will be troubled because it will not be very effective.

我们都用过刀子，这是很重要和很有用的工具。但要刀子锋利，我们必须经常磨它。100 步也是一样，这是很好的教导工具，但我们需要经常磨利它。经常预备好使用 100 步，能帮助你成为无愧的工人。

We all have used knives. They are very important and useful tools. But to keep them sharp, we must sharpen them regularly . The same is true of the steps. They are great teaching tools, but we need to keep them sharp. Keeping the 100 steps ready to use will help you become a worker who is worthy of shame.

2.         好好想想应该如何讲解每一步，好使你的听众能完全清楚明白。也许你需要写下每步的解释；你手上的材料能帮助你讲解每一步。最好能用你自己的话来解释，这样你所教导的人便能明白更多。

2.          Think carefully about how you should explain each step so that your audience can fully understand it. You may need to write down the explanation of each step; your materials can help you explain each step. It is best to explain it in your own words so that those you teach can understand it more clearly.

3.         仔细想想每一步的动作为何是这样。要能够解释动作背后的象征意义和每个动作的重要性。找个伙伴，叫他问你：“你为什么用这个动作代表这一步？”然后你就可以向他解释。

3.          Think carefully about why each step is performed the way it is. Be able to explain the symbolism behind the movements and the importance of each one. Find a partner and have them ask you, " Why did you use this movement to represent this step? " Then you can explain it to them.

4.         要成功地教导和传递这 100 步，关键在于多温习。一个充满智慧的导师曾经讲过：“温习永远不会嫌多。”假如要学唱一首歌，我们不是会唱完又唱，直至完全记得为止吗？因此，你也要用相同的方法去教这 100 步。

4.          The key to successfully teaching and passing on these 100 steps is frequent review. A wise teacher once said, " You can never review too much. " If we were learning a song, wouldn't we sing it over and over again until we memorized it? So, you should use the same approach when teaching these 100 steps.

你要让学员有足够的时间彻底温习这 100 步，这是最为重要的，因为只有这样做，学员才会更熟习这 100 步，从而有信心可以教导他人。平均来说，

It is most important that you allow your students enough time to thoroughly review the 100 steps, because only by doing so will they become more familiar with the 100 steps and have the confidence to teach others.

 

每教 10 步要温习 5 次。温习的方法如下：

10 per lesson Step 5 Review times. The review method is as follows:

(1)     你做动作，让全组学员一起说出步名。

(1)      You do the movements and have the whole group say the step names together.

(2)     让其中一人出来做动作，其他的人说出步名。

(2)      Have one person come out and do the action while the others say the step name.

(3)     让全组学员一起做动作，由你负责说出步名。你可以让他们在你说完一步之后才做一个动作，也可以让他们在你说之前先做动作。

(3)      Have the whole group do the movements together, and you will be in charge of saying the step name. You can have them do the movement after you say the step, or you can have them do the movement before you say it.

(4)     你和他们一起做动作，让他们边做边说。

(4)      You go through the actions with them and have them talk as they do it.

(5)     让全组学员和你一起做动作，但不要出声，只在心中想着要说的话。

(5)      Have the whole group do the movements with you, but don't say anything, just think about what you want to say in your mind.

(6)     把他们分组，2 人一组或 4 人一组，叫他们站起来，让他们在自己的小组里一起说出各步。他们说过两次之后就可以坐下来。

(6)      Divide them into groups of two or four , have them stand up, and have them say the steps together in their group. After they have said them twice, they can sit down.

(7)     把他们分组，２人一组，其中一个向伙伴说出各步（伙伴只需聆听），直至你叫“掉换”，他们就要互换角色，刚才听的那个要说出接下来的各步，刚才说的那个就要聆听。

(7)      Divide them into groups of two, and have one person say the steps to their partner (who just listens), until you say " swap " , then they will switch roles, and the one who just listened will say the next steps, and the one who just said will listen.

乙. 解释

B. Explanation​

1.         在 100 步里，很多步之下都有列出相关的圣经经文（有时是一节经文，有时是几节经文，有时是一卷书）。可以的话，请背诵每步所代表的经文章节。

1.          Under each of the 100 steps, there are corresponding Bible verses (sometimes a single verse, sometimes several verses, sometimes an entire book) listed below each step . If possible, please memorize the verse that represents each step.

(1)     例一：第 33 步“400 年”的相关经文是出 1:6~7、出 1:8~10 和创 15:13。你最好记住这些章节（经文内容无须背诵，但记了也无妨）。

(1)      Example 1: The relevant verses for step 33 , " 400 years, " are Exodus 1:6-7 , Exodus 1:8-10 , and Genesis 15:13 . It's best to memorize these verses (you don't need to memorize the verses, but it doesn't hurt to remember them).

(2)     例二：第 19 步“三部分”没有参考经文，所以这一步你无须背诵章节。

(2)      Example 2: There is no scripture reference in step 19 , “ Part Three ,” so you do not need to memorize the chapters in this step .

2.         练习讲 100 步里的故事，可因应学员的需要作适切的改动。你可能要因应学员的文化和情况，将故事的细节稍为改动。然而你必须注意故事的寓意，有关的改动不可减弱故事的感染力和说服力。

2.          Practice telling the 100- step story, adapting it to the needs of your participants. You may need to slightly change the details of the story to suit the participants' culture and circumstances. However, you must maintain the moral of the story, and any changes should not diminish its appeal or persuasiveness.

3.         在最重要的几步，学习设计自己的表达方式：你可以加入讲道、例子或详尽的讲解。你可以在讲 100 步时随时加入灵修分享，但要尽量简短（约 10分钟）。在讲最重要的几步时，你可以在讲完一步之后加入两三项的分享和至少一次约 10 分钟的灵修分享。那两三项的分享可以包括圣经的资料、细读该步的经文而得的领受，或就世界其他地方的历史而作出的评论。灵修分享则可以根据该步所讲的事件或经文而作出。

3.          In the most important steps, learn to design your own presentation: you can add sermons, examples or detailed explanations . You can add a spiritual sharing at any time during the steps, but keep it short ( about 10 minutes). When you are talking about the most important steps, you can add two or three sharings after each step and at least one spiritual sharing of about 10 minutes. The two or three items shared can include biblical information, insights gained from reading the scriptures in that step, or comments on the history of other parts of the world. The devotional sharing can be based on the events or scriptures discussed in that step.

请注意，你在教 100 步时，不需要将你所预备的资料全都用上。你只要做好准备，圣灵就能感动你去选择何时用什么资料。你也可以按时间的多少，增加或减少你要教的内容。请记着，你手上的材料是一个基础，这个基础有助你设计自己的表达方式。

Please note that you do not need to use all the materials you have prepared when teaching the 100 steps. Simply prepare, and the Holy Spirit will inspire you to choose which materials to use and when. You can also add or subtract content as time permits. Remember that the materials you have are a foundation; this foundation will help you design your own presentation.

下面的例子是其中一步的分享内容，当中的资料大部分都可以在教材里找到。你可以直接使用这些资料，也可以使用自己的材料。

The following example is part of a step-by-step tutorial. Most of the materials can be found in the textbook . You can use them directly or create your own.

例子：在讲到“以扫”的时候，你可以利用三项资料：i) “以扫”这个词解作“有毛”，因为他出生时浑身有毛。ii) 他成了以东人的祖先，而俄巴底亚书所写的就是有关以东人的预言。iii) 现在已经没有以东这个种族了。灵修分享：以扫轻视他的长子名分。很多基督徒就像以扫一样，轻视他们的属灵儿子名分（参创 25:29~34）；你有轻视你的属灵子女的名分吗？

Example: When discussing " Esau , " you can draw on three pieces of information: i ) The word " Esau " means " hairy " because he was born covered in hair. ii ) He became the ancestor of the Edomites, about whom the book of Obadiah contains a prophecy. iii ) The Edomites no longer exist. Devotional: Esau despised his birthright. Many Christians, like Esau, despise their spiritual sonship ( see Genesis 25:29-34 ). Do you despise your spiritual childship?

 

4.         记住导师是怎样用幽默风趣的方式教导你 100 步。用得适当的话，幽默会是一种很有用的工具，能帮助你的学员记得他们所学的。你可以问问自己，你可以在讲课的时候怎样运用幽默呢？以下的一些例子可以在这本手册里找到：

4.          Remember how the mentor taught you in a humorous way step. Used appropriately, humor can be a valuable tool to help your students retain what they have learned. Ask yourself, how can you use humor in your lectures? Some examples can be found in this manual:

(1)     例一：第 36 步“做梦”；“做梦”是一个幽默的语句。你可以讲述摩西怎样向法老提出让以色列人（过百万的“廉价劳工”）离去，然后想象自己就是法老，用坚决的语气讲“做梦”，断然拒绝摩西。

(1)      Example 1: Step 36 , " Dream . " " Dream " is a humorous phrase. You could describe how Moses proposed to Pharaoh that the Israelites (over a million " cheap laborers " ) leave. Then, imagine yourself as Pharaoh, firmly rejecting Moses' proposal by saying " Dream . "

(2)     例二：“三拉一罗”（见《附录六》）和“扫大所”（见《附录七》）的故事都是充满幽默感的。

(2)      Example 2: The stories of “ Three Pullers and One Luo ” (see Appendix 6) and “ Sweeping the Office ” (see Appendix 7) are both full of humor.

(3)     例三：第 21 步“后裔”；你可以做一个二人的幽默短剧，一个人扮作亚伯拉罕，另一个扮作他的朋友。这个朋友知道他的名字从亚伯兰

(3)      Example 3: Step 21 “ Descendants ” : You can do a two-person skit, with one person playing the role of Abraham and the other playing the role of his friend. The friend knows that his name comes from Abram.

 

（荣耀之父）改为亚伯拉罕（多国之父），就恭喜他，又问他他的儿子在哪里。亚伯拉罕那时当然还没有儿子，他可以怎样回应？他既可以教导别人要对神的应许有信心，也可以说一些幽默的话。

He changed the name "Father of Glory" to "Abraham" (Father of Many Nations), congratulated him, and then asked him where his son was. Of course, Abraham didn't have a son at the time, so how could he respond? He could teach others to have faith in God's promises or offer some humorous remarks.

 

丙. 其他要记住的事情

C. Other things to remember

1.         记住，你的学员参与愈多，你叫他们所做的事情愈多，他们愈能记住所学的。

1.          Remember, the more your students participate and the more you ask them to do, the more they will retain what they learn .

2.         记住，你教 100 步的目的，是为了让学员得益处；你的目标是要让学员学

2.          Remember, the purpose of teaching 100 steps is to benefit students; your goal is to enable students to learn

好这 100 步。你在教导时所做的每件事情，所讲的每句话，都是为了要让学员学得更好。请记住，你教导的目的不是要教越多资料越好。你教导的态度和所做的一切，要能帮助学员学习，不要只为教导而教导。

Okay, these 100 steps. Everything you do and say while teaching should be done to help students learn better. Remember, the purpose of teaching isn't to teach as much material as possible. Your attitude and everything you do should help students learn, not just teach for the sake of teaching.

 

叁. 如何教导旧约历史 100 步

How to Teach Old Testament History 100 step

你必须熟习每一步的循环，而每一步的循环都有 3 个部分：

甲. 第一部分：简介这一步

You must be familiar with each step of the cycle, and each step of the cycle has 3 Parts:

A. Part 1: Introduction to this step

1.         也许你需要将这一步和你上次教的那一步连起来。例如在“三个国王”这一步，你需要说明以色列人怎样厌恶灵性上的恶性循环，因而要求撒母耳为他们立一个王。结果是以色列有“三个国王”……

1.          Perhaps you need to connect this step with the one you taught last time. For example, in the " Three Kings " step, you need to explain how the Israelites, disgusted with their spiritual downward spiral, asked Samuel to appoint a king for them. The result was the " Three Kings " of Israel...

2.         也许在这一步你需要加几句简单的说明。例如在“加利利海”这一步，你可以说：“这个弟兄（或姊妹）会代表地图的一部分，这个部分就是加利利海。因此，当我将手这样放在这弟兄（或姊妹）的头上时，大家请一起说：‘加利利海’。”

2.          You may want to add a few simple instructions during this step. For example, for the " Sea of Galilee " step, you could say, " This brother (or sister) will represent a portion of the map, and that portion is the Sea of Galilee. So, as I place my hand on this brother (or sister)'s head like this, everyone will say together, ' Sea of Galilee . ' "

3.        当你做动作时，最好能大声讲解动作的做法。例如“双胞胎”这一步，你可以在做动作的同时说：“当我将两手食指并排，再向两边分开，你们就一起说：‘双胞胎’。两只手指并排代表一个母腹，再将手指分开代表从一个母腹生出两个生命。”有一些动作需要多一些解释，而有些却不需要解释（例如竖起三只手指代表“三部分”这一步）。

3.         As you perform the movements, it's best to explain them out loud. For example, for the " Twins " step, you could say, " As I place my index fingers side by side and spread them apart, you all say ' twins ' together . The two fingers together represent one womb, and the two fingers apart represent two lives being born from one womb. " Some movements require more explanation, while others don't (like holding up three fingers to represent " three parts " ).

乙. 第二部分：解释这一步

B. Part 2 : Explain this step

1.         你可以分享你事先准备好或背熟了的事实资料。

1.          You can share facts that you have prepared or memorized.

2.         假如你有时间，你可以顺着圣灵的带领作灵修分享（一段你为这一步而预备的灵修分享）。不要讲太长，因为这不是讲道。你可以简单地分享你如何应用圣经的真理，也可以分享你从圣经得到的领受。

2.          If you have time, you can follow the Holy Spirit's leading to share a devotional (a devotional you prepared for this step). Don't make it too long, as this isn't a sermon. Simply share how you've applied biblical truths or what you've learned from the Bible.

丙. 第三部分：温习这一步

C. Part 3: Review this step

当解释完一步之后，你可以叫学员温习这一步。你可以叫他们重复温习，直至全组人都能毫无困难地将这步说出来。然后你可以叫他们温习之前的3 至4 步，好让他们能熟习各步的次序。每教完 4 至 5 步，可以温习之前的 20 步左右。在上完一节课之后，与他们一起把你在课堂上所教的从头到尾温习一次。要经常温习，不过不要每次都用同一种方法，免得把他们闷坏了。要用不同的方式温习，让学员即使经常温习，也不觉得自己在经常温习。

After explaining a step, you can ask the students to review it. You can ask them to repeat it until the whole group can say it without difficulty. Then you can ask them to review the previous 3 steps. to 4 Step by step so they can become familiar with the order of the steps. After teaching every 4 or 5 steps, review the previous 20 or so steps. After each lesson, review everything you taught with them from beginning to end. Review frequently, but don't use the same method every time, as this will bore them. Use different methods so that even if students review frequently, they don't feel like they are reviewing frequently.

你可以不断重复这个循环，还可以加上问答比赛、图表、地图等等，使你的表达方式更加活泼。要令人听得兴奋，因为旧约是令人兴奋的；要讲得独特，因为教导神一直以来的计划是很独特的；要令人印象难忘，因为你的学员需要以此装备自己，好奔走前面的路程（参来 12:1~2；罗 15:4）。

You can repeat this cycle over and over, livening up your presentation with quizzes, charts, maps, and so on. Make it exciting to listen to, because the Old Testament is exciting; make it unique, because teaching about God's long-term plan is unique; and make it memorable, because your students need it to equip them for the race ahead ( see Hebrews 12:1-2 ; Romans 15:4 ).
