附录四  Appendix 4

 《旧约一》每周教学程序建议

Suggested Weekly Teaching Procedure for "Old Testament I"

 

这个课程基本上会以两种方式教授：  This course will basically be taught in two ways:

 

      密集式：这种方式是用三、四天的时间，从早到晚集中培训。对那些非常忙碌，又想在很短的时间内得到培训的工人来说，这种方式对他们更为有利。在这种培训中，最重要的圣经原则是“传递性”，所有受训者都有责任将他们所学得的真理与知识，传给他们的同工。因此，我们建议那些接受密集式培训的人，回到他们事奉的地方后，要开始长期的同工培训工作。
      Intensive training : This method involves three or four days of intensive training, from morning to evening. This method is particularly beneficial for workers who are very busy but want to receive training in a short period of time. The most important biblical principle in this type of training is " transferability . " All trainees have the responsibility to pass on the truth and knowledge they have learned to their fellow workers. Therefore, we recommend that those who receive intensive training begin long-term co-worker training upon returning to their place of ministry.

 

      每周式：对那些接受了密集式培训，回到他们事奉的地方的人来说，这是一种最实际可行的培训方式。他们可以用这种方式培训其他同工。下面建议的是一个为期 12 周、每周研读一课的教学程序。每节课大约要用两小时完成。
      Weekly : This is the most practical training method for those who have completed intensive training and returned to their ministry. They can then use this method to train other staff members. The following is a suggested teaching schedule for a 12- week period, with one lesson per week. Each lesson should take approximately two hours to complete.

注意事项:

Notes :

这是一套系统性的培训教材。  This is a set of systematic training materials.

课程的设计是，新的课程是根据前一个课程而设立的。

The courses are designed so that new courses are built upon the previous ones .

举例说，  For example,

《旧约精览（一）》和《旧约精览（二）》是《旧约精览》第三至第六部分的基础；

"Old Testament Intensive (I )" and "Old Testament Intensive (II )" are the foundation of Parts 3 to 6 of "Old Testament Intensive" ;

《旧约精览（一）》所教的历史进程和《旧约精览（二）》的专题会在《旧约精览（三）》中引用。

The historical process taught in "Old Testament Intensive (I )" and the special topics in "Old Testament Intensive (II)" will be cited in " Old Testament Intensive (III )".

因此，例如研读《旧约精览（三）》之前，学员必须先以密集的方式或每周学一课的方式修读《旧约精览》第一和第二部分，打好基础。

Therefore, for example, before studying "Old Testament Intensive Study (Part 3 ), students must first study the first and second parts of "Old Testament Intensive Study" in an intensive manner or by taking one lesson per week to lay a solid foundation.
