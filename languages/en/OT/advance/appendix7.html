三个王  Three Kings

引言  introduction

你可以跟学员讲下面的故事，借以鼓励他们无论做什么事，都要全心全意为神而做。这个故事也可以帮助学员记住以色列联合王国的三个王的名字。

You could share the following story with students to encourage them to devote themselves wholeheartedly to God in everything they do. This story can also help them remember the names of the three kings of the United Kingdom of Israel.

故事  story

 

从前在中国有一个非常繁荣和文明的城市，那里甚至有聘用工人清洁公厕。那里的市长能这样成功，是因为他有方法请到全心全

Once upon a time, there was a very prosperous and civilized city in China, where they even hired workers to clean public toilets. The mayor of that city was able to do this because he had a way of hiring people who were wholeheartedly working for him.

意工作的人。他是如何能够找到那些忠心的人，即使老板不在，仍尽心工作的人呢？这市长实在是个精明的人，他懂得如何分辨谁是好工人。

How does he find those loyal people who will work even when the boss is away? The mayor is really a smart man. He knows how to identify who is a good worker.

 

有一天，三个人来申请市政府一个高级职位。他们的资格都很好，也很有经验，但谁是最佳人选呢？那市长带他们从市政大楼走到街上，然后走进一个公厕。那里很脏、很臭、很令人讨厌。那是一个很大的公厕，一个人的力量实在很难把它清洁干净。

One day, three people applied for a high-ranking position in city government. They were all well-qualified and experienced, but who was the best candidate? The mayor led them from the city hall building to the street and into a public restroom. It was filthy, smelly, and disgusting. It was a large public restroom, and it was difficult for one person to clean it.

那市长给他们每人一桶水、一把扫帚、一个地拖和一些清洁剂，然后他说：“你们三个清洁这公厕一个星期。之后我会回来检查你们的工作；下星期见！”他走了，那三个人却呆了一会。“清洁这公厕一个星期？我是来申请政府的工作，不是来洗厕所的呀！”但他们别无选择；一声叹气之后，就拾起他们的工具。

The mayor gave each of them a bucket of water, a broom, a mop, and some cleaning fluid. Then he said, "You three will clean this public toilet for a week. I'll come back afterward to check on your work. See you next week!" He left, but the three men paused. "Cleaning this public toilet for a week? I'm applying for a government job, not to clean toilets!" But they had no choice; with a sigh, they gathered up their tools.

 

第一个人一开始便拒绝工作。他惊奇地目送他的上司离去，然后笑了一笑，就把他的工具放在一旁，什么都不做，只顾看报纸。除非他认为上司可能会到来，他才赶忙扫地、抹地；但只要上司一走，他便会继续看报纸。他每天迟到早退，他的午膳时间也很长。可是，他仍不断抱怨。他可以说是一个无心无意工作的人。

The first person refused to work from the start. He watched his boss leave in amazement, then smiled and put his tools aside, doing nothing but reading the newspaper. He swept and mopped the floor only when he thought his boss might arrive. But as soon as his boss left, he would return to reading. He was always late and left early, and his lunch breaks were long. Yet, he continued to complain. He could be described as someone who worked without a heart or a desire.

第二个人却刚刚相反。他定一定神，便马上开始辛勤工作；他每天准时上班，依时下班。他为人诚实有礼，做事热心勤奋。他会想尽办法好好利用他手上的清洁剂和他的时间。他可以说是一个全心全意工作的人。

The second person, on the other hand, was the complete opposite. He gathered his focus and immediately began to work diligently. He arrived on time and left on time every day. He was honest, courteous, and diligent. He sought ways to make the best use of his cleaning products and his time. He could be described as someone who worked wholeheartedly.

第三个人最初的时候也像第二个人一样，他在头两天辛勤工作。但他看着第一个人看报纸，听着他抱怨的声音，慢慢他发觉只有第二个人在做着所有的工作。然而，当上司到来时，他只会看到厕所清洁了，而第一个人也会因此而受赞赏。当他想到这里，他便将工作越拖越慢，终于他也像第一个人一样懒散。他最初像第二个人，最后却像第一个人。他可以说是一个半心半意工作的人。

The third person started out just like the second person. He worked diligently for the first two days. But as he watched the first person read the newspaper and heard him complain, he gradually realized that only the second person was doing all the work. However, when his boss arrived, he would only see the toilet cleaned, and the first person would be praised for it. With this thought, he began to work slower and slower, until he became as lazy as the first person. He started out like the second person, but ended up like the first. He could be said to be a person who works half-heartedly.

 

一个星期后，市长来了。当他进门的时候，三个人都在努力工作。他在这还算清洁的厕所四处张望（记着只有一个人在工作）。他对他们微笑，然后要他们拿着工具排成一行。他拿了一包药粉出来，然后将药粉放一点在他们的桶内；每个人桶内的水都变了不同的颜色。这时第一个人和第三个人的脸色都变得苍白了，并且目瞪囗呆。因为他们每个人桶内的水都变了不同的颜色：第一个变了蓝色；第二个变了红色，第三个变了黄色。

A week later, the mayor arrived. When he entered, the three men were hard at work. He looked around the relatively clean restroom (remember, only one man was working). He smiled at them and asked them to line up with their tools. He took out a packet of powder and placed a little in each of their buckets. The water in each bucket changed to a different color. The first and third men turned pale and were stunned. The water in each of their buckets had changed to a different color: the first to blue, the second to red, and the third to yellow.

 

然后那市长像农夫撒种一样，把药粉四处撒。他把药粉撒在每处地方，包括固定的设施、墙壁、地面等等。药粉撒到的每处地方都变了颜色；你想这公厕会变了什么颜色呢？(原来市长给各人的清洁剂都是不同的。)就是这样，各人的工作都显露出来了。市长对第一个人说：“不要再来这里申请工作了！”然后把他赶走了。他对第二个人说：“你是我们要找的人，我们接受你的申请，你今天可以开始上班了！”市长望着第三个人，对他说：“开始的时候你是有心的，但你被身边的事物引诱，结果走歪了。你不宜在政府担任领导的工作，但你可以在这人手下担当职位较低的工作。

Then the mayor scattered the powder like a farmer sowing seeds. He sprinkled it everywhere, including fixed facilities, walls, floors, and so on. Everywhere the powder touched changed color; what color do you think the public toilet changed to? (It turned out that the mayor gave each person a different detergent.) In this way, everyone's work was revealed. The mayor said to the first person, "Don't apply for a job here again!" and sent him away. He said to the second person, "You are the one we are looking for. We accept your application, and you can start work today!" The mayor looked at the third person and said to him, "You had good intentions at the beginning, but you were tempted by the things around you and ended up going astray. You are not suitable for a leadership position in the government, but you can take a lower-level position under this person.

 

教训  lesson

这个故事的教训正好叫我们反省：我们的工作态度是怎样的呢？我们是辛勤还是懒散？是准时上班还是经常迟到？是对人有礼还是态度恶劣？是竭力工作还是只做最低要求？你有服事的心还是要人服事？你是经常鼓励人还是经常抱怨？你是否须要别人监督着才会努力工作？哥林多前书 10 章 31 节说：“所以你们或吃或喝，无论作什么，都要为荣耀神而行。”无论你是在农田还是在工厂里工作，你在工作的时候，要让神成为你的老板（弗 6:7~8；西 3:23~24）。假如你是为神工作的人，你必须像保罗一样全心全意事奉他（罗 1:9）。

The lesson of this story is just right for us to reflect: What is our attitude towards work? Are we hardworking or lazy? Do we arrive at work on time or are we often late? Are we polite to others or do we have a bad attitude? Do we work hard or do we only do the minimum required? Do you have a heart to serve or do you want to be served? Do you often encourage others or do you often complain? Do you need others to supervise you to work hard? 1 Corinthians 10:31 says : " So whether you eat or drink or whatever you do, do all to the glory of God . " Whether you work in the fields or in a factory, when you work , let God be your boss (Ephesians 6:7-8 ; Colossians 3:23-24 . If you are someone who works for God, you must serve Him wholeheartedly like Paul did ( Romans 1 : 9 .

最后……

at last ……

这个故事如何帮助我们记得以色列三个王的名字呢？请记着他们每个名字的头一个字，这三个字合起来就是这个故事的名称：

How does this story help us remember the names of the three kings of Israel? Remember the first letter of each of their names; these three letters together form the title of this story:

扫罗的“扫” ＋ 大卫的“大” ＋ 所罗门的“所” ＝ “扫大所”（扫地的扫，大小的小，厕所的所）

Saul's " Sao " + David's " Dah " + Solomon's " Solomon " = " Sweep the floor " (sweep the floor, small and big, toilet)

以色列的三个王就像故事中的三个主角。第一个王扫罗就像第一个人，“无心无意”地事奉神。第二个王大卫就像第二个人，“全心全意”地事奉神。第三个王所罗门就像第三个人，“半心半意”地事奉神。

The three kings of Israel are like the three protagonists in this story. The first king, Saul, is like the first man , serving God " half- heartedly ." The second king, David, is like the second man , serving God " wholeheartedly ." The third king, Solomon, is like the third man, serving God " half-heartedly . "
