就像把一块石头抛到静止的水塘里后，所产生的圆形波纹会越来越大，甚至于伸展到水塘的边缘那样，神对福音的传递计划是：要从耶路撒冷开始延伸到犹太全地、撒玛利亚，直到地极。这个地区性的、从小范围到大范围的扩展原则，也能用于传递这个课程的计划上。做传递计划时不单要想到四代，也要考虑到地区。愿神帮助你有好的传递计划，使这门课被使用的范围越来越广。

2        消化步骤

2         Digestion steps

“消化”是从接受这本教材后，到传递这本教材之间的一个不可缺少的过程。我们不想你们盲目地、公式化地传递下去。如果要使这本教材传得更有力、更能养育下一代，首先要使这本教材和自己的生命联系起来。这就如母亲哺育下一代一样；她先把吃进去的食物消化，然后乳养自己的孩子（参帖前 2:7）。

Digestion is an essential process between receiving this material and passing it on. We don't want you to pass it on blindly or formulaically. If you want this material to be passed on with greater power and to better nourish the next generation, you must first connect it with your own life. This is like a mother nursing her children: she first digests the food she eats, then nurses her children ( see 1 Thessalonians 2 : 7 ).

以下我们简单介绍五个步骤：  Here are five steps:

 

一.回应祷告

1. Answered Prayer

首先要把神的话，用信心跟自己的生命调和（参来 4:2）。正如马利亚从天使口中接受神的吩咐后，她首先向神祈祷说：“我是主的使女，情愿照你的话成就在我身上。”（路 1:38）

First, we must reconcile God's Word with our lives through faith ( see Hebrews 4:2 ). Just as Mary, after receiving God's command from the angel , first prayed to God, " I am the Lord's servant; let it be done to me according to your word " ( Luke 1:38 ) .

 

二.重复阅读

2. Repeated reading

这是很重要的一步。盼望各位从接受这本教材的当天开始，便重复阅读。因为越读便越有亮光，越读就越明白。这样，你所学的就能温故知新地落实于生活中并顺利传递。

This is a very important step. I hope you will read this teaching material repeatedly from the day you receive it. The more you read, the more enlightenment you gain, and the more you understand. In this way, you can apply what you have learned in your life, applying it in a new way and passing it on smoothly.

 

三.反复思想

3. Repeated thinking

这是一个核心的步骤，也是可以直接和自己的生命联系的一步。我们要像大卫一样，“昼夜思想”神的律法（诗 1:2）。“在床上的时候，要心里思想，并要肃静”

This is a core step, one that can be directly connected to our own lives. Like David, we should meditate on God's law " day and night " ( Psalm 1:2 ) . " On your bed, meditate on it in your heart, and be still ."

（诗 4:4）。诗篇有很多地方都讲到要思想神的话，其实思想也就是揣摩，揣摩得多便会有所得（参徒 17:27）。

( Psalm 4:4 ). The Psalms often talk about meditating on God’s words. In fact, to meditate is to ponder over them. The more you ponder over them, the more you will gain ( see Acts 17:27 ).

 

四.调整（个人化）

4. Adjustment ( Personalization )

阅读这本教材时，遇到重点的地方，该画线的画线、该做记号的做记号、该做笔记的作笔记。要注明哪些地方要加重语气、哪些地方应加以解释。以上这些都是要详细调整、察看的地方。正如雅各书 1 章 25 节说，“唯有详细察看那全备、使人自由之律法的，并且时常如此”，才不会“听了就忘，乃是实在行出来”。

When reading this textbook, underline, mark, and take notes when you encounter key points. Note where emphasis should be placed and where explanation is needed. These are areas that require detailed review and examination. As James 1:25 says, "But those who look into the perfect law that gives freedom and continue in it" will not be "forgetful hearers " but " doers of the gospel who have heard it and now forget it " (John 1:25 ) .

 

🖂.补充

🖂.Supplement​

各位不要误会，以为“纯正话语的规模”（提后 1:13）需要补充。我们要说的是，单是依着教材读给下一代听，有时会枯燥乏味，所以要加上自己的经历、比喻、或故事。我们的主耶稣在讲道时，不是也常常用比喻吗？（参太 13:1~58，25:1~45）

Please do not misunderstand that the " form of sound words " ( 2 Timothy 1:13 ) needs to be supplemented. What we are saying is that simply reading to the next generation based on the textbook can sometimes be boring, so we should add our own experiences, parables, or stories . Didn't our Lord Jesus often use parables in His sermons? ( See Matthew 13 : 1-58 ; 25 : 1-45 )

学习了以上的五个步骤，会更彻底地帮助你消化这本教材。盼望各位借着学习和消化，按着你的信心，把这本教材的内容传递给下一代，造就更多蒙神喜悦的无愧工人。

Learning the five steps above will help you digest this material more thoroughly. I hope that through your study and digestion, you will, according to your faith, pass on the contents of this material to the next generation, cultivating more workers who are worthy of God and have no shame .
