  Appendix 6 

三拉一罗  Three pull one Luo

 

引言  introduction

圣经中提到有四个人离开吾珥城：亚伯拉罕、他的父亲他拉、他的妻子撒拉和他的侄儿罗得。下面是一个有关团结合作的故事，这个故事也可以帮助我们记住这四个人的名字：

The Bible mentions four men who left Ur: Abraham, his father Terah, his wife Sarah, and his nephew Lot. Here's a story about unity and cooperation that can also help us remember the names of these four men:

故事  story

在一个村庄里，有三个比邻而居的捕鸟人。但一直以来，从来没有人听过他们彼此交谈。没有人知道为何他们如此憎恶对方，也许是某一方的祖先曾经得罪另外两 方的祖先吧！总之，他们之间充满着猜疑、忌妒。假若其中一人有了一副新器具，另外两人第二天必定立即去购买相同的器具。每天他们都会在同一时间走出家门外，集齐他们的罗网和器具，然后默然无声地朝着山上进发。

In a village, three bird catchers lived side by side. Yet, they had never been heard speaking to one another. No one knew why they hated each other so deeply. Perhaps the ancestors of one had offended the ancestors of the others! In short, suspicion and jealousy permeated the land. If one of them bought a new set of tools, the others would immediately buy the same the next day. Every day, they would leave their homes at the same time, gather their nets and tools, and then silently head for the mountains.

有一天，其中一个捕鸟人在铺子里看见一张很漂亮的罗网，这个罗网比他以前见过的都要大和结实。他在确定没有人看见之后便买下那张网，并指明要晚上送货，好使他的邻居不会发现。然而，另外两个捕鸟人都是疑心很重的人，不论日夜，他们都非常留意对方的一举一动，所以他们就发现了那天晚上送货的事，也知道所送的是什么货。不但如此，他们还走到同一间铺子，买了同样的一张网。

One day, one of the birdcatchers saw a beautiful net in a shop—larger and stronger than any he had ever seen. Making sure no one was watching, he bought it and requested a night delivery so his neighbors wouldn't notice. However, the other two birdcatchers were very suspicious, closely watching each other's every move, day and night. So they discovered the delivery and the contents. Furthermore, they went to the same shop and bought the same net.

 

问题来了，原来三个捕鸟人都没有留意包装盒中标明的字句：“三至四人用的网”。一天早上，这几个捕鸟人准备带他们的新网上山，但他们根本搬不动，因为那些网 实在太大了。要靠一个人的力量将网带上山实在不可能！他们试了又试，全都想靠自己一个人的力量把网拉上山。他们都看见彼此的动静。他们最后都筋疲力竭，被 逼放弃，各自坐在一旁休息。

The problem was that the three birdcatchers hadn't noticed the label on the box: "Net for three to four people." One morning, the birdcatchers prepared to carry their new nets up the mountain, but they couldn't lift them. They were so large. Carrying the nets up the mountain on their own was impossible! They tried again and again, each trying to pull the nets up the mountain on their own. They all saw each other's movements. Finally, exhausted, they gave up and sat down to rest.

过了一会，其中一个捕鸟人终于开囗说话了：“听着！我们需要彼此合作。”另外两个捕鸟人都吓了一跳，他竟对他们说话！但他所说的有道理呀！这时他们都知道大家犯了什么错。于是，其中一个捕鸟人慢慢走到另一个人的身边，一起拉同一张网。那张网慢慢地被拉上山。

After a while, one of the birdcatchers finally spoke: "Listen! We need to work together." The other two birdcatchers were startled. He was actually speaking to them! But what he said made sense! They now understood their mistake. Then, one of the birdcatchers slowly approached the other, and together they pulled the same net. Slowly, the net was pulled up the hill.

第三个人也走过来拉，很容易就把网拉上山了。他们知道用了这张网，他们可以捉到的鸟儿要比他们各自用小网捉到的还多。他们越拉便越兴奋，越拉便越感到彼此心中温暖。他们快乐极了，于是喊着：“三个人来拉一个罗网！”从那时开始，他们建立了友谊，也建立了互相合作的工作模式，一直延续到后代。

A third person came along and pulled, easily pulling the net up the hill. They knew they could catch more birds with this net than they could with their own smaller nets. The more they pulled, the more excited they became, and the warmer they felt in each other's hearts. Overjoyed, they shouted, "Three of us, let's pull a net!" From that moment on, they forged a friendship and a pattern of cooperation that has endured to future generations.

 

教训  lesson

作为教会的一分子，我们需要从这个故事吸取教训。约翰福音 13 章 34 至 35 节和 17 章 20 至 21 节告诉我们，世人认识真理有两个方法，一个是借着我们彼此相爱的心，另一个就是借着我们合一的心。假如撒但要攻击作为教会一分子的你，他会特别在这两方面攻击。

As members of the church, we need to learn a lesson from this story. John 13:34-35 and 17:20-21 tell us that there are two ways the world can know the truth: through our love for one another and through our unity. If Satan wants to attack you as a member of the church, he will particularly target these two areas.

他会攻击你们彼此相爱的心，也会攻击你们合一的心。不要给魔鬼欺骗了；你们必须为你们的爱心和合一奋战，就像要得到最贵重的财宝一样（弗 4:1~6）。三个捕鸟人快乐地喊着“三个人来拉一张罗网”，这是一种作战的呼喊声，意思是：“我们要走在一起！我们要合一！我们要合作！”

He will attack your love for one another and your unity. Don't be deceived by the devil; you must fight for your love and unity as if it were the greatest treasure (Ephesians 4:1-6). The three birdcatchers joyfully cried out, "Three men to draw a snare!" This was a battle cry, meaning, "We must walk together! We must unite! We must cooperate!"

 

最后……

at last ……

“三拉一罗”帮助我们记得离开吾珥的四个人的名字。四个人名里面三个有“拉”字，一个有“罗”字，因此便成了“三拉一罗”了。

The phrase " Three Ras and One Lo " helps us remember the names of the four people who left Ur. Three of the four names contain the character " Ra " and one contains the character " Lo ," making it " Three Ras and One Lo . "
