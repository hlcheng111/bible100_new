《旧约精览（一）》大考

A Comprehensive Study of the Old Testament (Part 1)

每个学员都要将旧约历史 100 步的步名全部顺着次序写下来。当他们写完了，他们应当放下笔表示完成了。所有人都必须保持安静，不要和别人讨论答案。

Each student must learn 100 Write down all the steps in order. When they have finished, they should put down their pens to indicate that they are done. Everyone must remain silent and not discuss the answers with others.

当所有人都做完了，或时间到了，每个人都要跟伙伴合作。请在考试前先选好伙伴。

When everyone has finished, or the time is up, everyone will work with a partner. Please choose a partner before the test .

伙伴要互相交换考卷。然后伙伴甲读出伙伴乙考卷上的第一步，乙便会按着甲所读的做动作。甲会顺序读出乙在考卷上所写的。假如有些步只需指着地图上的某处，乙可以跳过不做，不会被扣分。

Partners exchange their papers. Partner A reads the first step on partner B's paper, and partner B follows the steps. Partner A then reads the steps written by partner B on the paper in order. If some steps only require pointing to a specific location on the map, partner B can skip them without losing points.

假如乙做对了动作，甲便会在考卷上给他一“剔”（），乙不会被扣分。假如乙做错了动作，或者忘记了，甲会给他一个“叉”（），乙要扣一分。甲会顺序读出乙在考卷上所写的，直至全部读完为止。

If B does the action correctly, A will give him a tick ( ü  on the test paper , and B will not lose points. If B does the action incorrectly or forgets it, A will give him a cross ( ū  , and B will lose one point. A will read out what B wrote on the test paper in order until he has read everything.

然后甲、乙便会互换工作，乙会按着上述的方式读出甲所写的每一步。

Then A and B will switch jobs, and B will read out each step written by A in the above manner.

 

100 步的计分方法：

100 Step scoring method:

写错了或写漏了，每步扣一分；不按顺序写，每步扣一分；

If you write wrong or miss something, one point will be deducted for each step; if you write out of order, one point will be deducted for each step;

动作做错了或忘记了，每步扣一分。  If you do the action wrong or forget it, one point will be deducted for each step.

计算被扣分数的总和；然后用 300 减去这个数，那便是你在大考所得的分数。

Calculate the total number of points deducted; then subtract this number from 300 to find your score on the exam.
