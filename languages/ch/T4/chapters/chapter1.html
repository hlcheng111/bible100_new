💎 Treasure 1: John 3:16 - Lesson 1

🌟 Learning Adventure: Discovering God's Love!

🎯 What We'll Learn Today

Today we're going to learn about one of the most important Bible verses - John 3:16! This verse tells us how much God loves us.

"For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life."
- John 3:16

🌈 What This Means for Kids

  • God loves you very, very much!
  • He sent Jesus to be our friend
  • When we believe in Jesus, we get to be with God forever
  • This is the best gift ever!

🎮 Fun Activity: Love Heart Drawing

Draw a big heart and write "God loves me!" inside. Then draw pictures of things that make you happy!

💭 Think About It

How does it make you feel to know that God loves you so much? Can you think of ways to show love to others?
