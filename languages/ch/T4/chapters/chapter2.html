💎 Treasure 1: John 3:16 - Lesson 2

🌟 Learning Adventure: More About God's Love!

🎯 What We'll Learn Today

In this lesson, we'll discover more amazing things about God's love and how we can share it with others!

"For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life."
- John 3:16

🌈 New Things to Discover

  • How to show God's love to friends
  • Ways to be kind and helpful
  • Why sharing makes us happy
  • How to pray and talk to God

🎮 Fun Activity: Kindness Challenge

Try to do three kind things today! Write them down and draw pictures of your acts of kindness.

💭 Think About It

Who can you show love to today? How can you make someone smile?
