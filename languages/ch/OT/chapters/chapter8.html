Lesson Scope: 第 59-63 步

Geographic Scope: 惡性循環 - 拯救

🎯 Course Overview

This course covers 舊約歷史第 59-63 步, from 惡性循環 - 拯救 歷史事件和地理背景，helping learners build a complete Old Testament history framework。

Through systematic learning, you will master key time points, important figures and major events of Old Testament history, laying a solid foundation for in-depth understanding of the Bible.

📚 Steps Covered in This Lesson

  • 第 59-63 步的詳細內容
  • Related Bible passages and historical background
  • Changes and significance of geographic locations
  • Biography and deeds of important figures
  • Theological themes and spiritual lessons

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • 掌握第 59-63 步的歷史脈絡
  • 理解 惡性循環 - 拯救 的地理意義
  • Recognize related Bible figures and events
  • Apply historical lessons to modern life
  • Build a complete Old Testament history framework

🚀 Learning Methods

1. Learn by Doing: Memorize historical steps through actions and rhymes

2. Map Learning: Build Middle East map concepts in the classroom

3. Storytelling: Understand historical events through vivid stories

4. Group Discussion: Learn and review with partners

5. Practical Application: Apply historical lessons to real life
