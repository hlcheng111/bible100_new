Four Treasures of Faith - Core Faith Curriculum

💎 Course Introduction

The T4 Four Treasures of Faith course is the core curriculum of Bible learning, helping learners establish a solid foundation of faith through four important faith themes. Each theme explores important truths in the Bible in depth.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Master the core truths of faith
  • Establish correct theological concepts
  • Cultivate spiritual character and life
  • Learn how to share the gospel
  • Build a solid foundation of faith
