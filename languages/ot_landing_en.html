Systematic Old Testament Bible Study

📚 Course Introduction

The Old Testament 100 Steps course systematically covers the main content of the Old Testament through 100 steps. From Genesis to Malachi, it covers the history, law, poetry, wisdom literature, and prophetic books of the nation of Israel.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Understand the overall structure and themes of the Old Testament
  • Master the main events and characters of Old Testament history
  • Understand Old Testament law and worship system
  • Study Old Testament poetry and wisdom literature
  • Recognize the messages and prophecies of the prophets
