為什麼教會要問恩賜與技能？

Why does the church ask about gifts & skills?

這不是一張「工作派遣單」，而是一段與主同行、與教會一起分辨呼召的旅程。
This is not a work order, but a journey of discernment with the Lord and the church.

  • 聖經基礎 · Biblical basis：基督是教會的頭，各肢體互相配搭（林前 12、弗 4:11–16）。認識恩賜與負擔，是為了建立身體。
    Christ is the head; each part works together (1 Cor 12; Eph 4:11–16). Knowing gifts and burdens serves building the body.
  • 實務需要 · Practical need：小教會同工有限，需要清楚「誰能負擔什麼」，才能邀請而不勉強。
    Smaller teams need clarity on capacity so leaders can invite without pressure.
  • 你的資料 · Your data：可隨時更新；系統建議是參考，最終仍由你與牧者禱告分辨。
    You may update anytime; software hints are reference only — you and pastors discern in prayer.
