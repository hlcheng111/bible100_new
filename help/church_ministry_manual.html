📜 第 0 章 · 教會存在理念與事工總論 Church Foundation & Ministry Overview

一、教會的存在理念（聖經教訓） Biblical Foundation

教會是基督的身體，是神在世上見證的群體。聖經教導我們： The church is the body of Christ and God's witnessing community on earth. Scripture instructs us:

  • 大使命 Great Commission：去使萬民作門徒，奉父、子、聖靈的名給他們施洗，凡主所吩咐的都教訓他們遵守（太廿八19–20）。 Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that the Lord commanded (Mt 28:19–20).
  • 大誡命 Great Commandment：盡心、盡性、盡意愛主你的神；愛人如己（太廿二37–39）。 Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, and mind; and love your neighbour as yourself (Mt 22:37–39).
  • 管家職分 Stewardship：各人要照所得的恩賜彼此服事，作神百般恩賜的好管家（彼前四10）。 Each one should use whatever gift he has received to serve others, as faithful stewards of God's varied grace (1 Pet 4:10).

因此，教會的存在不是為了組織本身，而是為了使人認識基督、在愛中彼此建造、在世上作鹽作光。 Therefore, the church exists not for the organisation itself, but to lead people to Christ, build one another in love, and be salt and light in the world.

三、以人為本的組織意念 People-Centric Design

核心問題：教會與 NGO 常面對人多事繁、資源有限、資訊分散，難以同時兼顧牧養深度與事工管理。 Core challenge: Churches and NGOs often face many people, limited resources, and scattered information, making it difficult to balance pastoral depth with ministry management.

我們希望： Our aspiration:

  • 讓每一位牧者與同工，一進來就看到：整體狀況 + 誰最需要關懷 + 下一步可以做什麼。 Enable every pastor and worker to see at a glance: overall status, who most needs care, and what the next step may be.
  • 讓每一個數字背後，都對應到一個真實的人、一個故事，而不是冷冰冰的報表。 Ensure that behind every figure there is a real person and a story, not merely cold statistics.
  • 用 AI 放大洞察力與執行力，但不取代牧者、長執與小組長的屬靈判斷。 Use AI to amplify insight and execution, yet never to replace the spiritual discernment of pastors, elders, and group leaders.
以人為中心 · People-centric 以團隊為單位 · Team-based 以管家職分為導向 · Stewardship AI 輔助 · 不取代屬靈帶領

3.2 關懷事工：探訪關懷 × 全事工互聯 Visitation & Module Integration

探訪關懷管理系統（visitation）與會眾關懷模式（congregation-care）是教會事工中「誰最需要被關心」的核心。任務與操作：總覽 → 戰略規劃（年度目標）→ 排程（小組／區域）→ 出動（新增任務，探訪對象可從會友選單選擇）→ 報表 → 培訓。 The visitation management system and congregation-care model are the core of "who most needs care" in church ministry. Flow: Overview → Strategic planning (annual goals) → Scheduling (groups/areas) → Deployment (new tasks; visitation targets selectable from member list) → Reports → Training.

與其他模組的具體連動： Specific integration with other modules:

  • 會友系統：流失預警名單 → 自動產生「待探訪」任務；新會友 → 自動產生「新人性探訪」任務
    Member system: At-risk list → auto-generates "pending visitation" tasks; new members → auto-generates "newcomer visitation" tasks.
  • 社群與捐款人：高風險會友名單 → 同步到探訪「危機性」或「優先關懷」
    Community & donors: High-risk member list → synced to visitation "crisis" or "priority care."
  • 志工系統：探訪同工從志工名單選；探訪完成可回寫志工服事記錄
    Volunteer system: Visitation workers selected from volunteer list; completed visits feed back to volunteer service records.
  • 工作流程（workflow）：新會友流程中的「7天後提醒探訪」可建立 visitation 任務
    Workflow: "7-day follow-up visitation" in new member flow can create visitation tasks.
  • 智能提醒（smart-reminders）：探訪計劃與提醒規則可共用
    Smart reminders: Visitation plans and reminder rules can be shared.

行政

理念與目的 Vision & Purpose

財務、資產、研究統計、媒體科技、工具支援、行政管理——忠心管家、資源善用，讓事工有穩固後勤。 Finance, assets, research and statistics, media and technology, tools support, administration—faithful stewardship and prudent use of resources for a solid ministry foundation.

功能與互聯：行政支援是各事工的後勤中樞。儀表板彙總 SPAC、會友事工與財務事工互聯、社群與捐款人與探訪關懷互聯。研究統計為決策提供數據支撐。 Functions & integration: Administration is the logistical hub for all ministries. Dashboard aggregates SPAC; member and finance ministries are interconnected; community and donors are linked with visitation care. Research and statistics provide data support for decision-making.
