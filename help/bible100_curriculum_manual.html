📜 第 0 章 · 核心理念與平台總論 · 點燃人對神話語的熱情 Core Philosophy & Platform Overview · Igniting passion for God's Word

用「動作」學經文、教經文 — 讀經 · 背經 · 應用 · 教導 · Learn and teach Scripture through action

一、聖經動作版理念 Action Edition Philosophy

你是否覺得聖經知識零碎、難記，像拿著地圖卻不會看？百步四寶把舊約、新約與信仰四寶，整理成 100 步 + 四寶，每一步都有口訣、動作、走位——邊說邊做，用身體與口說記住神的話。

Do you find Bible knowledge fragmented and hard to remember, like having a map but not knowing how to read it? Bible 100 Steps turns OT, NT, and the Four Treasures into 100 steps + 4 treasures—each with mnemonics, actions, and positioning. Say and do together; remember God's Word with body and voice.

核心理念（5 點）：

  • 入門不淺：100 步與四寶是骨架，每步可延伸至經文細讀與深度研經。Entry not shallow: 100 steps and Four Treasures form the skeleton; each step extends to detailed study.
  • 記憶有根：口訣、動作、走位建立多感官記憶，每動作對應屬靈意義，可放射理解。Rooted memory: mnemonics, actions, positioning build multisensory memory; each action maps to spiritual meaning.
  • 結構清晰：分析分段架構，組織零碎內容，把曾聽、曾學、曾讀的知識串起來。Clear structure: analyze sections, organize fragments, connect what you've heard, learned, and read.
  • 易教可傳：設計為「易教」，支持從助手到教授的教導階梯，實踐 2 Timothy 2:2。Easy to teach: designed for teachability; supports the ladder from assistant to professor; 2 Timothy 2:2.
  • 四寶四門：約 3:16→外展宣教，主禱文→敬拜禱告，使徒信經→教導聖經，屬靈軍裝→牧養團契事奉。Four Treasures, four doors: John 3:16→outreach; Lord's Prayer→worship; Apostles' Creed→teaching; Armor→pastoral care.

Passion is not a one-time "touching" but a virtuous cycle of remember, understand, teach. The action edition is the starting point and support of this cycle.

🎯 第 1 章 · 從記憶到教導：口訣、動作、走位 From Memory to Teaching: Mnemonics · Actions · Positioning

記得住、想得通、教得出 · Remember · Understand · Teach

不是「背了就忘」，而是記得住、想得通、教得出。口訣把經文濃縮成可誦的短句，動作用身體表達屬靈意義，走位用空間呈現結構——每步都是話題起點，可放射到經文、課題與生活應用。

Not "memorize and forget" but remember, understand, teach. Mnemonics condense Scripture into recitable phrases; actions express spiritual meaning with the body; positioning shows structure in space—each step is a conversation starter.

1.1 口訣（Mnemonics）

功能：把經文或教義濃縮成可誦、可記的短句。效果：每句都是話題起點。例子：約 3:16 的口訣可帶出救恩、信心、永生等主題。

Function: condense Scripture or doctrine into recitable phrases. Effect: each phrase is a conversation starter. Example: John 3:16 mnemonic leads to salvation, faith, eternal life.

1.2 動作（Actions）

功能：用身體表達屬靈意義。效果：體感記憶、每動作對應屬靈概念、可放射到相關經文與應用。

Function: express spiritual meaning with the body. Effect: kinesthetic memory; each action maps to a spiritual concept; radiates to related Scripture and application.

1.3 走位（Positioning）

功能：用空間與移動表達結構、順序、關係。例子：主禱文不同段落對應不同位置；屬靈軍裝各件對應身體部位。

Function: express structure, sequence, relationships through space and movement. Example: Lord's Prayer sections map to positions; Armor pieces map to body parts.

1.4 易記 · 易明 · 易教

易記 → 口訣、動作、走位，多感官記憶。易明 → 每動作對應屬靈意義，可放射理解。易教 → 結構清楚，可從助手逐步承擔教導。

Easy to remember: mnemonics, actions, positioning—multisensory. Easy to understand: each action maps to spiritual meaning; radiates. Easy to teach: clear structure; progress from assistant upward.

1.5 放射 · 組織 · 連結 Radiate · Organize · Connect

放射理解：每動作 → 對應屬靈意義 → 可放射到：相關經文、相關課題、生活應用、進一步問題與討論。

Radiating understanding: each action → spiritual meaning → radiates to: related Scripture, topics, life application, further questions and discussion.

分析分段架構：把經文或教義拆成有邏輯的段落。主禱文：稱謂→願你的→日用的→饒恕→試探→國度榮耀。使徒信經：父神→基督→聖靈→教會→末世。

Analyze structure: break Scripture or doctrine into logical sections. Lord's Prayer: address→your will→daily→forgiveness→temptation→kingdom. Apostles' Creed: Father→Christ→Spirit→Church→eschaton.

組織零碎內容：用 100 步、四寶、口訣、動作建立「骨架」，把零碎知識掛在對應的步驟上。記起「以往曾聽、曾學、曾讀」的內容，新學的內容有地方「歸檔」。

Organize fragments: use 100 steps, Four Treasures, mnemonics, actions as "skeleton"; hang fragmentary knowledge on corresponding steps. Recall what you've heard, learned, read; new content has a place to "file."
