0938  Crown Him King of Kings   _Damazio, Sharon   擁戴祂為王●

  • 　

　

　

Verse 1
Crown Him King of kings,
crown Him Lord of lords.
Wonderful,
Counsellor,
Almighty God.

Chorus
Emmanuel –
God is with us.
And He shall reign, He shall reign,
He shall reign for ever more.

擁戴祂為王，
擁戴祂為主，
奇妙神，
安慰師，
全能真神。

Chorus
以馬內利，
神與我們同在，
祂要掌權到永遠，
祂要掌權到永遠。
