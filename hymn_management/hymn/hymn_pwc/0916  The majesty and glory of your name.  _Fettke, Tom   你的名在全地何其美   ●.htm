THOMAS E. FETTKE (b. 1941) was born in the Bronx New York and educated on the west coast at California State at Hayward. Active in publishing, primarily through Lillenas, the music publishing house of the Nazarene Church, he now resides in "Music City" Brentwood, Tennessee. Best known in the Hope catalog for his hymn "Be Strong In The Lord" which he co-authored with lyricist Linda Lee Johnson, the hymn was first published in 1979 as an anthem GC 827. This best selling choral piece has been in the top 20 category of all time sales leaders for a number of years. The hymn version also appears in numerous hymnals and can be found as #514 in Hope's new hymnal WORSHIP & REJOICE (2001).
