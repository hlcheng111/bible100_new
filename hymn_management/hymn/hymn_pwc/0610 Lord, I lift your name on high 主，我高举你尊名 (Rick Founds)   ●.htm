《主，我高举你的名》(英文: Lord I Lift Your Name On High)

是一首基督教赞美诗，由Rick Founds作曲作词。

歌词的灵感源于Rick Founds当时阅读了的一些东西，其中一篇是科学杂志关于水力学的报导；另一篇文章是关于每天灵修必须与基督一起做；以及他坚持牺牲自己让我们得到永生的最大奥秘。科学文章主要集中于大自然和水经过冷凝循环时在不同形态下的表现，它如何聚集成较重的水分子至降雨状态，以雨和雪的形态降落至地上。降雨养育大地，并给予种子和植物水份，找到路径汇聚成小溪、河流、湖泊和海洋，最后透过蒸发过程回到天上。

Rick Founds被两种不同水的平行所吸引：就是”这个星球的水”的物理；以及心灵、洁净、给予生命、”活水” - 基督，我们的救主。

他从起初已经与天父同在”，”压缩限制了自己”，以人的身份来到地上，住在我们中间，透过十架的牺牲洗净我们的罪，他死后被埋葬，三天后复活，回到天父的身边为我们预备地方，歌词的灵感由此产生”你从天上降世间指引我路，从地上到十架，我罪得赎，从十架到坟墓，从坟墓到天上……而这个奥秘的回应就是”主我高举你的名”

Lord, I Lift Your Name on High

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
"Lord, I Lift Your Name on High"
Song
Released 1989
Genre Contemporary worship music
Songwriter(s) Rick Founds

"Lord, I Lift Your Name on High" is a worship song. It was written by Rick Founds in 1989.

Founds wrote the song during his morning devotion, while reading the scriptures on his computer monitor and watching television. He plucked his guitar thinking about the "cycle of redemption", comparing it with the water cycle.

You came from heaven to earth, to show the way
From the earth to the cross, my debt to pay
From the cross to the grave, from the grave to the sky
Lord I lift your name on high

Founds performed the song as a worship leader in his church. It was picked up by Maranatha! Music and initially recorded by the Maranatha! Singers followed by the Praise Band. Promise Keepers performed the song in English and Spanish in their drives.

Since the 1990s, it has been one of the most popular Christian songs. In the United States, Christian Copyright Licensing International (CCLI) reported Lord I Lift Your Name on High as the most popular song used in churches every year from 1997 to 2003. Currently it is No. 24 on the list.[1] CCLI UK report it as the fifth most popular printed, projected or recorded song in mid-2006.[2] In Australia this song was the seventh most used song by the beginning of 2007[3]

There have been many interpretations of the song by many artists in all styles: gospel, R&B, soul, rock, dance, reggae, hip hop, rap, soca, ska, punk, a cappella amongst others and has been translated to a number of languages and recorded in a number of countries.

LORD, I LIFT YOUR NAME ON HIGH                                          written in 1989

The Story

Rick Founds (1954 – ) wanted to major in music at college, but his high school choral teacher talked him out of it. Instead, he got a degree in media technology and began working in research and development at a fiber optics company.

When Rick wrote this song, he was studying the attributes of God on his electronic Bible. God was bringing all of Rick’s gifts and talents together: his interest in technology, his love of music, and his desire to know more of the Lord.

As he found himself amazed at the love of God, he was struck by God’s divine plan. It was a cycle of events that left him in awe; things like the rain falling… and watering the earth… evaporating into the clouds… and then falling again. It reminded him of the fact Christ down from Heaven to earth, lived among us, died on the cross for us, rose from the dead, and ascended back to Heaven to be with His Father. And He will come back again!

That is why Rick wrote this song.

In the United States Christian Copyright Licensing International (CCLI) reported this song as the most popular song used in Christian evangelical churches every year from 1997 to 2003, and – since then – has remained in the Top 10 list.

This song has become a favorite of kids at church camp… and many of them have developed motions that go with each line of the song.

Song Story: Lord, I Lift Your Name on High

July 19, 2020Leave a comment

　

This popular praise and worship song was written by Rick Founds in 1989.

Founds began writing music at an early age and has written over 300 songs.

He moved to California, where he served as Minister of Music for a church in California.

He recounted the writing of this popular song.

“The song was born out of a typical Bible study or devotional time that I have, usually in the mornings. At that time he was music director of a large church…I would read a portion of the New Testament followed by a portion of the Old Testament.”

During his devotional one day, the lyrics to the song came to him and he picked up his guitar and began to play and sing, the song coming quickly.

He went on to say, “It is just a simple song, but it is what the Lord dropped on my heart.”

Soon thereafter, he shared it at Bible Study and from there the song spread. Maranatha! Music published the song and it was often performed by Promise Keepers.

From 1997-2003 the CCLI reported Lord I Lift Your Name on High as the most popular song used in churches and it has been covered by many artists.

 It has been translated into at least 15 languages and dialects and he receives letters from around the world.

Founds has heard his song done in every style you can think of. “It’s a lot of fun to hear things you’ve written done in reggae, in 3/4, in country, and in Spanish ﬂavor. That means those groups have made the song their own. It’s exciting!”

We all need to remember to lift the Lords name on high.
