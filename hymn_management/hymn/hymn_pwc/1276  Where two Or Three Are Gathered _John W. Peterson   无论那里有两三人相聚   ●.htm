　105 Where two Or Three Are Gathered

• Where two or three are gathered, gathered in Jesus’ name, In the midst he’ll be – He promised, Kindling a holy flame, Come, come, Lord Jesus, Come in love and pow’r;
Quietly we wait before You – Make this a sacred hour.

• In Your name and for Your glory We are met today;
Stir our hearts to praise and worship, Teach us how to pray, Come, come, lord Jesus, Be our hallowed guest;
Breath Your holy breath upon us – may ev’ry heart be blest.

　105 無論那裏有三人相聚
無論那裏有兩三人，奉主的名相聚，
主必同在賜下聖靈，這是主的應許。
求主耶穌來，帶著能力與愛；
我們在你面前靜候－求主現在就來。

今日我們在此相聚，願主名得榮耀；
使我心靈熱忱敬拜，教我如何禱告。
求主耶穌來，來與我們同住；
聖靈吹入我們心中－使每顆心蒙福。
