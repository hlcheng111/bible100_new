簡介    Our God Is an Awesome God (Awesome God)

This is a contemporary worship song that ﬁrst gained recognition on Christian radio and later became a popular congregational song. “Awesome” here is not a trivial expression but carries the meaning of "awe-inspiring" and is based on scripture (e.g. Daniel 9:4).

Richard Wayne Mullins (October 21, 1955 – September 19, 1997) was an American contemporary Christian music singer and songwriter best known for his worship songs "Awesome God" and "Step by Step". Some of his albums were also considered among Christian music's best, including Winds of Heaven, Stuff of Earth (1988), The World As Best As I Remember It, Volume One (1991) and A Liturgy, a Legacy, & a Ragamuffin Band (1993). His songs have been performed by numerous artists, including Caedmon's Call, Five Iron Frenzy, Amy Grant, Carolyn Arends, Jars of Clay, Michael W. Smith, John Tesh, Chris Rice, Rebecca St. James, Hillsong United and Third Day.[1] During the tribute to Rich Mullins' life at the 1998 GMA Dove Awards, Amy Grant described him as "the uneasy conscience of Christian music.” [2]

Mullins was devoted to the Christian faith and heavily influenced by St. Francis of Assisi. In 1997, he composed a musical called Canticle of the Plains,[3] a retelling of the life of St. Francis set in the Old West.[4]

這個棕眼長髮披肩的超級歌唱巨星及作曲家，曾連續十年被提名為聖鴿獎的得主，居住在新墨西哥州的印第安人保留區的一輪拖車中。 他的童年及成長是在印第安納州的阿拔（Arba），以前貴格會的置徒區。

當地一半的居民都是他的親戚，曾祖母和堂表兄弟都是鄰居，主日學的教師也都是他的伯叔姑姨。 他的父親是一位誠實的教友，從不掩飾自己的優點和缺點。少年的穆林斯逐漸感覺自己外在的敬虔十分膚淺；神真正愛的是他本人，而不是他偽善的外表。 當他面臨靈命的低潮或遇撒但的試探時，他發覺最好的得勝途徑就是讀聖經。  

穆林斯認為基督徒應該日日與主同行。 他最愛傳道書，他說；「傳道書的要旨是生活，在神的旨意中活得既豐富又充實。」他記得在他參與全市青年自覺運動時，有一位牧師說：「我們必須讓年青人認識耶穌，這樣他們才可停止吸毒，未婚生子等罪行。」他則認為認識耶穌最重要的是告訴他們神無條件的愛和道成肉身降世的救恩。

他畢業於聖經學院，但時常感到內心空虛，與神隔離。 他經歷了自貶、自恃、自憐、酗酒，以及酖溺於嗜好等。 經過了十年心靈的掙扎，從禱告，讀經到悔改，他終於讓神光照了他內心。 他對神的崇敬、信心、心靈的密契，在他的詩歌中表露無遺。 為了避免受外界太多的試探和引誘，他揀選居住在印第安人保留區，過單純的生活，也隨時警惕自己不要忘本和認清自己是神的兒女。

「生命和生活來自神，而不是出之於教條和德行。」這是他在1997年秋，車禍喪生前的名言。 

Awesome God

Awesome God" is a contemporary worship song written by Rich Mullins and first recorded on his 1988 album, Winds of Heaven, Stuff of Earth. It was the first single from the album and rose to the number one spot on Christian radio and subsequently became a popular congregational song.[1] Its title is inspired by a biblical expression (Nehemiah 1:5, Nehemiah 9:32, Psalm 47, Daniel 9:4, etc.), variously translated as "Awesome God", (JPS, in the old-fashioned meaning "awe-inspiring"), "great" (KJV), among other alternatives. Due to the popularity of the song it became Mullins' signature song.[2]
