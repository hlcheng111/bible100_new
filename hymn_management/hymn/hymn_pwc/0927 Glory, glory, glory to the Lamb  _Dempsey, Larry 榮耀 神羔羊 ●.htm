Dempsey, Larry  1946-1995

https://hymnary.org/person/Dempsey_L

Larry D. Dempsey was known among many in the church and ministry community as a talented musician, prolific songwriter, and Minister of God's Word and grace. One of his most popular and well-known worship songs is the anthem, "Glory to the Lamb." The song has been used in worship services and pageants, especially in celebration of Resurrection Day.
