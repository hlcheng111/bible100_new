Verse 1
Like a lamb he goes to slaughter;
like a lamb so meek, so still.
Like a lamb he goes to slaughter
on a cross to do his Father’s will.
Like a lamb for sin atoning;
like a lamb, he pays the price.
Like a lamb for sin atoning,
on a cross, a perfect sacrifice.

Chorus
Like a lamb, his life is forfeit;
like a lamb, he bears our shame.
Like a lamb, his life is forfeit;
on a cross, he calls his Father’s name.
Like a lamb, laid on the altar;
like a lamb, his fate is sealed.
Like a lamb, laid on the altar; on a cross,
God’s wondrous love revealed.
On a cross, God’s perfect love revealed;
All for us, a Father’s love revealed,
revealed.

MARY KAY BEALL (b. 1943) was born and raised in Akron, Ohio. She has been a Writer/Editor with Hope Publishing Company since 1984. She holds a B.M. degree from Ohio Wesleyan University, an M.A. from Ohio State University, and a Masters in Theological Studies from Trinity Seminary in Columbus, OH. She and her husband, John Carter, are a well-known creative team and currently make their home in Columbus, Ohio. Mary Kay has been an editor for the Hope Publishing Company since 1991 and she conducts clinics and reading sessions throughout the United States and Canada. She is ordained in the American Baptist Church and is currently ministering to church musicians and choirs in the United States and Canada in the area of Spiritual Formation and Renewal. Hope Publishing Company has recently released her devotional collection for music ministry entitled SINGING IN THE SPIRIT, (Code No. 8007). She also has a large number of original two part choral compositions available from the company and as of 2002 Mary Kay has 84 separate titles available from Hope.

Like a Lamb  如羔羊

本诗词曲作者是Mary Kay Beall，歌词根据《以赛亚书》53:7经文所作的：“他被欺压，在受苦的时候却不开口；他像羊羔被牵到宰杀之地，又像羊在剪毛的人手下无声，他也是这样不开口。”本诗属混声二部合唱曲，中文翻译区美贤，简谱由洪洁光弟兄制作提供。音频由朱贵金老师训练指挥，音频中仍有许多欠缺不足，仅供参考。

排练说明：最开始部分的”Very slow and deliberate”意思是“非常缓慢并带有深思的”。第4小节的”Trebles”意思是“高音声部”，可以让女高女低一起唱。第7小节的“Man”意思是“男声”，可以让男高男低一起唱。第23小节的“Soprano and Tenor”意思是女高音和男高音唱上面一行的旋律，第24小节的“Alto and Bass”意思是女低和男低唱下面一行的旋律。”With motion”意思是流动的，速度也变为每分钟72拍。全曲最高音量出现在第32小节的“f”处，一直到第39小节才逐渐减弱为“mf”,“mp”。第42小节的“slowing”意思是慢下来，第43小节要恢复原速“a tempo”，第44小节的“slowing greatly”意思是“非常缓慢”，结束时需带有沉思缓慢的情感，停留在对“主爱”这个主题的思考上。
