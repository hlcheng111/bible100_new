　【祂本為聖】
詩集：世紀頌讚，28

Who pardons my iniquities, who heals me from within,
Who lifts my fallen life and sets me on my feet again,
Who leads me like a shepherd when I've gone astray--
My Lord, my God, my Jesus, the One I praise today.

Chorus
Holy is he and great is his glory.
Holy is he and worthy of our praise.
I stand in his presence amazed
and crown him with worship and praise.
Holy is he, holy is he, holy is he.

Who hears me in my loneliness, who finds me where I hide,
Who knocks outside my broken heart till I let him inside,
Who's life deserves the honor laid before his throne--
My Lord, my God, my Jesus, my Savior and my all


.祂本為聖，祂本是偉大榮耀；
祂本為聖，配受我們敬拜；
與祂同在，向祂歡呼，
尊祂為王，敬拜讚美！
祂本為聖，祂本為聖，祂本為聖。

Cloninger, Claire, 1942-2019  ［詞，曲作者］

https://hymnary.org/person/Cloninger_C

Claire Cloninger (August 12, 1942 – August 15, 2019) was an American songwriter of contemporary Christian music, author and speaker.[1] She (co-)wrote hundreds of songs, including You Gave Me Love When Nobody Gave Me A Prayer with Archie Jordan for B. J. Thomas, Friend of a Wounded Heart for Wayne Watson (who was also a co-writer),[2] and songs for Sandi Patty and Paul Overstreet.[1] She authored 18 books,[2] including Making ‘I Do’ Last a Lifetime, Dear Abba: Finding the Father’s Heart Through Prayer, and Postcards for People Who Hurt.[1] She won six Dove Awards from the Gospel Music Association.[2]
