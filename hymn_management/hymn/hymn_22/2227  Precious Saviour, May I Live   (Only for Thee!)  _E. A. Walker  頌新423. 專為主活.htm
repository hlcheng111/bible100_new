　颂主新歌
423专为主活

1 Precious Savior, may I live
Only for Thee!
Spend the powers Thou dost give
Only for Thee!
Be my spirit’s deep desire
Only for Thee!
May my intellect aspire
Only for Thee!

2 In my joys may I rejoice
Only for Thee!
In my choices make my choice
Only for Thee!
Meekly may I suffer grief
Only for Thee!
Gratefully accept relief
Only for Thee!

3 Be my smiles and be my tears
Only for Thee!
Be my young and riper years
Only for Thee!
Be my peace and be my strife
Only for Thee!
Be my love and be my life
Only for Thee!

4 Be my singing and my sighing
Only for Thee!
Be my sickness and my dying
Only for Thee!
Be my rising, be my glory
Only for Thee!
Be my whole eternity
Only for Thee!

Source: Hymns of Consecration and Faith #44 

1.惟愿救主容我活，专为我主；
尽心尽力尽才学，专为我主。
不论心里想何事，专为我主，
一生举动并行止，专为我主。
主先为我舍生命，赎罪重价都还清，
我今献身意立定，专为我主。

2.欢乐时候使我乐，专为我主，
谈论古今宣主德，专为我主。
事不顺利叫我忍，专为我主，
工虽艰难不灰心，专为我主。
主先为我舍生命，赎罪重价都还清，
我今献身意立定，专为我主。

3.或享安乐或受难，专为我主，
自幼到老不闲懒，专为我主。
随人爱我随人嫌，专为我主，
惟愿效主至完全，专为我主。
主先为我舍生命，赎罪重价都还清，
我今献身意立定，专为我主。
