593求主使用 　

BY MARY KAY BEALL

Christ has no hands but your hands 
to heal the world's infirmities 
to touch the hurting, comfort, 
calm, to offer healing balm.

Christ has no feet but our feet 
to walk the paths of righteousness,
to seek the sad, to lead the lost,
and never count the cost.

Christ has no arms but our arms
to lift the fallen , hold the faint, 
to bear the heavy weight of grief
for all who seek relief.

Christ has no heart but our heart
to love the lonely, prize the weak,
to cry the every troubled soul, 
to make the wounded whole.

Christ has no voice but our voice 
to sing the songs of joy and peace,
to shout the news of victory
that sets the captive fee.

 

　
基督，使用我雙手，能醫世人軟弱苦痛，
撫摸安慰，抹油塗膏、效主醫治事奉。

基督，使用我雙足，步向公義正直大道，
扶助悲苦，引領迷途，自甘任怨任勞。

基督，使用我雙臂，扶攙跌倒，支持怯弱，
承托苦痛，安慰哀傷，分擔艱難重壓。

基督，使用我心懷，憐恤孤寡，眷顧弱殘，
流淚代求失喪靈，受創之心復原。

基督，使用我聲音，唱出歡樂和平歌韻，
高呼基督勝利之源，同宣釋放、完全
