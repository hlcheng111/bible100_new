In This Quiet Moment


Written and composed in 1971 by Larry Mayfield, this beautiful call to worship. It is only published in one hymnal, being #592 in Word Music's "The Hymnal for Worship & Celebration".

Larry Mayfield was born, June 20, 1944, in Sampson, N.Y. He was a gifted musician who expressed his love of Jesus Christ by using his talents for the Lord's service. Larry was a composer and arranger, a pianist, an organist, a trombonist, an orchestra leader, choir conductor, and music teacher. His legacy will continue through the countless musical compositions and arrangements that church choirs use in their musical worship. Most of all he was a devoted husband, father, and grandfather, and coach. Larry was a highly respected free-lance composer/arranger for over 45 years. His works are published by the Benson Company, Word, Brentwood Music, Provident, Lillenas, Gaither Music, Glory Sound, Shawnee Press, Hal Leonard, Hope Publishing, LifeWay, Broadman Press, Lexicon, Star Song, Re-Generation, Good Life, Lorenz, FourMost Productions, Zondervan and Singspiration.

Larry was a teacher at heart and his desire to mentor young musicians was evident. Many young men would come to our door and sit with Larry as he taught them about the music they wanted to someday compose. Larry taught as an adjunct professor at Belmont and Trevecca Universities in Nashville on studio singing. He has also taught music seminars, planned and led worship, and produced recording sessions for well-known Christian artists. He has written and published numerous seasonal musicals for churches as well as patriotic musicals for schools. He has also recorded three of his own piano CDs.

Through his hundreds of publications Larry’s influence has been strongly felt in the churches of America and worldwide. His love for children also motivated him to create “Kids Sing Praise” in the ‘80’s, and musicals for churches, schools and Kids TV. Diane was blessed to be mother to their children, and collaborator with Larry on some of his original compositions, working on conceptualization, research and lyrics.

Larry had a Bachelor of Sacred Music from Moody Bible Institute (1967) a Bachelor of Music and a Master of Music from Chicago Musical College, Roosevelt University (1968). He began his doctorate work at Northwestern University, but set that aside as he was compelled to be in the studio, creating music for the church. The music that Larry has created throughout those years leaves a legacy for church music and has impacted the lives of God’s people.He died on December 20, 2010 in Brentwood, Tennessee.

In This Quiet Moment: Teach Me
 

The earth is filled with your love,
O Lord; teach me your decrees.
—Psalm 119:64


Imagine that you are all alone and that you are standing on a hillside looking out over the valley shown in the photograph at the beginning of this blog post. For just a moment, close your eyes and think about what you might hear and feel. The breeze whispers against your skin. You can hear the rustling of the grass. Perhaps you can hear a bird chirping in the trees. The brook is a ways away, but if you concentrate you can just barely hear the water flowing over the rocks.
Even more than what you hear and feel is what you don’t hear or feel. You do not hear any voices, any vehicles, or any human-made noise. You can’t smell the exhaust fumes of cars and trucks. No one is bumping into you. You don’t have to worry about stepping on some trash on a sidewalk. In place of the raucousness of life in a city, you only sense an overwhelming sense of peace.
Do you realize that what you feel in this moment is the love of God? That’s right. In the peaceful setting shown in the photograph above, you can feel the love of God. Why? Because God’s love inhabits the earth. When we can put aside all of the distractions of life, we can palpably feel the love of God. And, it is in just such a setting that we can ask God to teach us His decrees. We can ask Him to tell us what He wants us to do.
Larry Mayfield has written a song that captures the essence of the Scripture verse at the beginning of this blog post.

 

Here are the words:
In this quiet moment,
Jesus speak to me
Fill my heart with thy love divine,
Your power let me see.
