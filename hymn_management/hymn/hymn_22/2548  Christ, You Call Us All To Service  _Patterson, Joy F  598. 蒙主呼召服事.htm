　598蒙主呼召服事

1 Christ, You call us all to service,
call us all who follow You;
plant in us a deep commitment
all Your work and will to do.
Fire a passion for Your justice,
in us kindle love of peace;
help us heal the brokenhearted,
to the captive bring release.

2 Teach us how to work together,
brothers, sisters, side by side;
equal partners in the struggle,
in the cause of truth allied.
To each one some gift is given,
man or woman, young or old;
help us use each skill and talent
Your great purpose to unfold.

3 Let us be a servant people,
reconciling, ending strife;
seeking ways more just of sharing
and of ordering human life.
Fill us with a glowing vision
of this world as it should be;
send us forth to change that vision
into blest reality.

 

　
基督信徒蒙主呼召，跟隨主，忠心事奉；
求主栽種委身心志，樂意遵旨作聖工。
燃點心中熾熱火燄，愛慕公義、主和平；
幫助我們醫治傷心，釋放囚奴解罪困。

教導我們同心工作，兄弟姊妹肩並肩一起奮鬥，
無分彼此，願為真理同陣線，男女老少，
無論何人，皆有主所頒恩賜，
當盡本分各展所長，為要彰顯主意旨。

我們服役，誠信謙卑--使人和睦，解紛爭，
建設公平、正義社會，分享生命主豐盛。
求主賜與光明遠象：普世尊崇主統治；
差遣我們實踐異象，拓展天國主美意。

Joy F. Patterson (b. 1931),

of Wassau, Wisconsin, is an elder in the Presbyterian Church who has written many texts and tunes; twenty-nine are collected in Come, You People of the Promise (Hope Publishing, Co., 1994); another collection, Teach Our Eyes New Ways of Seeing, was published in 2005 (Selah). Patterson has enjoyed a varied career as a French professor, homemaker, and claim representative for the Social Security Administration.
