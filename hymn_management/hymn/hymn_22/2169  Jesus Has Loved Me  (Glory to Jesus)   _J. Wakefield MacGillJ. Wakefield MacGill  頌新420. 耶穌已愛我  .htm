420耶稣已爱我

1 Jesus has loved me— wonderful Savior!
Jesus has loved me, I cannot tell why;
He came to rescue sinners unworthy;
My heart He conquered, for Him I would die.

2 Jesus has saved me— wonderful Savior!
Jesus has saved me, I cannot tell how;
But this I do know, He came, my ransom,
Dying on Calv’ry, with thorns on His brow.

3 Jesus will lead me— wonderful Savior!
Jesus will lead me, I cannot tell where;
So I will follow, thru joy or sorrow,
Sunshine or tempest, since He leads me there.

Source: Reformed Press Hymnal: an all around hymn book which will meet the requirements of every meeting where Christians gather for praise #251 

1.奇妙的救主耶稣已爱我;
虽不知为何，主确已爱我；
他来施拯救，释放众罪奴，
我心已臣服，生死全为主。

2.奇妙的救主耶稣已救我;
虽不解如何，主确已救我；
只知主流血，舍命为赎价，
头戴荆棘冠，死在各各他。

3.奇妙的救主耶稣必领我;
任前途如何，救主必领我；
任领向何方，崎岖与平宁，
阴凄或晴明，我必要跟从！

4.奇妙的救主将赐我冠冕；
虽不知何日，为期应不远;
华美圣台前，伏受主恩赏，
圣众齐歌唱，欢乐而颂扬。
