Father In Heaven, Eternal God  _Thomas Crawford   頌新055. 永遠真神 

　

　

　

1.天父为神，无始无终，天地万物，由主造化；
赏给需用，无尽无穷，一切生灵，都在恩下。

2.天父为灵，无所不在，由天至地，无所不管；
主律是圣，主心是爱，旨意永远不能变换。

3.万群圣徒，一齐聚会，尽心尽力，同唱高声，
颂扬、感谢、公义、恩惠、荣华、权势、归主圣名。

　

　

　

Short Name: Thomas Crawford
Full Name: Crawford, Thomas, 1850-?
Birth Year: 1850

Crawford, Thomas, was born in 1850 at Falkirk, Scotland, and now (1906) resides at Stroud Green, London.

His hymn:—
Raise the song of triumph, swell the strains of joy [Service for Christ], gained a first prize for words and music (both original) in a S. S. Union competition, 1883. Both are in the Sunday School Hymnary, 1905, and the words in Voice of Praise, 1887, School Hymns, 1891, and others. [Rev. James Mearns, M.A.]

--John Julian, Dictionary of Hymnology, New Supplement (1907)
