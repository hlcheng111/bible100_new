5 First Poem (8 years old) Oh what a happy child I am Although I cannot see! I am resolved that in this world Contented I will be How many blessings I enjoy That other people don’t So weep or sigh because I’m blind I cannot, or I won’t!

6 Perspective on Blindness “It seemed intended by the blessed providence of God that I should be blind all my life, and I thank Him for the dispensation. If perfectly earthly sight were offered me tomorrow I would not accept it. I might not have sung hymns to the praise of God if I had been distracted by the beautiful and interesting things about me.”
