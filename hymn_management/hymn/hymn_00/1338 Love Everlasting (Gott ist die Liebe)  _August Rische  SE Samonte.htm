Words: Au­gust D. Rische, 1852 (Gott ist die Lie­be). Com­po­site trans­la­tion. 

God loves me dearly,
Grants me salvation;
God loves me dearly,
Loves even me.

Refrain

Therefore I’ll say again,
God loves me dearly,
God loves me dearly,
Loves even me.

Refrain

I was in slavery,
Sin, death, and darkness;
God’s love was working
To make me free.

Refrain

He sent forth Jesus,
My dear Redeemer,
He sent forth Jesus
And set me free.

Refrain

Jesus, my Savior,
Himself did offer;
Jesus, my Savior,
Paid all I owed.

Refrain

Now I will praise You,
O Love Eternal,
Now I will praise You
All my life long.

Refrain
