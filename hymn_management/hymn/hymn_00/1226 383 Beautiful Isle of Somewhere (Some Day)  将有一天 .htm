　 383 Some Day  将有一天

1 Somewhere the sun is shining,
Somewhere the songbirds dwell;
Hush, then, thy sad repining,
God lives, and all is well.

Refrain:
Somewhere, somewhere,
Beautiful isle of somewhere;
Land of the true, where we live anew,
Beautiful isle of somewhere.

2 Somewhere the day is longer,
Somewhere the task is done;
Somewhere the heart is stronger,
Somewhere the guerdon won. [Refrain]

3 Somewhere the load is lifted,
Close by an open gate;
Somewhere the clouds are rifted,
Somewhere the angels wait. [Refrain]

Source: African Methodist Episcopal Church Hymnal #494

    曲調名：WAY OF THE CROSS，11.7.10.8 複
    作詞者：Jessie Brown Pounds, 1861-1921
    作曲者：Charles Hutchinson Gabriel, 1856-1932

    第一節譜例：我要回天家，十字架領天路，此外方法皆錯誤，

    I must needs go home by the way of the cross, There’s no other way but this;


    本詩又名“I Must Needs Go Home By The Way Of The Cross”.

    本詩作詞者是潔西（Jessie Brown Pounds, 1861-1921），出生於美國俄亥俄州。幼時因體弱而選擇在家接受教育，15歲時，她開始將文章送交克利夫蘭報紙和各種宗教的出版物，並為費爾摩雅各（James H. Fillmore）創作宗教性質的詩歌歌詞達三十年以上。

    1896年，她與Reverend John Pounds先生結婚，夫婿是在印地安那州的印地安那波里斯的克里斯汀中心教堂擔任牧師。潔西早期因為常將她的詩作編寫成有韻律的作品，因此開始了她為歌曲著作的職業生涯。在她的一生中，她為清唱劇和小歌劇寫了五十首歌詞集、九本書，和超過四百首的福音詩歌。

    本會讚美詩第383首〈將有一天〉（Some Day!）與第432首〈祂大能手扶持我〉（The Touch of His Hand on Mine）都是她的作品。

    本詩的作曲者是加百列（Charles H. Gabriel 1856-1932）2，他是名噪一時的多產福音詩歌作家，也是20世紀初最具影響力的佈道詩歌作家。

    本詩創作源自《約翰福音》十四章6節：「耶穌說：我就是道路、真理、生命；若不藉著我，沒有人能到父那裡去。」

    耶穌基督帶來希望，並親自引導我們走上一條清晰的道路，旋律輕快悠揚呼應歌詞意境，複歌「十架領導我回家，十架領導我回家；常存大盼望，歡然奔天家，十架領導我回家」，是百唱不倦的歡愉頌讚！

    註1：曲調名──WAY OF THE CROSS，為本詩歌原文標題。

    註2：本詩作曲者與155首相同，其相關介紹請參見《聖靈》月刊2010年9月號（396期）。

    Full Name: Pounds, Jessie Brown, 1861-1921
    Birth Year: 1861
    Death Year: 1921

    Jessie Brown Pounds was born in Hiram, Ohio, a suburb of Cleveland on 31 August 1861. She was not in good health when she was a child so she was taught at home. She began to write verses for the Cleveland newspapers and religious weeklies when she was fifteen. After an editor of a collection of her verses noted that some of them would be well suited for church or Sunday School hymns, J. H. Fillmore wrote to her asking her to write some hymns for a book he was publishing. She then regularly wrote hymns for Fillmore Brothers. She worked as an editor with Standard Publishing Company in Cincinnati from 1885 to 1896, when she married Rev. John E. Pounds, who at that time was a pastor of the Central Christian Church in Indianapolis.

    A memorable phrase would come to her, she would write it down in her notebook. Maybe a couple months later she would write out the entire hymn. She is the author of nine books, about fifty librettos for cantatas and operettas and of nearly four hundred hymns. Her hymn "Beautiful Isle of Somewhere" was sung at President McKinley's funeral.

    Dianne Shapiro, from "The Singers and Their Songs: sketches of living gospel hymn writers" by Charles Hutchinson Gabriel (Chicago: The Rodeheaver Company, 1916)
