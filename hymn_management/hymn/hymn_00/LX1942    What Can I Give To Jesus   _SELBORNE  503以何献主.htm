LX1942    What Can I Give To Jesus  _SELBORNE  503以何献主

  • 　

　

　

1 What can I give to Jesus,
Who gave himself for me?
How can I show my love to him
Who died on Calvary?
Myself I give to Jesus,
Who gave himself for me:
Thus will I show my love to him
Who died on Calvary.

2 I give my life to Jesus,
My strength and health and all;
Assured he'll be my constant Friend,
Whatever may befall.
This will I give to Jesus, etc.

3 Thy Spirit give, Lord Jesus,
To strengthen me for this;
That I may have thy loving smile,
And share thine endless bliss.
Then shall I give to Jesus
A song more sweet, more free;
And ever show my love to him
Who died on Calvary.

Source: Laudes Domini: a selection of spiritual songs, ancient and modern for use in the prayer-meeting #414 

　

503以何献主

1.我献何物给耶稣，报祂救我宏恩？
我当如何爱恩主，因祂为我舍身。
我献我心给耶稣，虽然仅献微忱，
恩主必定不轻看，因祂所爱惟真。

2.我献我灵给耶稣，终日思念祂恩，
一切虚浮污秽事，完全撇弃净尽。
我献我魂给耶稣，果断，情感，赤心，
一切但求主旨意，愿成就在我身。

3.我献精力给耶稣，身体、耳目、手足，
完全听从主吩咐，谨守、遵行、顺服。
我将完全献耶稣，不自留存私物，
无论生死与祸福，定意永随我主。
