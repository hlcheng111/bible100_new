1443 GP129  March On Forward  向前走

　

　

“129 向前走 March On Forward.

March on forward

March on forward press on for the Lord.
Go and do not look back.
March on forward press on for the Lord,
Go and do not turn back.
Is not fit for heaven. March on forward,
press on for the Lord, He will lead us on.
向前走

向前走呀，努力向前走，一去不回頭。
向前走呀，努力向前走，前進莫退後。
手扶著犁向後看的人，不配進神國。
向前走呀，努力向前走，主就在前頭。
