Come to Jesus, ye who labor,
From vain strivings cease;
Heavy laden, He will give you
Rest and peace.

Come to Him, ye who, in sorrow,
Vainly seek relief;
He has precious balm and comfort
For each grief.

Come to Him, His yoke is easy,
And His burden light;
All your weakness He will strengthen
With his might.

Come to Him, all sweetest voices
Tenderly entreat;
lay your sins and cares and burdens,
At His feet.

　

1.来就主，你劳苦人们，勿徒然争竞，
担虽重他必定赐你得安宁。

2.来就主，你优伤人们，虽安慰难寻，
他有珍贵膏能治你慰优心。

3.来就主，主担子轻省，主的轭容易，
靠他大能软弱转强满能力。

4.来就主，听甜美柔声，频向你招呼，
应将一切忧虑罪担卸给主。

Parker, Edwin Pond,

D.D., born at Castine, Maine, Jan. 13, 1836, and educated at Bowdoin College, Maine, and Bangor Theo. Sem., Maine. Entering the Congregational ministry, he became pastor of the Second Church of Christ, Hartford, Conn., Jan. 1860, and has remained there to the present date.
