【Hymn of Promise】作於 1986 年，作者均為 Natalie Sleeth。

Natalie Sleeth 是一位師母，是作曲家也是管風琴家。

原文歌詞非常美，但中文很難翻出原來的意境，台語詩歌是由駱維道老師重新依照台語的音韻譜曲的，編在試煉盼望分類。

第三節的歌詞是說在面對親人的離去，我們因主的激勵安慰，依靠著祂能繼續向前行，世間雖有敗壞，若我們注目看環境容易灰心、絕望，但當我們轉眼仰望主耶穌，反而會行路有力。

　

一、發揮告別懷思禮拜的優質詩歌功能
論到基督教的聖詩，朱維之教授認為聖詩是基督教文學的奇笆，他在著作的《基督教與文學》書中，對詩歌的特質作了三個描述：
（一）是表現生之歡喜、感恩。
（二）是表達生之昂進，藉詩歌鼓勵人。
（三）是表達積極的安慰。


安慰人使人擦乾眼淚後能奮起；對於喪禮的詩歌運用，三種互為幫補。傳統詩本中有分類的編排，如果依詩本分類編排來找尋喪禮詩歌，就太受拘限了，事實上合適用於喪禮的詩歌，也可以在其他詩歌分類中找到。近幾年來，臺灣教會於主日禮拜中採用敬拜讚美的詩歌形式，也有人將此形式用於婚禮、喪禮中，對於禮拜、音樂的形式不是爭議所在，應該彼此提醒的是切實了解禮拜的真意。

基督教是歌唱的宗教，基督徒的人生是有盼望的人生。

全本詩篇都是詩歌，自有上帝子民以來，上帝一直使用詩歌，成為祂兒女的屬靈生活、事奉和追求信

（三）有連貫性：

留意詩歌與信息的連貫性。一般主日禮拜，我們選詩會配合當日的講題經文而選，但在告別禮拜中這一部分因已偏向安慰、永生和盼望，有時會忽略把詩歌與講題作配合。喪禮在結構性程序中，仍可與清楚
的主題及系統性的流程來安排，讓詩歌能更貼切發揮承載歌詞的作用，達到功能性的目的：敬拜上主、安慰活人、追念故人。

（四）新舊融合：

傳統與新詩歌的使用要融合、平衡選用。使用優良傳統聖詩，也寬廣去接受不同形式的曲調。因應年輕族群，傳統詩歌可加創意詮釋，在合宜的景況適度的轉調或變化拍子、節奏。


（五）幾首新詩歌推薦使用：（歌譜請參照詩歌本）

1761 年Wesley, John約翰‧衛斯理在《神聖的旋律》序言說：『若有人覺得唱詩像背十字架那麼難，那就揹起它，你會蒙福的』。
筆者指導老師陳琇玟在 2009 年新聖詩研習會中，鼓勵大家要敞開心、努力學習好的新詩歌。聖靈在歷世歷代感動詩人寫下隽永的詩歌，當我們吟唱時，聖靈會繼續感動這世代的人。

 Written as an Anthem , "Hymn of Promise" was later dedicated to Sleeth's husband shortly after he was diagnosed with Cancer. After hearing the hymn, Ronald Sleeth requested that the hymn be sung at his funeral. Honoring his wishes, the piece was performed at the funeral, being the only piece written by Natalie Sleeth to be sung at the ceremony.

The anthem was written for Pasadena Community Church, in St. Petersburg, Florida, where it was first sung and performed in 1985 under the direction of C. Fredrick Harrison. This hymn was very popular in the United Church of Canada Hymnal; Voices United (published in 1996), as well as in the United Methodist Church Hymnal (published in 1989). The text for the hymn comes from Sleeth's pondering the opposition of Spring and Winter, as well as the opposition in a line found in a T.S. Elliott poem reading as follows "In the end is our beginning."

Natalie Allyn Sleeth (née Wakeley; October 29, 1930 – March 21, 1992) was an American composer of hymns and choral music.[1]

Sleeth was born in Evanston, Illinois. In 1934, she began to study the piano at the early age of four. In 1952, she earned a BA in music theory at Wellesley College in Massachusetts. She married the Rev. Dr. Ronald E. Sleeth, a professor of Homiletics.[1]

Sleeth received an honorary doctorate from West Virginia Wesleyan College in 1989 and then from Nebraska Wesleyan University in 1990. An organist, she wrote over 180 highly successful selections for church and school. One of Sleeth's best-known anthems for the choir is entitled "Joy in the Morning" and was written for the West Virginia Wesleyan College concert chorale on the occasion of her husband's inauguration as president of the college in 1977.[2]

The anthem "Hymn of Promise" was composed in early 1985, and dedicated simply to Dr. Sleeth, "To Ron," who was diagnosed with cancer and died weeks after its premiere. Soon after the anthem was published, it became a hymn under two different names. Under the title "In the Bulb There is a Flower," it appears as hymn #703 in the United Church of Canada hymnal, Voices United and as hymn #433 in The New Century Hymnal produced by the United Church of Christ in the US. It appears as hymn #707 under the title "Hymn of Promise" in The United Methodist Hymnal. It is frequently sung in the United Church of Canada and the United Church of Christ. The hymn was translated into German by Lothar Pöll in 1999 and is included in the Gesangbuch der Evangelisch-methodistischen Kirche.[3]

Death[edit]

Sleeth died of cancer in Denver, Colorado in 1992, aged 61.[1] She was a member of the Highland Park United Methodist Church in Dallas, Texas.

"In the Bulb there is a Flower"
Natalie Sleeth
The United Methodist Hymnal, No. 707


Natalie Sleeth

In the bulb there is a flower; in the seed, an apple tree; in cocoons, a hidden promise: butterflies will soon be free! In the cold and snow of winter there’s a spring that waits to be, unrevealed until its season, something God alone can see.*

From time to time, a choral anthem inspires a hymn. "Hymn of Promise" was first conceived as an anthem in 1985, performed at the Pasadena Community Church, St. Petersburg, Florida, during a festival concert on Natalie Sleeth's music. Long known as a composer of anthems, especially for children, Natalie Sleeth (1930-1992) contributed one of the favorite new hymns to The United Methodist Hymnal.

Sleeth was as native of Evanston, Illinois. She began piano study at the age of four and gained much of her musical experience by singing in choral ensembles during her earlier years. Studying music theory, piano, and organ at Wellesley College in Massachusetts, she received her B.A. in 1952.

Married to Ronald E. Sleeth, a United Methodist clergyman and professor of homiletics at Perkins School of Theology, Southern Methodist University, she served as music secretary at Highland Park United Methodist Church from 1969-1976. During this time, she studied music theory with Jane Marshall and audited a course in choral arranging taught by Lloyd Pfautsch at SMU. Choristers Guild published her first anthem, "Canon of Praise," in 1969, the highest selling anthem in the history of this publisher. Her choral works for all ages number more than 200.

Sleeth received honorary doctorates from West Virginia Wesleyan College (1989), and Nebraska Wesleyan College (1990). Some of her compositions are described in her devotional book, Adventures for the Soul (1987).

Sleeth had the ability to compose both texts and music. Though "Hymn of Promise" has become a favorite hymn for funerals, it was written at a time when the author states that she was "pondering the ideas of life, death, spring and winter, Good Friday and Easter, and the whole reawakening of the world that happens every spring." Inspired by a T.S. Eliot line, the germ of the hymn grew from the idea "in our end is our beginning," the phase that begins the third stanza of the hymn.

While it carries the promise of spring and the hope of Easter in its beautiful metaphors, it is a very appropriate hymn for funeral and memorial services. Shortly after its composition, the composer’s husband was diagnosed with what turned out to be a terminal malignancy. Ronald Sleeth requested that "Hymn of Promise" be sung at his funeral service.

A wonderful child-like simplicity permeates "Hymn of Promise." Natalie Sleeth had a gift for composing texts on complex theological ideas that were still accessible to children. Her melodies seemed totally natural and therefore effortless for people to learn. "Hymn of Promise" is one of the most memorable hymns written by an American United Methodist in the last part of the twentieth century, and it promises to be sung for many years to come.

Composer R. G. Huff, who attended her funeral in Denver, Colorado, describes her impact on him in Worship Matrix, an online hymnal companion for the hymnal Celebrating Grace (2010): "In March 1992, I attended the memorial service of Natalie Sleeth at Wellshire Presbyterian Church in Denver, across the street from the church I served there. I had been a pen-pal of sorts with her for several years and wanted to be there for the celebration of her contribution to church music, especially the music of children.

"For a full hour before the funeral, the church’s choir, soloists and organist performed her songs, hymns and anthems. It was a great tribute to her writing legacy. Of course, this was one of the songs we heard that day . . ..

"Like the theologian she was, Natalie Sleeth moved on to worship: songs rising from silence, words seeking melodies, darkness becoming light, hope becoming evident in future days.

"Finally – and this relates to the afternoon in March 1992 – she expressed how life moves into death, death gives way to eternity, and ultimate victory comes for those whose doubt has evolved into belief."

凡事都有定期

筆者很喜歡上面所羅門王所說的話，常常用心體會，發現對人生做事很又幫助。

上面已共列舉了十四對「有時」。開宗明義說到生死。小孩出生慢慢會長大，不能期望一夜長成大人，因此大人要慢慢扶養，不能操之過急，也不能揠苗助長。

　　有一天老了，人自然會死亡，因此生死是自然的現象，不要因死亡而傷心，但也不會去自殺。

　　第二對說到「栽種有時，拔出所栽種的也有時。」就是說植物栽種有它的時候，收成有時一年、兩年、三年不等，到了收成晚期，有些植物就會被拔掉（如香蕉和甘蔗），再重新種植原作物或其他作物。

　　 第三對說到「殺戮有時，醫治有時」，「殺戮是指遭受刑罰而言。醫治乃是指人在疾病中蒙醫治說的，有些人的病看了好幾年都看不好，忽然到了某個時間，卻好了，時有所聞。

　　第四對：「拆毀與建造」，也是有它的時間，有些大樓蓋了兩三年才完工，通常壽命在六十年，所以在拆毀時，用炸藥一下就把大樓拆平了。就像美國世貿大廈一樣，也是被阿拉伯恐怖分子用飛機撞毀，實在令人料想不到是用這樣的方式拆毀。

　　「哭有時，笑有時；哀勵有時，跳舞有時。」說到人生的遭遇有悲歡離合，有時高興笑，有時悲傷苦，都有它的時機。

　　「拋擲石頭有時，堆聚石頭有時；懷抱有時，不懷抱有時。」古代猶大人風俗，見面時候，互相擁抱；在埋葬時，要三次擲土在墳墓裡。意思就是說見面跟離別，都有他的時機。懷抱也可以說是得志，不懷抱也可說是失意，得意和失意也有時。

　　「尋找有時，失落有時；保守有時，捨棄有時」，是說到尋找財富，有時得到，有時投資失利或被偷而失去；有時存錢，有時也會施捨或用去金錢。

　　「撕裂有時，縫補有時」，說到衣服穿久了會破，有時要逢補，也可以說有時心被人傷害如「撕裂」一般，有時也「縫補」人際關係的破裂來修好。

　　「靜默有時，言語有時」，就是說有時該說話，有時該沈默，這也要看時機。如喜宴就不要說不吉利的話，弔喪則不能說笑話。

　　「喜愛有時，恨惡有時」，是說有時你喜歡一個人或東西，但是後來又不喜歡了。

　　就像男女談戀愛有它的時機，起初兩人愛得死去活來，山盟海誓，最後卻投向他人懷抱，時有所聞。所以常常會「戀人結婚了，丈夫不是我」，也不要太大驚小怪。

　　「爭戰有時，和好有時」，是說戰爭有它的時候，和平有它的時候。人類經歷兩次世界大戰，死傷無數。以後是否會發生第三次的核子大戰，也沒有人敢說。希望人類永遠能夠抱持和平，不要戰爭。

　　從以上的十四對有時，可以知道人生的事情都包括在裡面了。因此人做事如果能把握時機，順應天時，相信必能早日得到成功。

失時會「事倍功半」，因此要好好注意時機，掌握時機。
