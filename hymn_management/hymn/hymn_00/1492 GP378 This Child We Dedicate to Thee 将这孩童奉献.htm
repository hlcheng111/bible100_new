378 This Child We dedicate to Thee

This Child We dedicate to Thee,
O God of grace and purity!
In Thy great love its life prolong,
Shield it, we pray, from sin and wrong.

O may Thy Spirit gently draw
Its willing soul to keep Thy law,
May virtue, piety, and truth
Dawn even with its dawning youth.

Henry Kemble Oliver (b. Beverly, MA, 1800; d. Salem, MA, 1885) was educated at Harvard and Dartmouth. He taught in the public schools of Salem (1818-1842) and was superintendent of the Atlantic Cotton Mills in Lawrence, Massachusetts (1848-1858). His civic service included being mayor of Lawrence (1859­1861) and Salem (1877-1880), state treasurer (1861-1865), and organizer of the Massachusetts Bureau of Statistics and Labor (1867-1873). Oliver was organist at several churches, including Park Street Congregational Church in Boston, North Church in Salem, and the Unitarian Church in Lawrence. A founder of the Mozart Association and several choral societies in Salem, he published his hymn tunes in Hymn and Psalm Tunes (1860) and Original Hymn Tunes (1875).

Notes

Henry Kemble Oliver (b. Beverly, MA, 1800; d. Salem, MA, 1885) composed FEDERAL STREET in 1832, possibly as an imitation of earlier psalm tunes in long meter. He took it to a music class taught by Lowell Mason (who may have contributed to the harmony); Mason (PHH 96) published it in his Boston Academy Collection of Church Music (1836).The tune name refers to the street in Boston where Oliver's boyhood church stood, al1 to the street in Salem where Oliver's wife, Sally, was “reared, wooed, won, and married.”

孩童奉獻禮                                             翁玉明

　

「所以，我將這孩子歸與耶和華，使他終身歸與耶和華。於是在那裡敬拜耶和華。」（撒上1:28）

孩童奉獻禮的起源：

在早期，特別是東正教會，他們仿效聖經記載：當馬利亞懷孕生了耶穌後，滿了潔淨的日子，她和約瑟一同帶着耶穌上耶路撒冷的聖殿，在那裏按着摩西律法的教訓，將嬰孩耶穌獻與主（路2：22）。那時候，照舊約利未記12:1-8的記載，婦人懷孕生男孩，她就不淨七天，第八天要給嬰孩行割禮。婦人在摩西不潔之中，要居家三十三天，滿了潔淨的日子，那時，嬰孩便剛好出生四十天了。所以，早期教會便為滿了四十天的嬰孩進行奉獻禮，甚至繼而進行浸禮。（註一）

發展至十六世紀，1521年（宗教改革時期），有人提出反對嬰兒受洗，因為他們認為嬰孩未有能力決定悔改信耶穌，所以洗禮對嬰孩是沒有意義的。（註二）然而，對於嬰孩奉獻禮，卻沒有反對，因為嬰孩或孩童奉獻禮是有其重要的意義。

孩童奉獻禮的意義：

「兒女是耶和華所賜的產業；所懷的胎是他所給的賞賜。」（詩127：3）兒女既然是神所賜的，故此父母不應視兒女為自己所有的，甚至希望兒女長大後達成自己的願望，而應視為神所交託的產業，讓我們盡力地照神的心意養育栽培他們，使他們長大後，明白救恩，將生命交託給神；樂意為主而活，讓神帶領他們的一生。孩童奉獻禮的意義如下：

1.          孩童奉獻禮乃父母對神的心意，與孩童的得救無關。

2.          父母甘心樂意地向神表示感恩，將孩童獻給神。（故此，教會鼓勵會友，若有感動作孩童奉獻禮，應主動提出，藉此表示甘心樂意。）

3.          父母樂意遵照聖經的教訓，教導孩童自小明白神的話。（箴22：6）

4.          當孩童漸長時，鼓勵他們參加各項合適其年齡聚會。

5.          藉着孩童奉獻禮，讓教會弟兄姊妹認識孩童，為他們在主裏的成長不斷禱告。（教會接受孩童的年齡為──初生至十四歲）在十四歲後，即可鼓勵他們受浸加入教會。

孩童奉獻禮可說不單是父母的心意，也是一個公開的要求：希望教會的弟兄姊妹幫助孩童成長，為他們禱告守望，意義是十分重大的。試想，今天培育孩童，使他們有好的成長，確是一件不容易的事！我們必須齊心努力，為教會的未來接班人作好準備！

Child dedication  or baby presentation 

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Jump to navigationJump to search

A child dedication or baby presentation is an act of consecration of children to God practiced in evangelical churches, such as those of the Baptist tradition.[1]

Child dedication is practiced by organisations, such as the Woman's Christian Temperance Union, in which parents promise to help their child live a life free from alcohol and other drugs.[2]

Christianity[edit]

Origin[edit]

The child's presentation has its origin in the Book of Exodus in chapter 13 verse 2; "Consecrate to me every firstborn male. The first offspring of every womb among the Israelites belongs to me, whether human or animal".[3] The Bible relates some presentations of children. That of Samuel, in the Old Testament by Hannah.[4][5] And especially the presentation of Jesus in the Temple in the New Testament by Joseph and Mary.[6] Likewise, Jesus blessed children.[7] This biblical practice was done only for the first born sons and often they are left at the Temple to serve God. The dedication of the first born was a practice different from circumcision, which was a covenant sign for the community of God. In the New Testament, the covenant sign of circumcision was effectively replaced with baptism (Colossians 2:11-12).[8]

History[edit]

Even though the Christian Church had not practiced child dedication for 15 centuries from its inception, in 1523, the Anabaptist movement, which taught that baptism is only for adults (believer's baptism) according to their understanding of the bible, first instituted child dedication practice for all children from believing households instead of just the first born sons.[9] The child dedication was subsequently adopted by many evangelical denominations (Baptists and Pentecostalism) adhering to the doctrine of the believers' Church.[10][5] Other evangelical denominations, such as many Methodist Churches, contain rites for both infant baptism and child dedication, leaving the option to families for what they wish for their sons and daughters; examples include the Free Methodist Church and Allegheny Wesleyan Methodist Connection.[11][12]

Form[edit]

　

Child dedication at Crossing Church, Tampa, United States.

The form of the presentations may vary by church. The ceremony is usually performed before or after the Worship service of Sunday.[13] When the parents have come forward with the child, the pastor presents it to the congregation, or asks the parents to do so.[14] Most often, the pastor asks parents to say orally their commitment to raising the child in the Christian faith.[5] This public commitment is followed by one or more prayers and a blessing by the pastor, often after the latter has taken the child into his arms.[15] The purpose of the presentation is to express the recognition of parents and the church of the divine gift of birth and the responsibility of parents that results from it.[13]

Practice by denomination[edit]

Baptist Churches[edit]

Many Baptist Churches contain rites for child dedication, in which parents commit to raise their children "in the fear and nurture of the Lord."[1]

Methodist Churches[edit]

In the Methodist Churches, baptism is a sacrament of initiation into the visible Church.[16] Wesleyan covenant theology further teaches that baptism is a sign and a seal of the covenant of grace:[17]

Of this great new-covenant blessing, baptism was therefore eminently the sign; and it represented "the pouring out" of the Spirit, "the descending" of the Spirit, the "falling" of the Spirit "upon men," by the mode in which it was administered, the pouring of water from above upon the subjects baptized. As a seal, also, or confirming sign, baptism answers to circumcision.[17]

Infant baptism, in Methodism, is celebrated as "an acceptance of the prevenient grace of God and as a confession on the part of the church of its responsibility for children in general and for every child in particular."[18][19] Methodists teach that people receive justifying grace, which is integral to salvation, after they repent and personally accept Jesus as Saviour.[20][11] Many Methodist denominations, such as the Free Methodist Church and Allegheny Wesleyan Methodist Connection, practice infant baptism for families who desire it for their children, but provide a rite for child dedication for those who have a preference for credobaptism only after their child has made a personal acceptance of Jesus as his/her saviour.[11][12]

Presbyterian Churches[edit]

In many Presbyterian churches, one of the questions that is asked the parents during infant baptism is: "Do you unreservedly dedicate your child to God?" Thus, for them, infant baptism includes child dedication. This essentially makes it dedication baptism, which is something different from adult or believer's baptism.[21]

Woman's Christian Temperance Union[edit]

The Woman's Christian Temperance Union (WCTU) conducts a White Ribbon Recruit (WRR) ceremony, in which babies are dedicated to the cause of temperance through a white ribbon being tied to their wrists, with their adult sponsors pledging to help the child live a life free from alcohol and other drugs.[2]
